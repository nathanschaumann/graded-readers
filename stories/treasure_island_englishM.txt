Treasure Island

The Inn by the Sea / The Captain's Warning / Rum Songs and a Strange Visitor / Black Dog at the Inn
1. Jim Hawkins lived at an inn.
2. Jim's mother kept the inn.
3. His father had died long ago.
4. The inn stood near the sea.
5. Many sailors came to the inn.
6. The sailors told Jim many tales.
7. Jim heard of ships and gold.
8. He spoke of far islands.
9. He loved these old tales.
10. One day a strange old sailor came to the inn.
11. The old sailor had a dark face.
12. He had one hand.
13. The old sailor had many scars.
14. He came to the inn door.
15. "Do you have a room?" the old sailor asked.
16. Jim's mother gave him a room.
17. He stayed at the inn some days.
18. Jim watched the old sailor with care.
19. He did not speak much.
20. He did not spend much.
21. He sat and looked to sea.
22. Jim wanted to know about the old sailor.
23. The old sailor told Jim to call him Captain.
24. The Captain gave Jim some gold coins.
25. "Watch for a sailor with one leg," said the Captain.
26. "Tell me when you see him."
27. Jim took the gold coins.
28. Jim watched for the one-legged sailor each day.
29. Each night the Captain drank rum.
30. The Captain sang loud songs of dead men.
31. He sang of ships and coins.
32. The songs made the guests afraid.
33. Many guests went away from the inn.
34. Jim's mother had less gold.
35. But Jim's mother did not ask the Captain to go.
36. One night the Captain told a long tale.
37. He told of a great fight at sea.
38. He told of ships full of gold.
39. The guests sat still and looked away.
40. The Captain kept watch at the door.
41. He watched for the sailor with one leg.
42. Jim watched the door each day.
43. One day a strange man came to the inn.
44. The strange man had a dark face.
45. "Is the Captain here?" the strange man asked Jim.
46. Jim said the Captain was away by the sea.
47. "I am Black Dog," the strange man said.
48. Black Dog waited at the table.
49. Then the Captain came back from the sea.
50. He watched Black Dog with fear.
51. The Captain and Black Dog sat at the table.
52. The Captain took out his blade.
53. Black Dog pulled out his blade too.
54. The Captain and Black Dog fought.
55. Black Dog ran from the inn with a cut.
56. Then the Captain fell down and gave a cry.
57. Jim ran out to the doctor.
58. Doctor Livesey came to look at the Captain.
59. Doctor Livesey told Jim that rum would kill the Captain.
60. The Captain stayed in bed for many days.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Jim Hawkins lives with his mother at an inn by the sea, where many sailors stop.
6-10: The sailors tell Jim tales of ships and gold, and one day a scarred old sailor arrives.
11-15: The strange sailor has a dark face, one hand, and many scars, and asks for a room.
16-20: The old sailor tells Jim to call him Captain and gives him coins to watch for a one-legged sailor.
21-25: Jim watches each day but never sees the man; at night the Captain drinks rum and sings loud songs that frighten the guests.
26-30: The Captain tells of a sea fight and watches the door for the one-legged sailor, who does not come.
31-34: The Captain's songs of dead men drive guests away and leave Jim's mother with less gold.
35-39: The Captain stays on, telling long tales of ships and gold while the guests sit still.
40-43: The Captain keeps watch at the door for the one-legged sailor; one day a strange man arrives.
44-49: A dark-faced man named Black Dog waits for the Captain, who returns from the sea.
50-56: The Captain watches Black Dog with fear; the two draw blades, fight, and the Captain collapses.
57-60: Jim runs for Doctor Livesey, who warns that rum would kill the Captain, now laid up in bed.

The Black Spot / The Captain Falls and the Chest Opens / The King's Men Arrive
1. Jim watched the Captain each day.
2. Jim sat by his bed.
3. The Captain spoke to Jim of old fights at sea.
4. He spoke of dead sailors too.
5. But the Captain did not speak of the one-legged sailor.
6. The Captain looked at the door in fear.
7. One cold morning a blind man came to the inn.
8. The blind man asked Jim to help him.
9. "Take me to the Captain," the blind man said.
10. Jim took the blind man to the Captain.
11. The blind man put a small thing in the Captain's hand.
12. Then the blind man tapped his stick down the road.
13. The Captain looked at the small thing.
14. The small thing was a black spot.
15. "The black spot!" said the Captain in fear.
16. He stood up fast.
17. Then the Captain fell down dead.
18. Jim called his mother to the Captain.
19. Jim took a key from the Captain.
20. The key opened the Captain's old sea chest.
21. Jim looked in the old dark chest.
22. The chest had a small packet and gold coins.
23. Then Jim and his mother heard the men call out.
24. The blind man came back to the inn.
25. Many old pirates came back with him.
26. The old sailors came for the Captain's gold.
27. The old sailors wanted to kill Jim and his mother.
28. Jim took the packet and the coins.
29. Then Jim and his mother ran away from the inn.
30. They did not stop until night came.
31. Jim and his mother hid in the dark.
32. They watched the inn from the road.
33. The blind man and the old sailors were in the inn.
34. The old sailors found the Captain's chest.
35. But the treasure was gone.
36. Jim had the packet with him.
37. "The gold is not here!" said a sailor.
38. The blind man stood up and called out.
39. "Find the gold!" the blind man said.
40. The old sailors looked all through the inn.
41. Then Jim kept still and heard horses on the road.
42. The king's men came fast on horses.
43. The old sailors ran away in the dark.
44. But he could not see them go.
45. He stood alone on the road.
46. The horses came fast at the blind man.

Paragraphs:
1-6: Jim sits by the Captain's bed each day, hearing old sea tales, but the Captain never speaks of the one-legged sailor.
7-11: A blind man comes to the inn and has Jim take him to the Captain, where he presses a small thing into the Captain's hand.
12-15: The Captain sees it is a black spot and cries out in fear.
16-19: The Captain stands up in fright, falls dead, and Jim and his mother open his sea chest with the key Jim takes.
20-23: Jim looks in the dark chest and finds a small packet and gold coins; then they hear the men call out.
24-31: The blind man and old pirates return for the Captain's gold; Jim takes the packet and the coins and he and his mother flee into the dark.
32-37: From the road Jim and his mother watch the sailors search the chest, but the treasure is already gone.
38-41: The blind man calls out in anger; the sailors hunt through the inn but cannot find the gold.
42-46: The king's men ride in on horses; the sailors run away in the dark and do not look back, but the blind man is left alone on the road.

The Treasure Map / A Ship and a Plan / Silver the Cook / Pirates in the Barrel
1. The horses went over the blind man.
2. The blind man fell down dead.
3. Then Jim went out to the king's men.
4. Jim told them about the old sailors.
5. The king's men went after the old sailors on horses.
6. Jim still had the Captain's packet.
7. Doctor Livesey had helped the Captain before.
8. "I will take the packet to Doctor Livesey," said Jim.
9. Doctor Livesey sat with the rich man Trelawney.
10. Jim gave the packet to Doctor Livesey.
11. Doctor Livesey opened the packet.
12. Inside the packet was an old map of a small island.
13. A red cross marked a spot on the map.
14. "Gold is buried here," said Doctor Livesey.
15. "An old pirate buried it long ago."
16. Trelawney looked at the old map.
17. "We will find this gold!" said Trelawney.
18. "I will buy a ship and take us to the island."
19. Doctor Livesey was glad to go too.
20. Doctor Livesey would be the ship's doctor.
21. "Jim will come with us," called Trelawney.
22. Jim would keep watch on the ship.
23. "We must not tell the old sailors," said Doctor Livesey.
24. "The old sailors want this map too."
25. Trelawney told them to keep the map safe.
26. Then Trelawney went to buy a boat.
27. Trelawney bought a great ship for the sea.
28. He found good men to sail the ship.
29. Trelawney also hired a cook named Silver.
30. Then Trelawney asked Jim to come.
31. Jim went to the ship by the sea.
32. There Jim met Silver.
33. Silver had only one leg.
34. Jim thought of the Captain at the inn.
35. The Captain had feared a sailor with one leg.
36. Jim was afraid of Silver.
37. But Silver was very kind to Jim.
38. "Come and see the ship, Jim!" said Silver.
39. He smiled and told Jim about the sea.
40. Soon Jim was not afraid of Silver.
41. Then Doctor Livesey and Trelawney came to the ship.
42. They liked the ship and all the men.
43. The ship was ready to sail.
44. The ship sailed on the sea for many days.
45. Silver was the ship's cook.
46. Silver cooked for all the men on the ship.
47. The men liked Silver very much.
48. One day Jim wanted an apple.
49. A big barrel of apples sat on the ship.
50. Jim climbed into the apple barrel.
51. The barrel was almost empty.
52. No one could see Jim down in the barrel.
53. Then Silver sat down by the barrel.
54. Silver did not know Jim was in the barrel.
55. He talked to some of the men.
56. Jim heard every word.
57. Silver was a pirate!
58. The other men were pirates too.
59. "We will take all the gold," said Silver.

Paragraphs:
1-5: The king's men ride over the blind man; Jim tells them about the sailors, then keeps the packet safe.
6-8: Jim brings the packet to Doctor Livesey, who opens it and finds an old map of a small island.
9-15: A red cross marks the buried gold; Doctor Livesey tells Trelawney, and they read that an old pirate buried it long ago.
16-21: Trelawney plans to buy a ship and sail to the island; Doctor Livesey is glad to go as the ship's doctor and calls Jim to come.
22-25: Jim also wants to see the island; Doctor Livesey warns them not to tell the old sailors, and Trelawney tells them to keep the map safe.
26-28: Trelawney buys a boat and then a great ship for the sea, while Jim and Doctor Livesey wait for the day they sail.
29-33: Trelawney hires a cook named Silver; Jim goes to the ship and meets him, noting he has only one leg.
34-38: Jim fears Silver because the Captain once warned of a one-legged sailor, but Silver is very kind.
39-43: Silver smiles and talks with Jim about the sea until Jim's fear is gone; Doctor Livesey and Trelawney arrive and the ship sails out.
44-47: The ship sails many days; Silver cooks for the men and all like him, Jim included.
48-52: Jim climbs into an empty apple barrel and hides; Silver sits nearby and does not know Jim is inside.
53-59: Jim hears every word: Silver and the other men are pirates who plan to take all the gold.

Jim Warns the Captain / Alone on the Island / Ben Gunn's Tale / The Attack on the Fort
1. "Then we will kill Trelawney and Doctor Livesey."
2. Jim was very afraid in the barrel.
3. Soon Silver left with the men.
4. Then Jim climbed out and away.
5. Jim ran to Doctor Livesey and Trelawney.
6. "Silver is a pirate!" said Jim.
7. "The men want the gold, and they will kill us."
8. Soon the ship came near the island.
9. The island was green with many dark trees.
10. Silver and his men wanted to go to the island.
11. They got into the boats by the ship.
12. Jim hid in a boat with the men.
13. The boats came to the land.
14. Jim jumped onto the sand and ran to the trees.
15. The men called out to Jim.
16. But Jim did not stop.
17. Jim ran away from Silver and the men.
18. He was very afraid of the pirates.
19. Jim hid by a big rock on the hill.
20. Then the men looked for Jim.
21. But the men did not find him.
22. Soon Silver and the men went away.
23. Jim went through the trees on the island.
24. Then Jim saw a man.
25. The man was not one of Silver's pirates.
26. Jim was afraid of the man.
27. "Who are you?" asked Jim.
28. "I am Ben," said the man.
29. Ben had lived on the island for three years.
30. Long ago Ben had sailed with pirates.
31. The pirates had left Ben on the island alone.
32. "I will help you, Jim," said Ben.
33. Ben knew all of the island.
34. He knew about the gold.
35. Then Jim told Ben about Silver.
36. "Silver and the men are pirates," said Jim.
37. "They want all the gold."
38. Ben knew the pirates were bad.
39. Ben and Jim were friends now.
40. Ben took Jim through the trees on the island.
41. Soon they came to an old fort.
42. The fort was made of wood.
43. Doctor Livesey and Trelawney were inside.
44. They had left the great ship.
45. Jim was glad to see his friends again.
46. Jim told them all about Ben.
47. Then Silver and his men came.
48. The pirates had swords and guns.
49. "Take the fort!" Silver said to his men.
50. The men ran at the fort.
51. But the good men shot at them.
52. Two pirates fell down dead.
53. They all ran back to the trees.
54. Silver could not take the fort.
55. Jim was safe with his friends.
56. But Silver still wanted the buried gold.
57. The pirates would come back again.

Jim Takes the Ship / Silver Catches Jim / The Gold Is Gone / Treasure and Home
1. That night Jim left the fort in the dark.
2. He did not tell his friends.
3. Jim went down to Ben's small boat.
4. He got into the boat and went out to sea.
5. The boat came to the big ship.
6. Jim climbed up onto the ship.
7. A rope held the ship on the sea.
8. Jim cut the rope with his knife.
9. The ship moved away from the pirates.
10. But one pirate was still on the ship.
11. The pirate had a big sword.
12. He ran at Jim to kill him.
13. Jim was very afraid of him.
14. But he had a gun.
15. Jim shot the pirate.
16. The pirate fell down into the sea.
17. Jim had the big ship now.
18. Jim was alone on the ship at sea.
19. Jim left the ship and went back to land.
20. Jim came to the fort in the dark.
21. But the pirates were in the fort now.
22. Doctor Livesey and the good men were gone.
23. Silver and the pirates caught Jim.
24. The pirates wanted to kill Jim.
25. But Silver told them no.
26. "Jim will stay with me," said Silver.
27. Silver kept Jim safe from the pirates.
28. Jim saw the map in the fort.
29. Doctor Livesey had given Silver the map.
30. But Jim did not know why.
31. "We will find the gold now," said Silver.
32. "Help me, and I will keep you safe."
33. Jim was afraid, but he said yes.
34. The pirates would look for the gold soon.
35. One day the pirates went to find the gold.
36. Silver had the map.
37. The pirates took Jim with them.
38. They came to the spot on the map.
39. But there was a big hole.
40. The gold was gone!
41. Someone had taken the gold.
42. The pirates were angry at Silver.
43. They ran at Silver and Jim.
44. But then Doctor Livesey and Ben came.
45. They shot at the bad pirates from the trees.
46. Some pirates fell down dead.
47. The other pirates ran away.
48. Ben had taken the gold long ago.
49. Ben had put it in his cave.
50. That was why the map was no good.
51. Jim and his friends went to Ben's cave.
52. The treasure was in the cave.
53. They took the gold to the ship.
54. Then they got ready to sail home.
55. The good men kept Silver on the ship.
56. But Silver took some gold and ran away.
57. Jim was not sad that Silver had gone.
58. The ship sailed home over the sea.
59. Ben had gold for his help.
60. Soon the ship came home again.
61. Jim gave his mother some gold.
62. He was home again at the inn.
63. But Jim never went back to the sea.
64. He did not want any more treasure.

Paragraphs:
1-5: That night Jim slips out of the fort alone, finds Ben's small boat, and rows out to the big ship.
6-9: Jim climbs onto the ship and cuts the rope so the ship moves away from the pirates.
10-16: A pirate on board runs at Jim with a sword; Jim is afraid but shoots him, and the pirate falls dead into the sea.
17-20: Jim has the ship but is alone at sea; he returns to land and comes to the fort in the dark.
21-27: The pirates hold the fort now; Silver catches Jim but keeps him safe from the others.
28-32: Jim sees the treasure map in the fort — Doctor Livesey gave it to Silver — and Silver promises to keep Jim safe if Jim helps him find the gold.
33-36: Jim agrees to help Silver; the pirates keep Jim with them and set out to find the gold.
37-41: They follow the map to the spot, but find only a big hole — someone has already taken the gold.
42-48: The angry pirates turn on Silver and Jim; but then Doctor Livesey and Ben come from the trees and drive the pirates away, and Jim is safe with his friends — Ben had taken the gold long ago.
49-54: Ben had hidden the gold in his cave; Jim and his friends carry it all to the ship and prepare to sail home.
55-59: The good men keep Silver on board, but he steals some gold and escapes; the ship sails home, and Ben has gold for his help.
60-64: The ship arrives home; Jim gives his mother gold and is glad to be back at the inn, but he never wants to go to sea again.
