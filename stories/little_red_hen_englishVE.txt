The Little Red Hen

Seeds in the Farmyard
1. A little red hen lived in a small farmyard.
2. She lived there with her chicks.
3. The chicks were too small to work.
4. A cat, a dog, and a pig lived nearby.
5. The hen wanted fresh bread for her hungry chicks.
6. One day, she found wheat seeds on the ground.
7. "Who will help me plant the wheat?" she asked.
8. "Not I," said the cat.
9. "Not I," said the dog.
10. "Not I," said the pig.
11. "Then I will plant it myself," said the hen.
12. So she planted the wheat in a small field.

Paragraphs:
1-6: A little red hen lives in a farmyard with her chicks, who are too small to work, near a cat, a dog, and a pig, and because she wants bread for her hungry chicks, she is glad to find wheat seeds on the ground.
7-12: She asks who will help her plant the wheat, the cat, the dog, and the pig all refuse, so she plants the wheat in a small field by herself.

The Wheat Grows Tall
1. Sun and rain fell on the small field every day.
2. The hen pulled the weeds herself.
3. The wheat grew tall in the sun.
4. Soon the wheat turned a golden color.
5. The hen looked at the tall wheat.
6. "The wheat is ready to cut," she said.
7. "Who will help me cut the wheat?" she asked.
8. "Not I," said the dog.
9. "Not I," said the pig.
10. "Not I," said the cat.
11. "Then I will cut it myself," she said.
12. The hen cut the wheat alone.

Paragraphs:
1-6: Sun and rain help the wheat grow tall in the field while the hen pulls the weeds herself, until the wheat turns a golden color and she decides it is ready to cut.
7-12: She asks who will help her cut the wheat, the dog, the pig, and the cat all refuse, so she cuts the wheat alone.

Grinding the Flour
1. The cut wheat lay in a big pile.
2. The hen wanted to grind it into flour.
3. "Who will help me grind the wheat?" she asked.
4. She looked at the cat, dog, and pig.
5. "Not I," they all said.
6. "Then I will grind it myself," said the hen.
7. The hen had to grind the wheat alone.
8. Soon the wheat became white flour.
9. The hen put the flour into a bag.

Paragraphs:
1-5: The cut wheat lies in a big pile, and when the hen asks who will help her grind it into flour, the cat, the dog, and the pig all refuse together.
6-9: So the hen grinds the wheat alone until it becomes white flour, and she puts the flour into a bag.

Baking the Bread
1. The hen mixed the flour with water.
2. She made a soft dough.
3. "Who will help me bake the bread?" she asked.
4. "Not I," said the pig.
5. "Not I," said the cat.
6. "Not I," said the dog.
7. "Then I will bake it myself," she said.
8. The hen had to bake the bread alone.
9. She put the dough into a hot oven.
10. Soon a warm smell filled the small house.
11. The cat, dog, and pig smelled it from far away.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The hen mixes the flour with water into a soft dough, but when she asks who will help her bake the bread, the pig, the cat, and the dog all refuse.
7-11: So the hen bakes the bread alone in a hot oven, and a warm smell fills the house until the cat, the dog, and the pig smell it from far away.

Who Will Eat It?
1. The cat, dog, and pig came to the house.
2. "That smells so good," said the cat.
3. "Who will eat this bread?" asked the hen.
4. "Give it to me!" said the cat.
5. "I want some too!" said the dog.
6. "Share it with us!" said the pig.
7. "You did not help me," said the hen.
8. "My chicks and I will eat it," she said.
9. So the hen ate the warm bread with her chicks.
10. The bread tasted sweet.
11. The chicks pecked every crumb from the plate.
12. The cat, dog, and pig walked away hungry.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The cat, the dog, and the pig come to the house because the bread smells good, and when the hen asks who will eat it, all three ask for a share.
7-12: The hen tells them they did not help her, so she eats the warm bread with her chicks, who peck every crumb from the plate, while the cat, the dog, and the pig walk away hungry.
