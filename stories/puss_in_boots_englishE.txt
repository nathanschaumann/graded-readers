Puss in Boots

The Miller's Three Sons / Boots and a Bag / A Gift for the King / More Gifts for the King
1. A miller lived with his three sons.
2. He owned a mill, a donkey, and a cat.
3. One day, the old miller died.
4. His mill, his donkey, and his cat went to his sons.
5. The oldest son got the mill.
6. The second son got the donkey.
7. The youngest son got only the cat.
8. The youngest son was not happy with his gift.
9. "My brothers can grind grain and ride to town," he said.
10. "I can only eat this cat," he said to the cat.
11. "After that, I will have nothing at all," he said.
12. The cat sat near him and listened to every word.
13. The cat looked up at the youngest son.
14. "Do not eat me," the cat said.
15. The son was surprised the cat could talk.
16. "I can help you," said the cat.
17. "Bring me a bag and a pair of boots."
18. His brothers had laughed at his gift.
19. The son had nothing to lose.
20. He brought the bag and boots to the cat.
21. The cat put the boots on his feet.
22. The cat put the bag over his shoulder.
23. "I will make you rich," the cat said.
24. The son did not believe the cat.
25. But he had no better plan.
26. A field lay near the mill.
27. The cat went there with his open bag.
28. He put some grain inside the bag.
29. A young rabbit went into the bag to eat.
30. The cat closed the bag.
31. Soon he brought the bag to the king's palace.
32. The cat gave the rabbit to the king.
33. "A gift from the Marquis of Carabas," said the cat.
34. The king was happy with the gift.
35. "Thank the Marquis for me," the king said.
36. Soon the cat went home.
37. The son knew nothing of this gift.
38. Weeks went by, and the cat caught more gifts.
39. He caught partridges in the fields near the mill.
40. He brought every gift to the king's palace.
41. The king always thanked the cat.
42. The king wanted to meet the Marquis.
43. But the cat still had a secret plan.
44. This went on for many weeks.
45. The son had nothing but the cat.
46. The cat's plan would make him rich.
47. The cat said nothing more.

Paragraphs:
1-2: A miller lives with his three sons and owns a mill, a donkey, and a cat, with little else.
3-7: One day the old miller dies, so his mill goes to the oldest son, his donkey to the second son, and his cat to the youngest.
8-12: The youngest son is not happy with his gift and says his brothers can work or travel while he can only eat the cat and then have nothing at all, but the cat sits near him and listens to every word.
13-19: The cat surprises the youngest son by talking and promises to help him, asking for a bag and a pair of boots; his brothers have already laughed at his gift, so with nothing to lose, the son agrees and brings them to the cat.
20-25: The cat puts on the boots and slings the bag over his shoulder, looking ready for a journey and promising to make the son rich, though the son does not believe him and simply has no better plan.
26-31: A field lies near the mill, and the cat goes there with his open bag, putting grain inside it until a young rabbit goes into the bag to eat, so the cat closes the bag and soon brings it to the king's palace, his boots helping him on the journey.
32-37: The cat gives the rabbit to the king as a gift from the Marquis of Carabas, and the happy king asks the cat to thank the Marquis, so the cat goes home, where the son knows nothing of the gift and only the cat knows of his clever plan.
38-42: Weeks go by, and the cat keeps catching gifts, mostly partridges from the fields near the mill, always bringing every one to the king's palace in the name of the Marquis of Carabas; the king always thanks the cat and wants to meet the Marquis.
43-47: But the cat still has a secret plan and looks happy with it, and this goes on for many weeks, with the son still having nothing but the cat, whose plan would one day make him rich, though the cat says nothing more.

A Plan for the River / The Stolen Clothes / Fine Clothes / The Field of Wheat
1. One day, the cat heard news.
2. The king would ride by the river soon.
3. The king's young daughter was a princess.
4. The princess would ride with the king.
5. The cat had a plan for the son.
6. The cat wanted the son to look rich.
7. "Go swim in the river tomorrow," the cat said.
8. Grass was near the river there.
9. The son said nothing.
10. He believed the cat now.
11. He had seen many of the cat's gifts.
12. Soon the Marquis of Carabas would meet the king.
13. The son went to the river the next day.
14. The son left his clothes on the grass.
15. He swam in the river.
16. The cat got the clothes and hid them in some grass.
17. Soon the king's coach was near the river.
18. The young princess was with the king.
19. The cat ran to the coach.
20. "My master's clothes are stolen!" the cat cried.
21. The king still remembered the cat's many gifts.
22. "This cat belongs to the Marquis of Carabas," the king said.
23. The king wanted to help the Marquis.
24. "Bring fine clothes for the Marquis," the king told his men.
25. The king's men brought the fine clothes to the river.
26. The son put on the fine clothes.
27. He looked like a rich marquis.
28. The princess looked at the young man.
29. She had fine clothes of her own.
30. She liked him.
31. "Come ride with us," the king said.
32. The young man got into the coach.
33. He thanked the king.
34. He sat next to the princess.
35. The king was happy with the young Marquis.
36. The cat watched from the road.
37. The coach rode on.
38. The cat had more to do.
39. He ran ahead of the coach.
40. He wanted to reach the fields.
41. Soon the cat came to a field.
42. Harvesters were cutting grain in the field.
43. The fields belonged to a rich ogre.
44. No harvester had ever seen the ogre.
45. The cat ran to the harvesters.
46. "The king will soon ride by," the cat said.
47. "You will say this field belongs to the Marquis of Carabas."
48. "If not, I will cut you into pieces," said the cat.
49. The harvesters were afraid of the cat.
50. They believed every word.
51. They said they would always do it.
52. The cat ran on ahead of the coach.

The King and the Harvesters / The Ogre's Castle / The Ogre Becomes a Lion / The Ogre Becomes a Mouse / The King at the Castle / A Royal Wedding
1. The coach came to the field.
2. "Whose field is this?" the king asked.
3. "This field belongs to the Marquis of Carabas," the harvesters said.
4. The king was surprised.
5. He would remember this rich Marquis.
6. "The Marquis has so much land," the king said to the princess.
7. The princess wanted to meet the Marquis.
8. The coach rode on.
9. Soon they left the fields.
10. The cat was already far ahead.
11. He had one more place to reach.
12. Soon the cat reached a great castle.
13. The castle was near the fields.
14. The ogre was the richest in the land.
15. The ogre owned three castles like this one.
16. A servant brought the cat inside.
17. The cat was not afraid to ask.
18. "I want to see the great ogre," said the cat.
19. The servant brought the cat to the ogre.
20. The ogre was strong and proud.
21. The ogre always liked to talk.
22. "I heard you can change into any animal," the cat said.
23. "Is this true?" the cat asked.
24. "This is true," said the ogre.
25. The cat had seen many rich castles.
26. The ogre became a big lion.
27. The lion roared.
28. The cat was afraid of the lion.
29. The cat did not want to become dinner.
30. The cat ran up to the roof.
31. His boots made it hard to climb.
32. But the cat still held on.
33. The lion looked up at the cat.
34. Soon the lion became the ogre.
35. The ogre laughed at the afraid cat.
36. Soon the cat came down from the roof.
37. "That was a great trick," said the cat.
38. "But I do not believe you can become small," the cat said.
39. "Small like a mouse," the cat said.
40. The proud ogre wanted to prove this.
41. The ogre became a small mouse.
42. The mouse ran across the floor.
43. The cat jumped on the mouse.
44. The cat ate the mouse.
45. The ogre was dead.
46. Now the castle belonged to no one.
47. Soon the king's coach reached the castle.
48. The young man was still with them.
49. The cat stood at the castle gate.
50. "Welcome to my master's castle," said the cat.
51. "This is the castle of the Marquis of Carabas," the cat said.
52. The king was surprised by the great castle.
53. They all went inside.
54. The servants had made a great feast.
55. This feast was for the ogre's friends.
56. The princess and the king ate the feast.
57. The king liked the rich castle so much.
58. The king wanted the Marquis for his daughter.
59. The king gave his daughter to the Marquis.
60. Soon the Marquis married the princess.
61. The wedding was at the castle.
62. The son was now a real marquis.
63. The son once had only the cat.
64. The Marquis always remembered the clever cat.
65. The cat lived with them in the castle.
66. He became a great lord too.
67. The cat did not need to catch a mouse now.
68. But the cat still liked to catch one.
