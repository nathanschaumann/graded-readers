Walton and the Ice
1. Robert Walton was a ship captain from England, and he wanted to sail to the far north.
2. No man had been there before, and Walton wanted to be the first.
3. His sister Margaret waited for him at home, and he wrote to her from the ship.
4. "I will find a new road over the sea," he wrote.
5. "Ships will use this road, and I will learn what no man knows."
6. "Do not be afraid for me, Margaret, because my men are good."
7. "I have no friend on this ship, and I want one," he wrote.
8. The ship sailed for many days, and it was cold.
9. Ice was on the sea, and every day there was more of it.
10. One day the ice closed around the ship, and the ship could not move.
11. "We cannot go on, and we cannot go back," a man said to Walton.
12. Walton looked out over the ice and saw only ice.
13. The men sat on the ship and waited for the ice to move.
14. The next day Walton saw a sledge far off on the ice, and dogs were pulling it.
15. The man on the sledge was bigger than any man Walton had seen.
16. "Look there!" Walton said to his men.
17. "What man could live on this ice?"
18. The sledge was gone, and no man could answer Walton.
19. In the morning the men found another sledge on the ice.
20. Only one dog was alive, and a sick man sat behind it.
21. The man was not a giant, but he was weak.
22. Walton took him on the ship and gave him hot food.
23. "Did you see a sledge go by before me?" the man asked.
24. "Yes, a giant went by on a sledge," said Walton.
25. The sick man tried to get up, but he fell back on the bed.
26. After two days the man could talk to Walton.
27. His name was Victor Frankenstein.
28. Walton came to love Victor as a friend, and he sat with him every day.
29. "Why are you here on the ice?" Walton asked.
30. "I was like you once," Victor said.
31. "I wanted to know all things, and it was bad for me."
32. "I made a thing that I should not have made," Victor said.
33. "Sit with me, and I will tell you all of it, so that you will not do what I did."
