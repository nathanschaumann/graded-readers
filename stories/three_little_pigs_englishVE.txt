The Three Little Pigs

The Three Pigs Leave Home
1. Three little pigs lived with their mother.
2. They lived in a small home in the woods.
3. One day, their mother called the three pigs.
4. "You are big pigs now," she said.
5. "Go and build houses of your own," she said.
6. "But watch out for the wolf!" she called.
7. A wolf lived deep in the woods.
8. The wolf liked to eat little pigs.
9. The three pigs said goodbye to their mother.
10. They left home to build their own houses.
11. Each pig went a different way.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Three little pigs live with their mother in a small home in the woods, and one day she tells them they are big enough to build houses of their own.
6-11: Their mother warns them about a dangerous wolf that likes to eat little pigs, so the three pigs say goodbye, leave home, and each go a different way to build their own houses.

Three New Houses
1. The first pig found a pile of straw.
2. He built a house of straw in one day.
3. "Now I can play all day," he said.
4. The second pig found sticks in the woods.
5. He built a house of sticks in two days.
6. The stick house was stronger than the straw house.
7. The third pig remembered the wolf.
8. He wanted a strong house, so he gathered many bricks.
9. The bricks were heavy and slow to carry.
10. The brick house took many days to build.
11. But the third pig was tired and did not stop.
12. At last, the brick house was done.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The first pig quickly builds a house of straw and looks forward to playing, while the second pig builds a slightly stronger house of sticks in the woods.
7-12: The third pig remembers the wolf and wants a strong house, so he gathers heavy bricks that are slow and tiring to carry, and after many days he finally finishes the brick house.

The Wolf and the Straw House
1. Soon, the wolf was hungry.
2. He went to find the three pigs.
3. The wolf found the house of straw.
4. He knocked on the door.
5. "Little pig, let me come in!" called the wolf.
6. "No, I won't let you in!" said the first pig.
7. "I'll huff and puff and blow your house down!"
8. The wolf huffed.
9. The wolf puffed.
10. The straw house fell down.
11. The first pig ran to the second pig's house.
12. He was safe inside the stick house.

Paragraphs:
1-7: A hungry wolf goes looking for the three pigs and finds the first pig's straw house, but when he knocks and the pig refuses to let him in, the wolf threatens to huff, puff, and blow the house down.
8-12: The wolf huffs and puffs until the straw house falls down, and the first pig escapes to the safety of the second pig's stick house.

The Wolf and the Stick House
1. The wolf was still hungry.
2. He went to find the pigs.
3. Soon, the wolf found the stick house.
4. Two little pigs were inside.
5. "Little pigs, let me come in!" called the wolf.
6. "No, we won't let you in!" said the pigs.
7. "I'll huff and puff and blow your house down!"
8. The wolf huffed and puffed hard.
9. This time, the house did not fall fast.
10. But the stick house fell down too.
11. Both pigs ran to the third pig's house.
12. They were safe inside the brick house.

Paragraphs:
1-7: The wolf, still hungry, finds the second pig's stick house where both pigs now hide together, and when they refuse to let him in, he threatens again to huff, puff, and blow the house down.
8-12: The wolf huffs and puffs hard, and though the stick house holds for a moment, it soon falls down too, so both pigs run to the safety of the third pig's brick house.

The Wolf and the Brick House
1. All three pigs were inside the brick house.
2. The wolf found them.
3. "Little pigs, let me come in!" called the wolf.
4. "No, we won't let you in!" said the pigs.
5. "I'll huff and puff and blow your house down!"
6. The wolf huffed and puffed hard.
7. But the brick house did not move.
8. The wolf huffed and puffed.
9. Still, the house did not fall.
10. The wolf stopped to rest.
11. He wanted another way into the house.
12. The wolf saw the chimney on the roof.

Paragraphs:
1-7: All three pigs now hide together in the strong brick house, and when the wolf finds them and threatens again to huff, puff, and blow it down, the brick house does not move.
8-12: The wolf huffs and puffs again with all his strength, but the house still will not fall, so he stops to rest, wants another way in, and spots the chimney on the roof.

Down the Chimney
1. The wolf climbed up onto the roof.
2. The third pig heard him up there.
3. "He will come down the chimney," said the third pig.
4. The third pig heated a pot of water.
5. Soon, the water in the pot was hot.
6. The wolf climbed down the dark chimney.
7. Splash!
8. The wolf fell into the hot water.
9. He jumped out with a yelp.
10. The wolf ran out through the door.
11. He ran into the woods and never came back.
12. Now the three pigs were safe forever.

Paragraphs:
1-5: The wolf climbs onto the roof, and the third pig hears him and guesses he will come down the chimney, so he quickly heats a pot of water until it is hot.
6-12: The wolf climbs down the dark chimney and falls into the hot water, then jumps out with a yelp and runs away into the woods forever, leaving the three pigs safe at last.
