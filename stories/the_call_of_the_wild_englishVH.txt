The Big House
1. Buck was a big dog, and he lived on a farm in the Santa Clara Valley.
2. Judge Miller owned the farm, and Buck was the king of it.
3. All the other dogs on the farm obeyed Buck, and he loved the Judge and his family.
4. Buck did not know that men in the north had found gold.
5. Those men wanted big strong dogs to pull their sleds in the snow.
6. Buck knew nothing about the cold, and he had never left the farm.
7. Manuel worked on the farm, and he knew about the gold.
8. Manuel liked to play games for money, and he always lost.
9. His wife and his children needed food, and he had no money to buy it.
10. "I will sell a dog," Manuel said to himself.
11. "I will sell Buck, and then I will have money."
12. One night the Judge was away, and Manuel took Buck for a walk.
13. No one saw them go, and no one saw them come to the station.
14. At the station a stranger waited, and he had money in his hand.
15. "Is this the dog?" the stranger asked.
16. "This is the dog," Manuel said, "and he is a very big dog."
17. "Is he a good dog?" the stranger asked.
18. "He is a very good dog," Manuel said.
19. "Give me the money," Manuel said.
20. The stranger gave him the money, and Manuel gave him the dog.
21. Manuel put a rope around Buck's neck, and the stranger took the rope.
22. "You must go with this man, Buck," Manuel said.
23. Buck did not like the rope, and he did not like the stranger.
24. Buck growled at the stranger, but the stranger did not stop.
25. He pulled the rope, and Buck jumped at his throat.
26. The stranger caught Buck by the neck and held the rope tight.
27. Buck could not breathe, and he fell down on the ground.

The Wolves
1. The next night the wolves came down from the hills to the camp.
2. They came out of the trees one by one, and there were many of them.
3. They stood in a ring around Buck, and they did not make a sound.
4. Buck remembered Curly and the ring of dogs, and he stood still in the moon.
5. He stood with his back to the pool, and no wolf could come at him from behind.
6. A big wolf ran at Buck, and Buck cut his neck, and the wolf fell.
7. Two more wolves came at him together, and Buck fought them as he had fought Spitz.
8. He was quick, and he was clever, and he was strong from the forest.
9. Three wolves lay dead on the ground, but the ring did not break.
10. The wolves came again and again, and each time Buck fought them off.
11. He was tired now, and blood ran from his neck and his legs.
12. The whole pack came at him together, and Buck stood at the edge of the water and fought them.
13. At last the wolves stopped and fell back, and they stood in the moon and looked at him.
14. One wolf came out of the ring, and he was thin and grey.
15. Buck knew him, for this was the wolf who had run with him in the forest.
16. The wolf cried softly, and Buck touched noses with him.
17. An old wolf came next, and his face was cut in many places.
18. The old wolf sat down in the moon and lifted his nose to the sky and cried.
19. The other wolves sat down and cried too.
20. Buck sat down among them, and he lifted his nose to the sky and cried with them.
21. It was the cry that he had cried at the pool, and now it came back to him from many voices.
22. When the cry was over, the old wolf got up and ran into the forest.
23. The pack ran behind him, and the thin wolf ran beside Buck.
24. Buck ran with the pack through the trees, and he did not look back at the camp.

The Ghost Dog
1. Many years went by, and the Yeehats saw a change in the wolves of that country.
2. Some of the wolves had brown on their heads and white on their chests, and they were bigger than the other wolves.
3. The Yeehats told each other about a Ghost Dog who ran at the head of the pack.
4. "The Ghost Dog is more clever than any man," they said.
5. "He steals from our camps in the winter, and he kills our dogs."
6. "He is not afraid of our knives, and he is not afraid of our arrows."
7. Some hunters went into the forest and did not come back.
8. Other men found them in the snow, and their throats were cut.
9. Around them were the marks of a wolf's feet, and the marks were bigger than the feet of any wolf.
10. "That is the Ghost Dog," the Yeehats said, "and he is killing our men."
11. The Yeehats did not go near the valley with the pool, for they said that the Ghost Dog lived there.
12. Every summer a wolf came to that valley, and no Yeehat ever saw him there.
13. He was a big wolf with a thick coat, and he came alone out of the forest.
14. He walked down to the pool and stood at the edge of the water.
15. He stayed there for a long time, and he did not move.
16. He lifted his nose to the sky and cried a long cry, and it was a sad cry.
17. When the cry was over, he turned and went back into the forest.
18. In the winter the nights were long, and the wolves went out to hunt.
19. The Yeehats saw the big wolf run at the head of the pack in the moon.
20. He ran faster than any of the other wolves.
21. He cried the cry of the pack, and the wolves cried it after him.
22. That wolf was Buck, who had once been a dog on a farm, and now he was the leader of the wolves.
