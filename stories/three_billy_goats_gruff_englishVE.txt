The Three Billy Goats Gruff

The Bare Hill
1. Three goats lived on a bare hill.
2. Little Goat, Middle Goat, and Big Goat were hungry.
3. The grass on their hill was thin and dry.
4. Across the bridge, a green hill stood tall.
5. Middle Goat: Look at that fresh grass!
6. Big Goat: We will go across the bridge.
7. Little Goat: I will go first.
8. Middle Goat: Good. We will wait here.
9. Little Goat walked down to the bridge.
10. Under the bridge, a troll lived in the dark.

Paragraphs:
1-4: Three hungry goats live on a bare hill and see a tall green hill across a bridge.
5-10: The goats agree that Little Goat will cross first, so he walks down to the bridge, not knowing a troll lives underneath it.

Little Goat Crosses
1. Little Goat's hooves went trip-trap on the bridge.
2. Troll: Who is trip-trapping over my bridge?
3. Little Goat: It is only me, the little goat.
4. Troll: I will eat you up!
5. Little Goat: Do not eat me, Troll.
6. Little Goat: Wait for my brother, Middle Goat.
7. Little Goat: He is bigger than me.
8. Troll: Bigger, you say?
9. Troll: Then go, little goat.
10. Little Goat trotted over the bridge to the green hill.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Little Goat's hooves go trip-trap on the bridge, and the troll stops him, threatening to eat him up.
6-10: Little Goat tells the troll to wait for his bigger brother instead, and the troll lets him trot on to the green hill.

Middle Goat Crosses
1. Middle Goat walked down to the bridge.
2. He was careful and quick.
3. Middle Goat's horns were still small.
4. Middle Goat's hooves went trip-trap on the bridge.
5. Troll: Who is trip-trapping over my bridge?
6. Middle Goat: It is only me, the middle goat.
7. Troll: I will eat you up!
8. Middle Goat: Do not eat me, Troll.
9. Middle Goat: Wait for my brother, Big Goat.
10. Middle Goat: He is bigger than me.
11. Troll: Bigger still?
12. Troll: Then go, middle goat.
13. Middle Goat trotted over the bridge to the green hill.

Paragraphs:
1-8: Middle Goat walks down to the bridge, careful and quick, his horns still small, and the troll stops him and threatens to eat him.
9-13: Middle Goat points the troll toward his even bigger brother, and the troll lets him trot on to the green hill.

Troll Waits, Angry
1. The troll was still hungry.
2. Two goats had crossed to the green hill.
3. The troll had eaten nothing all day.
4. Troll: No more goats will trick me.
5. The troll waited under the dark bridge.
6. He listened hard for more hooves.
7. Big Goat was the biggest of the three.
8. Troll: The next goat, I will eat.

Paragraphs:
1-4: The troll is still hungry, since two goats have already crossed and tricked him out of a meal.
5-8: The troll waits under the bridge, listening for hooves, and decides he will eat the next goat that crosses.

Big Goat Crosses
1. Big Goat walked down to the bridge.
2. Big Goat's hooves went trip-trap on the bridge.
3. Troll: Who is trip-trapping over my bridge?
4. Big Goat: It is I, the big goat.
5. Troll: I will eat you up!
6. Big Goat: Come and try, Troll.
7. Troll: You are not small like your brothers.
8. Big Goat: No, I am big and strong.
9. Troll: I will eat you now!
10. The troll jumped onto the bridge.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Big Goat walks down and crosses the bridge, and the troll stops him and threatens to eat him.
6-10: Big Goat dares the troll to try, and the troll jumps onto the bridge to attack him.

The Fight on the Bridge
1. The troll was hungry and angry.
2. The troll ran at Big Goat.
3. Big Goat did not run.
4. Big Goat lowered his sharp horns.
5. He charged at the troll.
6. Big Goat's horns hit the troll hard.
7. The troll flew off the bridge.
8. The troll fell into the water below.
9. Troll: Help me, I am falling!
10. The water carried the troll away.
11. Big Goat stood alone on the bridge.
12. Big Goat's legs were strong.
13. Big Goat: The bridge is safe now.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The hungry, angry troll runs at Big Goat, who stands his ground, lowers his sharp horns, and charges back.
7-13: Big Goat's horns hit the troll hard, and he flies off the bridge into the water, leaving Big Goat alone and safe.

Grass on the Green Hill
1. Big Goat trotted to the green hill.
2. Little Goat: You crossed the bridge!
3. Middle Goat: Is the troll gone?
4. Big Goat: The troll fell in the water.
5. Big Goat: He will not come back.
6. Little Goat: We can eat all day now.
7. The three goats ate the fresh grass.
8. They grew fat on the green hill.
9. Middle Goat: This grass is the best.
10. Big Goat: We are safe and full.
11. The three goats lived happily on the green hill.

Paragraphs:
1-6: Big Goat trots up to his brothers and tells them the troll fell in the water and will not come back.
7-11: The three goats eat the fresh grass, grow fat, and live happily together on the green hill.
