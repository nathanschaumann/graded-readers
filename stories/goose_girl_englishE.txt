The Goose Girl

The Princess and Falada / The Long Road / The First Refusal / An Oath on the Road / A False Bride
1. A queen lived in a kingdom with her daughter.
2. The princess had golden hair.
3. The princess was kind and wise.
4. The queen had a horse named Falada.
5. Falada spoke like a person.
6. Only the queen and the princess knew this.
7. The queen taught her a wind charm.
8. Far away, a prince lived in another kingdom.
9. The princess would marry him.
10. "Ride to his castle," the queen said.
11. The queen told a maid to ride with her.
12. She rode away from the queen.
13. The princess rode Falada down the road toward the castle.
14. The maid rode another horse near her.
15. They would ride for days.
16. The maid was proud.
17. The princess missed the queen.
18. But she wanted to see the prince.
19. Falada spoke to the princess.
20. "I will help you," Falada said.
21. The princess smiled.
22. The maid overheard and knew Falada's secret.
23. One day, on the road, the princess was thirsty.
24. She saw a stream near the road.
25. "Please bring me water," she said to the maid.
26. "I will not serve you anymore," said the maid.
27. The princess was surprised.
28. She walked to the stream herself.
29. She bent down and drank the water.
30. The maid watched her and smiled to herself.
31. The princess said nothing.
32. They rode on toward the prince's castle.
33. The next day, on the road, the princess was thirsty again.
34. "Please bring me water," she said again.
35. "Get the water yourself," said the maid.
36. The princess said nothing and bent down to drink.
37. The maid saw this and was not afraid.
38. "Get down from Falada," said the maid.
39. "I will take your clothes," she said.
40. The princess was afraid and obeyed her.
41. "Swear to me you will tell no person," said the maid.
42. "If you tell, you will die," she said.
43. The princess swore the oath.
44. They changed clothes and horses near the stream.
45. The maid rode Falada in the princess's clothes.
46. The princess rode the maid's old horse instead.
47. The bride rode Falada to the old king's castle.
48. He came to see her.
49. The prince took her hand and smiled.
50. "You are beautiful," he said.
51. The king was glad for his son.
52. He planned a wedding feast.
53. Servants helped with the feast.
54. The bride smiled at everyone.
55. She was glad about her plan.
56. No one at the old castle knew her secret.
57. Falada watched the bride and said nothing.

Paragraphs:
1-7: A queen and her daughter, a princess with golden hair who is kind and wise, live in a kingdom, and the queen has a horse named Falada who can talk, though only she and the princess know it; the queen also teaches her a charm for the wind.
8-12: Far away, a prince lives in another kingdom, and the princess will marry him, so the queen tells her to ride to his castle and picks a maid to go with her, and the princess feels glad but a little afraid as she rides away from the queen.
13-16: The princess and the maid ride out together, Falada and another horse carrying them for a journey of days, and the maid is proud.
17-22: The princess misses the queen but wants to see the prince, though the prince's kingdom is far away; Falada speaks to the princess, promising to help her, and she smiles, but the maid overhears and now knows Falada's secret.
23-27: One day the princess grows thirsty and asks the maid to bring her water, but the maid refuses to serve her anymore, surprising the princess.
28-32: The princess walks to the stream and drinks the water herself while the maid watches and smiles to herself, and the princess says nothing as they ride on toward the prince's castle.
33-39: The next day the princess is thirsty again and asks the maid for water, but the maid refuses and tells her to get it herself, and seeing that the princess will not fight back, the maid is no longer afraid to demand she get off Falada and hand over her clothes.
40-46: Afraid, the princess obeys, and the maid makes her swear to tell no person what happened, or die, so the princess swears the oath, and the two change clothes and horses by the stream, with the maid now wearing the princess's clothes and riding Falada while the princess rides the maid's old horse instead.
47-51: The bride rides Falada to the old king's castle, where he comes to greet her, and the prince takes her hand and smiles, calling her beautiful, so the king is glad for his son.
52-57: He plans a wedding feast, and servants help with it, and the bride smiles at everyone, glad about her plan, for no one at the castle knows her secret, though Falada watches the bride and says nothing.

The Girl and the Geese / Falada's Fate / A Day with the Geese / The Goose Boy Complains
1. The girl stood near the castle gate.
2. No one looked at her.
3. Everyone at the castle looked at the bride instead.
4. The old king saw the girl.
5. "Who is this girl?" he asked the bride.
6. "She can tend the geese," said the bride.
7. He agreed.
8. A goose boy watched the king's geese in a field.
9. "You will help him," the king told the girl.
10. The girl said nothing and obeyed.
11. Falada had seen the clothes and horses change.
12. The bride knew that Falada spoke.
13. The bride was afraid that Falada would tell the truth.
14. She told the old king that Falada should die.
15. The old king agreed and knew nothing about the secret.
16. Servants took Falada away, and his head was cut.
17. The goose girl said nothing, but she had a plan.
18. "Please nail Falada's head over the town gate," she said.
19. "I pass here with the geese every morning," she told him.
20. The servant agreed to help her.
21. He nailed the head over the gate that morning.
22. No one but the goose girl knew the truth about Falada.
23. Every morning, the goose girl came to the gate with the geese.
24. She missed Falada and was glad no one knew.
25. Falada's head hung over the gate and spoke to her.
26. "If the queen knew this, her heart would break," it said.
27. She drove the geese to a field outside the town.
28. The goose boy waited for her.
29. The goose girl had golden hair.
30. The goose boy wanted to pull it.
31. The goose girl remembered her wind charm.
32. "Blow, wind, blow his hat away," she said.
33. The wind blew and took his hat.
34. The goose boy ran for his hat.
35. The goose girl combed her hair in peace.
36. Every day, the wind blew the goose boy's hat away.
37. The goose boy did not like this anymore.
38. He went to the old king.
39. "I do not want to help her anymore," he said.
40. "Why not?" the old king asked.
41. "Odd things happen every morning," said the goose boy.
42. "A horse's head speaks at the gate," he said.
43. "The wind blows my hat away," he told him.
44. The old king was curious about this.
45. "I will come and see," he said.
46. The goose boy smiled and said nothing.
47. The old king planned to see it all.

The Old King Watches / The Stove Loophole / The Stove Confession / Restoration
1. The next morning, the old king rose early.
2. He followed the goose girl to the gate.
3. He hid near the gate and watched her.
4. "If the queen knew this, her heart would break," it said.
5. The old king heard this and was surprised.
6. He wondered who this goose girl really was.
7. He followed the goose girl to the field.
8. He hid again and watched the geese and the goose boy.
9. The goose boy tried to pull the goose girl's hair.
10. The goose girl called on the wind again.
11. The wind blew and took the boy's hat.
12. He decided to learn the truth.
13. That day, the old king went to the goose girl.
14. "Tell me who you really are," he said.
15. The goose girl was afraid to speak.
16. "I cannot tell any person," she said.
17. "I swore an oath on the road," she told him.
18. The old king thought about her words.
19. He knew she would not tell any living person.
20. But a stove is not a person.
21. "Tell your story to the iron stove," the old king said.
22. "I will listen at the pipe outside," he said.
23. The goose girl understood the old king's plan.
24. She agreed to tell the stove her story.
25. That evening, the goose girl came to the stove room.
26. She thought no one was in the room with the stove.
27. The old king was outside, near the pipe, and listened.
28. The goose girl told the iron stove all her story.
29. "I am really a princess," she said to the stove.
30. "A queen was my mother, and Falada was my horse."
31. "My maid told me to swear an oath on the road."
32. "The maid and I changed clothes and horses on the road."
33. "She is the bride, and I am the goose girl."
34. "The bride had Falada's head cut."
35. "I asked for his head over the gate."
36. "His head speaks to me at the gate every morning," she said.
37. The old king listened to every word.
38. The next morning, the old king came to the goose girl.
39. "I heard your story last night," he said.
40. "I know you are the true princess," he said.
41. The goose girl was surprised that he knew.
42. "You do not have to hide anymore," said the old king.
43. The old king had royal clothes brought for her.
44. She wore clothes like a true princess.
45. Her golden hair shone in the morning sun.
46. The goose girl looked like a true princess again.
47. "Come with me to the court," he said.
48. The old king led her to the prince.
49. The prince looked at her golden hair with wonder.
50. "She is the true princess," said the old king.

The False Bride's Sentence / A Wedding at Last
1. That night, there was a feast at the castle.
2. The true princess sat near the prince.
3. The bride did not know the true princess sat there.
4. The old king wanted to ask the bride a question.
5. "A maid tricked a good princess," the old king said.
6. "The maid made the princess swear a false oath," he said.
7. "What should happen to a maid like this?" he asked.
8. The bride did not know he meant her.
9. "She should be sent away forever," said the bride.
10. "You have chosen your own fate," said the old king.
11. "You are the maid, and here is the true princess," he said.
12. The bride was shocked to see the princess.
13. The old king sent the bride away forever.
14. The false bride never returned to the castle.
15. Soon the true princess married the prince.
16. The wedding was at the old king's castle.
17. The queen came from her own kingdom to see it.
18. The goose boy came to the wedding too.
19. The true princess wore golden clothes.
20. The prince took her hand and smiled.
21. Falada was gone, but the princess remembered him.
22. The people loved their new princess.
