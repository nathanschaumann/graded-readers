Goldilocks and the Three Bears

The Three Bears
1. Three bears lived in a house in a big wood.
2. One was a big bear, one was a middle bear.
3. One was a little bear.
4. Every morning, the bears made hot porridge.
5. But today, the porridge was too hot to eat.
6. "Let's walk while it cools," said the big bear.
7. The three bears walked into the wood.
8. Nearby, a girl named Goldilocks walked through the wood too.
9. She had golden hair, like her name.
10. Goldilocks saw a small house among the trees.
11. She walked toward it.

Paragraphs:
1-7: Three bears — a big one, a middle one, and a little one — live in a house in a big wood, and one morning their porridge is too hot to eat, so they decide to walk while it cools.
8-11: Nearby, a girl named Goldilocks walks through the wood too, sees a small house among the trees, and walks toward it.

The Three Bowls of Porridge
1. Goldilocks was hungry after her long walk.
2. She knocked on the door, but no one answered.
3. She pushed the door open and walked inside.
4. Goldilocks saw three bowls of porridge on the table.
5. She tasted the porridge in the big bowl.
6. It burned her tongue.
7. "This is too hot!" said Goldilocks.
8. Next, she tasted the porridge in the middle bowl.
9. "This is too cold!" said Goldilocks.
10. Then she tasted the porridge in the little bowl.
11. "This is just right!" said Goldilocks.
12. Goldilocks ate up all the porridge in the little bowl.

Paragraphs:
1-7: Hungry after her long walk, Goldilocks knocks on the door of the empty house, goes inside, sees three bowls of porridge, and finds the big bowl so hot it burns her tongue.
8-12: She finds the middle bowl too cold, then the little bowl just right, so she eats up all its porridge.

The Three Chairs
1. After she ate, Goldilocks saw three chairs by the fire.
2. Then she climbed onto the big chair.
3. "This is too hard!" said Goldilocks.
4. Her back began to hurt.
5. She climbed onto the middle chair.
6. "This is too soft!" said Goldilocks.
7. She climbed onto the little chair.
8. "This is just right!" said Goldilocks.
9. But the little chair was too small for her.
10. It broke into pieces under her weight.
11. Goldilocks fell down onto the floor.
12. She stood up and looked at the broken chair.

Paragraphs:
1-6: After eating, Goldilocks finds three chairs by the fire; the big chair is so hard her back begins to hurt, and the middle chair is too soft.
7-12: The little chair feels just right, but it is too small for her and breaks into pieces under her weight, so Goldilocks falls to the floor and looks at the broken chair.

The Three Beds
1. Goldilocks was tired from her long walk.
2. No one else was in the house.
3. She walked upstairs to look for a bed.
4. Goldilocks lay down on the big bed.
5. "This is too hard!" said Goldilocks.
6. She lay down on the middle bed.
7. "This is too soft!" said Goldilocks.
8. She lay down on the little bed.
9. "This is just right!" said Goldilocks.
10. Goldilocks pulled up the blanket and closed her eyes.
11. Soon, she fell fast asleep in the little bed.

Paragraphs:
1-7: Tired from her long walk, Goldilocks finds no one else home, goes upstairs, and tries the big bed and the middle bed, but one is too hard and the other too soft.
8-11: The little bed feels just right, so Goldilocks pulls up the blanket, closes her eyes, and soon falls fast asleep in it.

The Bears Come Home
1. Soon, the three bears came home from their walk.
2. The door of their house was open.
3. The hungry bears saw their empty bowls.
4. "Someone ate my porridge!" said the big bear.
5. "Someone ate my porridge too!" said the middle bear.
6. "Someone ate all my porridge!" cried the little bear.
7. Then they saw their chairs by the fire.
8. "Someone sat in my chair!" said the big bear.
9. "Someone sat in my chair too!" said the middle bear.
10. The little bear looked at the pieces on the floor.
11. "Someone broke my chair!" he cried.
12. They did not know who came, so they walked upstairs.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The three bears come home from their walk to find their door open and their bowls empty, and one by one they discover that someone has eaten their porridge.
7-12: The bears then see their chairs by the fire, and the little bear finds the pieces of his broken chair on the floor, so they walk upstairs not knowing who came.

Goldilocks Runs Away
1. Upstairs, the three bears looked at their beds.
2. "Someone slept in my bed!" said the big bear.
3. "Someone slept in my bed too!" said the middle bear.
4. "Someone slept in my bed!" cried the little bear.
5. Goldilocks was still asleep in the little bear's bed.
6. The little bear cried, and Goldilocks woke up.
7. She opened her eyes and saw the three bears.
8. Goldilocks jumped up, scared of the bears.
9. Her heart beat fast.
10. She ran out of the bed and down the stairs.
11. Goldilocks ran out the door and into the wood.
12. She never came back to the small house again.

Paragraphs:
1-6: Upstairs, the bears find their beds disturbed too, and the little bear finds Goldilocks still asleep in his bed and cries out, waking her.
7-12: Goldilocks opens her eyes, sees the three bears, and jumps up scared with her heart beating fast, then runs out of the house and into the wood, never to return.
