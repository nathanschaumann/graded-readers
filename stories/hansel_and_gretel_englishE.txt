Hansel and Gretel

Not Enough Food / The White Stones / Left in the Forest / The Way Home
1. A poor woodcutter lived near a forest.
2. He lived with his two children, Hansel and Gretel.
3. Their mother had died when the children were young.
4. Soon he had a new wife, their stepmother.
5. The stepmother did not love Hansel and Gretel.
6. But that year, people had little money to buy wood.
7. Hansel and Gretel were hungry too.
8. The stepmother did not want two hungry children.
9. She talked to the woodcutter.
10. "We have too little food," she said.
11. "We must leave the children in the forest," said the stepmother.
12. He loved his children, but did not want to lose them.
13. He agreed to the stepmother's plan.
14. Soon, Hansel and Gretel were too hungry to sleep.
15. They heard the stepmother's plan.
16. The poor woodcutter wanted food for his hungry children.
17. Gretel cried.
18. "Do not cry, Gretel," said Hansel.
19. He got up and went outside.
20. White stones shone near the house in the moonlight.
21. Hansel filled his pockets with the stones.
22. He lay near Gretel.
23. "Do not be afraid," he said.
24. Soon the children slept.
25. Soon, the family left the house and walked to the forest.
26. The woodcutter said they would cut wood.
27. Hansel walked behind the family.
28. He took a stone from his pocket.
29. He dropped the stone on the path.
30. Hansel did this again and again.
31. Soon, the family came to a deep part of the forest.
32. The woodcutter made a fire for them from wood he had cut.
33. "Wait here. We must cut wood," said the woodcutter.
34. The woodcutter did not want to leave them alone.
35. The stepmother and the woodcutter walked away.
36. Hansel and Gretel waited by the fire.
37. The fire grew small, and night came.
38. The children were alone in the dark forest.
39. Gretel cried again.
40. "The moon will show the way home," said Hansel.
41. The white stones shone in the moonlight.
42. Hansel and Gretel followed the stones.
43. They walked in the night.
44. Soon they saw their house.
45. Hansel and Gretel went to the house.
46. The woodcutter came outside and saw the children.
47. He was glad to see Hansel and Gretel.
48. But the stepmother was not glad.
49. The house still had little food.
50. Soon winter came, and food grew scarce.
51. The children grew thin and cold.
52. That winter, the stepmother wanted to leave the children again.

The Locked Door / Deeper into the Woods / The Crumbs Are Gone / Lost / The Candy House
1. That night, Hansel heard the stepmother again.
2. "We must leave the children again," she said.
3. Hansel got up again for stones.
4. But the door would not open.
5. The stepmother had locked the door.
6. He went back to Gretel.
7. "The door is locked," he said.
8. Gretel was afraid again.
9. Hansel and Gretel waited in the house.
10. The house was cold.
11. "I have bread from supper," said Hansel.
12. "I will drop crumbs, not stones," he said.
13. Hansel broke the bread apart.
14. Soon, the family walked to the forest again.
15. The children were hungry again.
16. But this time, they walked deeper in the forest.
17. The path grew narrow and dark.
18. Hansel dropped crumbs of bread on the path.
19. Birds sat near the path and watched the crumbs fall.
20. He did this again and again.
21. Gretel held Hansel's hand.
22. Gretel was afraid, but Hansel was not.
23. Soon, the family came to a deeper part of the forest.
24. Again, the woodcutter made a fire and said they would cut wood.
25. The stepmother and the woodcutter left again.
26. Hansel and Gretel waited near the fire.
27. Again, the fire grew small.
28. The children were alone in the dark forest again.
29. When night came, Hansel and Gretel got up.
30. "We can follow the crumbs home now," said Hansel.
31. But the crumbs were gone.
32. Birds had eaten every crumb.
33. They were lost.
34. The forest looked the same in every direction.
35. Gretel cried again.
36. Hansel was not afraid, but Gretel was.
37. "We will get home soon," he said.
38. But Hansel was not sure this time.
39. For three days, Hansel and Gretel walked in the forest.
40. They were hungry every day.
41. They had nothing to eat.
42. Gretel could not walk in the forest.
43. Gretel cried, for she was hungry.
44. Hansel did not want Gretel to cry.
45. Then a white bird came to them.
46. Hansel and Gretel followed the small bird.
47. The bird flew from tree to tree.
48. Soon, the bird led them to a small clearing.
49. Ahead stood a strange house.
50. The old house was made of bread and sugar.
51. The forest was dark near the strange house.
52. Hansel and Gretel ate the bread and sugar.
53. Then an old woman came from the house.
54. Hansel held Gretel's cold hand.
55. "Who eats my house?" said the old woman.
56. The old woman could not see well.
57. She touched Hansel and Gretel with her cold hands.
58. "You poor children," said the old woman.
59. "Come inside, and I will feed you," she said.
60. The poor children went inside with her.
61. The old woman had a plan.

Paragraphs:
1-5: That night Hansel hears the stepmother say they must leave the children again, so he gets up for more stones, but the door will not open, for the stepmother has locked it, and Hansel cannot go outside.
6-13: He goes back to Gretel and tells her the door is locked, and though she is afraid again, Hansel and Gretel wait in the cold house until Hansel says he has bread from supper and will drop crumbs instead of stones, and he breaks the bread apart.
14-21: Soon the family walks to the forest again, for the children are hungry again, but this time they go deeper and the path grows narrow and dark, so Hansel drops crumbs of bread on the path while birds sit nearby and watch them fall, doing this again and again, and Gretel holds Hansel's hand.
22-28: Gretel is afraid, but Hansel is not, and soon the family comes to a part of the forest deeper than before, where the woodcutter again makes a fire and says they will cut wood, but the stepmother and the woodcutter leave again, and as Hansel and Gretel wait near the fire, it grows small again and the children are alone in the dark forest again.
29-33: When night comes, Hansel and Gretel get up, and Hansel says they can follow the crumbs home now, but the crumbs are gone, for birds have eaten every crumb, so Hansel and Gretel have no path to follow, and though the stones had worked, the crumbs do not, and they are lost.
34-38: This time they have no stones to show them the way, and the forest looks the same in every direction, so Gretel cries again, and though Hansel is not afraid, Gretel is, but when he says they will get home soon, Hansel is not sure this time.
39-44: For three days Hansel and Gretel walk in the forest, hungry every day with nothing to eat, until Gretel can not walk in the forest and cries from hunger, though Hansel does not want her to cry.
45-49: Then a white bird comes to them, and Hansel and Gretel follow it as it flies from tree to tree, until soon it leads them to a small clearing, where ahead stands a strange house.
50-54: The strange house is made of bread and sugar in the dark forest, and Hansel and Gretel, hungry and afraid, eat the bread and sugar, so an old woman comes from the house, and Hansel holds Gretel's hand.
55-61: "Who eats my house?" asks the old woman, who cannot see well, so she touches Hansel and Gretel with her cold hands and, calling them poor children, invites them inside to feed them; glad for the food, the children go inside with her, no longer feeling lost, though the old woman has a plan of her own.

The Witch's True Face / The Bone Trick / Into the Oven / The Witch's Treasure / The Wide Water / Home Again
1. The old witch lived alone in the little house near the forest.
2. The old witch grabbed Hansel.
3. She put him in a cage in the house.
4. The old witch locked the cage.
5. "You will grow fat here," said the witch.
6. "Then I will eat you," she said.
7. Gretel wanted to leave the house again, but she could not.
8. "You must cook and clean for me," said the witch.
9. Every day, Gretel did what the old witch said.
10. Gretel cooked and carried water for the witch.
11. Every day, the witch fed Hansel a lot of food again.
12. Hansel stayed in the cage for many days.
13. The little cage was cold.
14. Every day, the witch checked Hansel's finger.
15. She wanted to know if he was fat.
16. But the witch could not see well.
17. She touched his finger to check instead.
18. Gretel was afraid every day and cooked food for the witch.
19. The witch went away every day.
20. Hansel had an old bone in the cage.
21. He held out the bone, not his finger.
22. The witch touched the thin bone.
23. "You are still too thin," said the witch.
24. The witch put Hansel back in the cage.
25. She checked him again and again.
26. Hansel was still in the cage, but the witch would not wait.
27. "I will cook Hansel," said the witch.
28. The witch made the oven hot.
29. "Gretel, look in the oven," said the witch.
30. Gretel did not want to lose Hansel.
31. Gretel did not know how to look in the oven.
32. "Show me how," said Gretel.
33. The witch put her head in the oven.
34. Though afraid, Gretel loved Hansel and did what she must.
35. Gretel pushed the witch in, and she could not get out.
36. Gretel shut the oven door.
37. The old witch died alone in the hot oven.
38. Hansel, still thin and afraid, heard the witch cry out.
39. Gretel opened the cage, and Hansel came out to her.
40. Hansel and Gretel looked around the witch's dark house.
41. The house had gold.
42. Jewels and pearls were in the house too.
43. Hansel filled his pockets with gold.
44. Gretel filled her pockets with jewels and pearls.
45. "We are rich," said Hansel.
46. "This gold will feed our family," he said.
47. Hansel and Gretel wanted to go home to see the woodcutter again.
48. Gretel held Hansel's hand and was glad to leave the dark house.
49. They left the witch's house and walked to the forest.
50. Hansel and Gretel walked to a wide water.
51. They could not cross the water.
52. A white duck came to them.
53. "Will you carry us across the water?" said Hansel.
54. The duck carried Hansel across, and then Gretel too.
55. They thanked the white duck, and the duck flew away.
56. The water was cold and wide.
57. They walked in the dark forest again.
58. Hansel and Gretel followed the path home.
59. They walked for three days.
60. The forest was cold in the night.
61. They still had gold in their pockets.
62. Soon they saw their old house.
63. The woodcutter was alone and sad every day.
64. The stepmother was gone.
65. Hansel and Gretel went to him.
66. "You are home!" said the woodcutter.
67. He held Hansel and Gretel again.
68. The woodcutter cried from joy.
69. Hansel gave him the gold and jewels.
70. The gold shone in the woodcutter's hands.
71. "This gold will feed our family," said Hansel.
72. The family was not poor now, and they were never hungry again.
