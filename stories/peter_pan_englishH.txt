Peter Pan

The Nursery / The Boy Comes Back
1. Wendy, John, and Michael lived in a tall house in a big city.
2. Their room was called the nursery.
3. Every night, the mother came to the nursery and told the children a story.
4. The best stories were about Peter Pan.
5. "Peter Pan is a boy who never grows up," the mother said.
6. "His home is an island called Neverland, far away over the sea."
7. Often, at story time, a boy came and sat outside the nursery window.
8. He listened at the window, for he had no mother to tell him stories.
9. One night, the mother saw the boy at the window.
10. She ran to him, and the boy flew out into the night.
11. The mother closed the window fast.
12. The boy was fast, but his shadow was not.
13. The window caught the shadow, and the shadow came off him.
14. The mother took the shadow up from the floor.
15. She knew the boy would come back for it.
16. She rolled up the shadow and put it away in a drawer.
17. One night, not long after, the nursery window was open.
18. A little light flew in through the window.
19. The light was a fairy, and her name was Tinker Bell.
20. She flew around the dark room, and her light went with her.
21. Peter Pan flew in after her.
22. He had come back for his shadow.
23. "Find my shadow, Tinker Bell," said Peter.
24. The fairy flew to the drawer, and her light fell on it.
25. Peter opened the drawer, pulled his shadow out, and was glad to have it back.
26. He put the shadow at his feet, but it fell off.
27. He put soap on the shadow, but it fell to the floor again.
28. Tinker Bell pulled at the shadow, but the shadow would not stick to Peter.
29. Peter sat down on the floor and cried.
30. Wendy woke in her bed and saw the boy on the floor, with tears on his face.
31. "Boy, why are you sad?" Wendy asked.
32. "My shadow will not stick to me," said the boy.
33. "I am Peter Pan," he said.
34. "I am Wendy," she said.
35. "I can sew your shadow on for you."

29-35: Peter sits on the floor and cries, and Wendy wakes and sees the boy with tears on his face; she asks why he is sad, he says his shadow will not stick and names himself Peter Pan, and Wendy gives her own name and says she can sew the shadow on for him.

The Shadow Sewed On / Fairy Dust
1. Wendy found a needle and thread, and Peter took the shadow up from the floor for her.
2. She sat down on her bed, and Peter sat still at her feet.
3. Wendy sewed the shadow to Peter's feet with the needle and thread.
4. The needle went in and out, in and out of the shadow.
5. It hurt a little, but Peter did not pull his feet away.
6. When the shadow was on him, Peter jumped up from the floor.
7. When Peter flew up, the shadow went up, and when he came down, it came down.
8. "See, Wendy, my shadow comes with me!" said Peter.
9. John and Michael woke in their beds and saw the boy over them.
10. "Who are you?" John asked.
11. "I am Peter Pan, and I come from Neverland," said Peter.
12. "The lost boys are on the island with me, and I tell them the stories I hear at your window."
13. "They fell from their beds when they were little, and no mother came for them."
14. "Come with me, Wendy, and you can be our mother."
15. "You can tell us stories at night and sew for us."
16. "I cannot leave John and Michael," said Wendy.
17. "They can come too," said Peter, and John and Michael got out of their beds fast to go with him.
18. "How can we go to Neverland?" John asked.
19. "We cannot fly like you," said Wendy.
20. "You can fly too," said Peter to the children.
21. "I blow fairy dust on you, and you think of happy things."
22. "The dust and the happy things take you up into the air, and you fly."
23. Peter took Tinker Bell in his hand and blew her bright dust over the children and their beds.
24. Tinker Bell was angry when her dust fell on Wendy.
25. "Think of happy things now," said Peter, and the children closed their eyes to think.
26. Michael thought of his mother's stories, and his feet came up off the floor.
27. "I can fly!" Michael called.
28. He flew over the beds and did not know how to come down to the floor.
29. John thought of pirates and pirate ships, and went up into the air after him.
30. Wendy thought of stories at night for the lost boys, and up she went too.
31. The children flew around the nursery with Peter, and their shadows flew with them on the floor.
32. At the window, Peter took a button from his coat and put it in Wendy's hand.
33. Wendy put the button on a string and put the string around her neck.
34. "We go to Neverland now," said Peter.
35. "How do we find Neverland?" John asked.
36. "The second star to the right, and straight on till morning," said Peter.
37. Peter flew out through the window into the night, and Tinker Bell flew with him.
38. Wendy, John, and Michael flew out after him, over the city and up into the stars.

The Flight to Neverland / The Wendy Bird
1. All night, Peter, Wendy, John, and Michael flew on over the dark sea, under the stars and the moon.
2. Michael was the littlest, and soon, over the dark sea, his eyes closed.
3. He thought of nothing at all, and down he fell through the air toward the sea.
4. Wendy cried out, but Peter was fast and caught Michael by the arm.
5. "Think of happy things again, Michael," said Peter.
6. Michael thought of the island, and up he went over the sea again.
7. Soon John's eyes closed too.
8. Wendy saw and pulled him up by the arm.
9. Tinker Bell flew down to Wendy and pulled her hair.
10. "Tinker Bell is jealous of you," said Peter to Wendy.
11. When morning came, the sun came up far over the sea.
12. Far below them, down on the sea, they saw an island.
13. "There is Neverland," said Peter.
14. They came down out of the morning air and flew low over the island.
15. They saw the woods of the island, and a lagoon with mermaids in the water.
16. On the sea near the island, low in the water, they saw a ship.
17. "A pirate ship!" John called out.
18. "The captain of that ship is called Hook," said Peter.
19. Tinker Bell flew down toward the woods first, fast, and Peter did not see her go.
20. In the air over the island, Tinker Bell came back to Wendy.
21. "Follow me down, and you will see the home of the lost boys first," said the fairy.
22. Wendy followed her down toward the woods.
23. Tinker Bell flew fast, and soon Wendy was far back, alone in the air.
24. "A great white bird comes, and Peter says you must shoot it down!" Tinker Bell called to the lost boys.
25. The boys believed her, for she was Peter's fairy.
26. One boy had a bow and a sharp arrow, and he looked up over the trees.
27. A white thing flew over the trees toward them.
28. "The Wendy bird!" called the boys, and the boy shot his arrow up at it.
29. The arrow hit, and Wendy fell down through the branches to the grass.
30. The boys ran to her and saw a girl, not a bird.
31. "She is dead," said one of the boys.
32. Peter flew down through the trees with John and Michael.
33. "I brought you a mother, and you have shot her!" said Peter.
34. "Tinker Bell told us to shoot the bird," said the boy with the bow.
35. "I did not say that," said Peter, and he turned to the fairy.
36. "Go away from me, Tinker Bell, and do not come back for many days."
37. But Wendy was not dead.
38. The arrow had hit the button on the string at her neck.
39. Wendy opened her eyes and saw the boys around her.

The Home Under the Ground / Captain Hook and the Crocodile
1. The lost boys went home under the ground, and Wendy, John, and Michael went with them.
2. Every boy went down through his hollow tree, for the trees were the doors of the home.
3. One tree was wide, for one of the lost boys was big.
4. Under the ground there was one warm room, with a bed for every one of the boys.
5. Wendy was the mother of the home from that night on.
6. She told the boys stories every night, as her mother had told her.
7. She sewed their clothes with her needle and thread.
8. Every night, she gave the boys their medicine from a little cup.
9. The boys did not like the medicine, but they drank it, for Wendy was the mother.
10. Peter was the father of the home, and the boys did as he said.
11. All day, the children hunted and played in the woods with the lost boys.
12. John and Michael forgot their old home a little more every day.
13. But Wendy did not forget, and she told them of their mother and father.
14. After many days, Tinker Bell came back to the home under the ground and said she was sorry.
15. Peter was angry for days, but he let her come home.
16. One night, after the medicine and the stories, Wendy asked Peter about fairies.
17. "A fairy light is strong when children believe in fairies," said Peter.
18. "When children say they do not believe in fairies, a fairy light must go out."
19. The pirate ship of Captain Hook was on the sea near the island.
20. Hook, the captain of the pirates, had a long coat with gold buttons and long hair.
21. His right hand was gone.
22. An iron hook was on his right arm now.
23. That was why the pirates called him Hook.
24. In a great fight one day, Peter Pan had cut off Hook's right hand.
25. The hand fell into the sea, and a crocodile ate it.
26. From that day on, the crocodile followed Hook over the sea, for it wanted the rest of him.
27. One day, the crocodile ate a clock too.
28. The clock was still inside it, and it still made its sound: tick, tick.
29. When the crocodile came near the ship, the tick came first.
30. When Hook heard the tick, he knew the crocodile was near, and he turned and ran.
31. One night, on the ship, Hook talked to his pirates about Peter Pan and the fight.
32. "It was Peter Pan who cut off my hand," Hook said to them that night.
33. "One day I will find his home, and I will kill him with my iron hook."
34. From the dark water came a sound: tick, tick.
35. The great crocodile swam near the ship, and its two green eyes were up over the dark water.
36. Hook turned white at the sound and ran inside the ship.

Paragraphs:
1-4: The lost boys take the children to their home under the ground, where hollow trees are the doors and every boy goes down through his own tree — one tree wide because one boy is big — into one warm room with a bed for every boy.

The Voice at the Lagoon / The Fight at the Lagoon
1. One day, the children went down to the lagoon to see the mermaids in the water.
2. A great rock stood up out of the water in the lagoon.
3. When the sun went low, the mermaids swam down to their caves under the water.
4. The children sat on the great rock in the last light.
5. A boat came over the water from the pirate ship.
6. Peter saw it first, and the children went down from the rock into the water to hide.
7. In the boat were two pirates, and with them was a girl.
8. The girl was Tiger Lily, the princess of the island people.
9. Her hands were tied with rope.
10. She had gone too near the pirate ship in the night, and the pirates had caught her.
11. The two pirates put Tiger Lily out on the wet rock.
12. "The water will rise over the rock in the night," said one pirate.
13. "And the princess cannot swim with her hands tied," said the other pirate.
14. Behind the rock, Peter made his voice deep, like the voice of Hook.
15. "Set the princess free!" came the deep voice over the water.
16. The two pirates believed it was the voice of their captain, Hook.
17. They were afraid, and they took the ropes off Tiger Lily.
18. Tiger Lily went down into the water without a sound, and she saw Peter behind the rock.
19. She swam away home to her people, and Peter laughed low at his trick.
20. But that night, Hook came out to the lagoon.
21. He swam out to the rock, and the two pirates took him up into the boat.
22. "We set the princess free, as you told us," said a pirate.
23. "I did not tell you that," said Hook.
24. "A voice came over the water, and it was your voice," said the other pirate.
25. "Who is there?" Hook called out over the dark water of the lagoon.
26. "I am Peter Pan!" came the voice of a boy over the water.
27. "Take him!" Hook called to his pirates.
28. Peter flew up over the rock with his dagger out.
29. Hook jumped from the boat to the rock, and his iron hook went at Peter through the air.
30. Peter and Hook fought on the rock, over the dark water.
31. Again and again the iron hook came down at Peter, and again and again Peter was not there.
32. From the dark water came the tick, tick of the clock, and Hook stood still.
33. The crocodile came up out of the water with its jaws open.
34. Hook jumped into the water and swam for his ship, and the crocodile swam after him.
35. The water had risen over the rock, and Peter took Wendy's hand, and they flew home over the water.
36. Tiger Lily told her people that Peter had set her free at the rock.
37. After that night, the island people were friends of Peter and the lost boys.

The Open Window / Wendy's Last Story
1. Far away, over the sea, the tall house stood in the big city.
2. In the nursery of the tall house, three beds stood empty, night after night.
3. Every night, the mother opened the nursery window and kept watch.
4. "The window must be open when the children come home," she said to the father.
5. The father sat with her by the open window in the cold.
6. Days went by, and then months went by, and still the window stood open, and still the mother waited.
7. But one day, Wendy asked John about the home over the sea.
8. "John, do you remember our mother and our father?" she asked.
9. John thought and thought.
10. "I remember a little of the home," said John.
11. "I remember the stories, but I do not remember her face."
12. Wendy turned to Michael.
13. "Michael, do you remember our mother?"
14. "You are my mother, Wendy," said Michael.
15. Wendy looked at him and had nothing to say.
16. "Soon we must go home to our mother and father," said Wendy.
17. John and Michael said yes, and then they ran out to play again.
18. That night, Wendy gave the boys the medicine from the cup, and then she told them a story.
19. "There was a mother who had three children," said Wendy to the boys.
20. "One night, the children flew away from her, out the window and far away over the sea."
21. "The mother waited for them, and the window was open every night, for she knew they would come home."
22. "Did the three children go home to her?" said Michael.
23. "They flew home to her at last, and the mother was at the open window."
24. "That mother is our mother, and we are the three children in the story," said Wendy.
25. "We must go home to our mother tonight."
26. "I want to see our mother," said Michael, and John came up out of his bed.
27. The lost boys did not want Wendy to go.
28. "Come with us, and our mother and father will take you in," she said.
29. The lost boys said they would come to the home over the sea.
30. But Peter said, "I will not go with you."
31. "In that home, they will make me go to school and grow up, and I will never grow up."
32. Wendy filled Peter's cup with his medicine and put it on the shelf by the wide tree.
33. "Promise me you will drink your medicine every night after we go home," said Wendy.
34. "I promise," said Peter, and he went to his bed.
35. After the fight at the lagoon, Hook had walked the dark woods every night to find Peter's home.
36. At last, he had seen one of the lost boys go down a hollow tree.
37. Now Hook and his pirates waited in the woods over the home of Peter and the boys.

Caught by the Pirates / I Believe in Fairies
1. That night, the children went up out of the home under the ground, and Peter did not go with them.
2. Wendy went up, and John and Michael and the lost boys came after her, one by one.
3. But the pirates of Hook waited at the trees in the dark.
4. As they came up, the pirates took them and put hands over their mouths.
5. No cry and no sound went down to Peter under the ground.
6. The pirates tied the children and the lost boys with ropes, hand and foot.
7. They carried them through the dark woods, down to the black ship at anchor on the sea.
8. Hook did not go down to the ship with his pirates.
9. The trees were too little for Hook, but the wide tree took him down.
10. At the foot of the tree, the way in was low.
11. Hook could not get through the low way, but he could see the bed where Peter slept.
12. On the shelf by the foot of the tree was the cup of medicine.
13. Hook carried a little bottle of poison with him that night.
14. His arm went in through the low way, and the poison went into the cup, drop by drop.
15. Hook went up the wide tree and away into the dark woods.
16. As he walked, he talked to himself.
17. "The children are on my ship, and there is poison in Pan's cup," said Hook.
18. Tinker Bell was out in the woods that night, and she heard all that Hook said.
19. She flew down the wide tree to Peter.
20. Tinker Bell flew down into the quiet home and to Peter's bed.
21. "Wake up, Peter!" she called, and Peter woke.
22. "The pirates came in the night and took Wendy and the boys to the ship!"
23. Peter got up fast from his bed and put his dagger in his belt.
24. "I will save them, but I must drink my medicine, for I promised Wendy."
25. Peter took the cup down from the shelf.
26. "Do not drink it, for Hook came down, and there is poison in your cup!"
27. "Hook cannot get down our trees," said Peter, and he did not believe her.
28. Peter put the cup to his mouth, but Tinker Bell flew in fast and drank the medicine down.
29. "That was my medicine!" said Peter.
30. But her light grew weak, and she fell down into Peter's hand.
31. "There was poison in it, Peter," she said, and her voice was slow.
32. "You drank the poison to save me, and your light must not go out!" said Peter.
33. He took her in his hands and flew up, high over the homes of the island people.
34. "I am Peter Pan, and I believe in fairies!" he called down in the night.
35. The children of the island people woke and knew that name, for he set their princess free.
36. "We believe in fairies too!" the children called back up to him from their homes.
37. In Peter's hands, Tinker Bell's light grew strong again.
38. She flew up, well again, and she and Peter flew fast to the ship.

Tick, Tick / The End of Captain Hook
1. On the deck of the pirate ship, the children were tied to the mast.
2. Hook walked up and down the deck.
3. "Join my crew and be pirates with me, or you will walk the plank tonight," said Hook.
4. "We will not be pirates," said John.
5. "The plank for all of you, then!" said Hook.
6. The boys looked down at the black water, and at Wendy.
7. "Do not be afraid of them," said Wendy to her boys.
8. From over the water came a sound: tick, tick.
9. The pirates stood still on the deck.
10. "The crocodile!" cried a pirate.
11. Hook ran from the deck and hid inside the ship.

The Window / Young Forever
1. In the morning, the children and Peter flew up together, and they flew home over the sea.
2. Peter and Tinker Bell came first, over the streets, to the tall house and the window.
3. He wanted to close the window, for then Wendy would believe her mother had forgotten her.
4. And then she would go back over the sea to Neverland, home with him.
5. But at the window, Peter looked in and saw the mother.
6. She slept in a chair by the open window, and there were tears on her face.
7. Peter looked at the tears, and he could not close the window on the mother.
8. He left the window open and flew up over the roof of the tall house.
9. Wendy, John, and Michael flew in through the open window.
10. They went to their own beds without a sound.
11. Their mother slept on in her chair, and the moon was on the sill.
12. The mother woke and saw the children, and she believed it was a dream she had dreamed many times.
13. "Mother, Mother, we are home!" called Wendy.
14. Wendy went to her mother and held her hand, and the hand was warm.
15. It was not a dream, and the mother ran to the beds and hugged all the children together.
16. The father came in, and the family was together again.
17. The children had grown a little, but the mother knew every face.
18. The lost boys stood at the window and looked in at the family.
19. "They have no mother and no father," said Wendy.
20. "We will be their mother and father," said the mother.
21. The mother and father said yes to every one of the lost boys.
22. The boys came in through the window, glad of a home at last.
23. The mother asked Peter to come in too.
24. "They will make me go to school and grow up," said Peter.
25. "No one will ever make me grow up."
26. Peter said goodbye to Wendy at the window.
27. He flew back to Neverland with Tinker Bell, and he stayed young.
28. Sometimes, at story time, a boy came and sat outside the nursery window to listen.
29. The mother saw him there and knew him now, and she did not close the window.
30. Wendy told the stories again, and the boy at the window listened.
31. The lost boys listened too, for the stories were their stories now.
32. The best stories were still about Peter Pan.
