The Frog Prince

The Gold Ball
1. A princess lived in a big castle with the king.
2. The king was her father.
3. The young princess had a little ball of gold.
4. She loved to play with her ball.
5. One day, the princess played by an old well.
6. The well was deep and full of water.
7. The princess threw her gold ball up high.
8. But the ball fell into the deep well.
9. The ball went down and did not come back.
10. The princess looked into the well.
11. She could not reach her gold ball.
12. The princess sat by the well and cried.

Paragraphs:
1-4: A princess lives in a castle with her father the king, and she loves to play with her little ball of gold.
5-8: One day she plays by an old well, throws the ball up high, and it falls into the deep water.
9-12: The ball sinks and does not come back, so the princess looks into the well and cries.

The Frog's Promise
1. Then a green frog came up out of the well.
2. "Why do you cry, little princess?" the frog asked.
3. "My gold ball fell into the well," she said.
4. "I can get your gold ball," said the frog.
5. "But you must be my friend," the frog said.
6. "Let me eat and live with you," he said.
7. "Yes, yes! I promise!" the princess said.
8. So the frog went down into the deep well.
9. The frog came back up with the gold ball.
10. The princess took her ball and ran to the castle.
11. She forgot all about the little green frog.
12. The frog called out, but the princess was gone.

Paragraphs:
1-4: A green frog comes out of the well, asks why the princess is crying, and offers to get her gold ball.
5-8: The frog asks her to be his friend; the princess promises, and the frog dives down into the well.
9-12: The frog brings the ball back, but the princess takes it and runs to the castle, forgetting the frog.

A Knock at the Door
1. The next day, the princess sat at dinner.
2. The king sat at the table too.
3. Then they heard a tap at the door.
4. The princess went to open the big door.
5. The little green frog sat on the step.
6. "You made a promise to me," the frog said.
7. "Let me come in and eat with you."
8. The princess shut the door and went back.
9. "What is wrong?" the king asked the princess.
10. So the princess told the king about the frog.
11. "I made a promise by the well," she said.
12. The king looked at the princess and the door.

Paragraphs:
1-4: The next day, while the princess and the king sit at dinner, a tap comes at the door and the princess opens it.
5-8: The frog on the step asks her to keep her promise, but the princess shuts the door and goes back.
9-12: The king asks what is wrong; the princess tells him about the frog and her promise, and the king looks at her and the door.

The King's Word
1. "A princess must keep her promise," the king said.
2. "Open the door and let the frog come in."
3. So the princess let the little frog in.
4. The frog hopped up on the table.
5. It ate from the gold plate.
6. The princess did not like it at all.
7. But she did what the king asked.
8. "I am very tired," the frog said.
9. "Take me up to your room to sleep."
10. The princess looked at the king.
11. "You must take the frog up," the king said.
12. So the princess took the frog up the stairs.

Paragraphs:
1-5: The king says a promise must be kept, so the princess lets the frog in, and it hops onto the table and eats from the gold plate.
6-12: The princess dislikes it but obeys; when the tired frog asks to go to her room, the king insists, and she carries the frog up the stairs.

A Kiss for the Frog
1. In her room, the princess put the frog down.
2. The little frog looked up at the princess.
3. "Please do not be afraid of me," it said.
4. "I only want a friend," the frog said.
5. The frog was very kind to the princess.
6. Soon the princess was not afraid.
7. She felt sorry for the little frog.
8. So the princess gave the frog a kiss.
9. Then the frog began to change.
10. The little frog became a young prince.
11. "A witch put a spell on me," the prince said.
12. "Only a kind princess could break the spell."

Paragraphs:
1-4: In her room, the little frog asks the princess not to be afraid and says it only wants a friend.
5-8: The frog is kind, so the princess stops being afraid, feels sorry for it, and gives it a kiss.
9-12: The frog changes into a young prince, who explains that a witch put a spell on him.

The Prince and the Princess
1. "You are very kind," the prince said.
2. The prince took the kind princess by the hand.
3. Then they went down to the king.
4. The king saw the prince and was very happy.
5. The prince told the king all about the witch.
6. The princess loved the young prince.
7. Soon they wanted to be together always.
8. The king made a big wedding in the castle.
9. The princess married the young prince that day.
10. All the people came to see the two.
11. The prince and princess lived in the castle.
12. And they were happy together for many years.

Paragraphs:
1-4: The prince thanks the kind princess, takes her hand, and they go down to the king, who is very happy.
5-8: The prince tells the king all about the witch; the princess loves him, and the king makes a big wedding in the castle.
9-12: The princess and the prince marry, all the people come, and they live happily together for many years.
