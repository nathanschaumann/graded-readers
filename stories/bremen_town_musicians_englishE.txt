The Bremen Town Musicians

The Old Donkey
1. A donkey carried heavy sacks for a miller.
2. The sacks were full of grain for the mill.
3. The donkey carried the grain for many years.
4. But the donkey grew old and weak.
5. His legs grew weaker every day.
6. He could no longer carry the heavy sacks.
7. The miller gave him no food now.
8. The miller planned to kill him.
9. The donkey was afraid, and he left the mill that night.
10. There was a town named Bremen.
11. He thought he could still sing well.
12. "I will go to Bremen," said the donkey.
13. "I will become a town musician there."

Paragraphs:
1-6: A donkey carries heavy sacks of grain to the mill for a miller for many years, but he grows old and weak, his legs grow weaker every day, and he can no longer carry the heavy sacks.
7-9: The miller gives him no food now and plans to kill him, so the donkey is afraid and leaves that night.
10-13: There is a town named Bremen, and the donkey thinks he can still sing well, so he decides to go there and become a town musician.

The Old Dog
1. A dog hunted with a hunter for many years.
2. Every day, the dog and the hunter ran together.
3. The dog caught many animals for him.
4. But the dog grew old and slow.
5. The old dog could not catch animals now.
6. The hunter did not want an old dog.
7. But the dog wanted to stay.
8. The hunter planned to kill him.
9. The dog was afraid, so he left that night.
10. On the road, he met the old donkey.
11. "Why are you afraid?" asked the donkey.
12. The dog told the donkey the plan.
13. "Come to Bremen," said the donkey.
14. "You can still bark loudly there."
15. The dog agreed, and they walked on together.

The Old Cat

6. She could no longer catch the mice for the woman.
7. The woman did not want the old cat now.
8. The woman planned to drown the old cat.
9. The old cat was afraid, and she left that night.
10. On the road, she met the donkey and the dog.
11. "Why are you afraid?" the donkey and the dog asked.
12. The cat told the donkey and the dog the plan.
13. "Come to Bremen with us," said the donkey.
14. "You can still sing well at night."
15. The cat agreed, and they walked on together.

The Old Rooster
1. A rooster lived on a farm for many years.
2. Every morning, he crowed loudly to wake the farm.
3. But the rooster grew old and tired.
4. He did not crow loudly now.
5. No one on the farm wanted the old rooster now.
6. The farm had guests that day.
7. The cook planned to cook the old rooster for the guests.
8. The old rooster did not want to be cooked.
9. He was afraid, so he flew from the farm that night.
10. On the road, he met the three animals.
11. "Why are you afraid?" the three animals asked.
12. The rooster told the three animals the cook's plan.
13. "Come to Bremen with us," said the donkey.
14. "You can still crow loudly there."
15. The rooster agreed, and all four walked on together.

A Light in the Dark
1. The four animals walked all day toward Bremen.
2. But night came before they reached the town.
3. They stopped in a forest.
4. The forest was dark.
5. The donkey and the dog lay down by a tree.
6. The cat climbed up into the tree.
7. The rooster flew to the top of the tree.
8. He looked all around the forest.
9. He saw a small light.
10. "I see a light in the forest," said the rooster.
11. "A house is near," said the donkey.
12. "A house may have food and be warm."
13. "We must go and see," said the dog.
14. The four animals walked toward the small light.

Paragraphs:

6-9: The cat climbs into the tree too, and the rooster flies to the top, where he looks all around and sees a small light.
10-14: The rooster announces the light, the donkey says a house is near and may have food, and the dog says they must go and see, so the four animals walk toward the small light.

The House in the Forest
1. Soon, the four animals came to a small house.
2. They saw a light in one window.
3. The four animals walked to look.
4. The donkey's front feet were on the window.
5. He looked inside the house.
6. Robbers sat around a table and ate.
7. The table was full of food.
8. The donkey came down and told the others.
9. "Robbers have food in there," he said.
10. The animals had no food all day.
11. They wanted the food and the warm house.
12. "We must make the robbers run away," said the donkey.

The Musicians' Trick
1. "I have a plan," said the donkey.
2. "We can make one loud sound together."
3. "The robbers may think a monster came."
4. All four animals went to the window.
5. The donkey's front feet were on the window again.
6. The dog climbed onto the donkey's back.
7. The cat climbed onto the dog's back.
8. The rooster flew up on top of the cat.
9. The four animals made the sound together.
10. The donkey brayed.
11. The dog barked.
12. The cat screeched.
13. The rooster crowed.
14. The robbers saw a strange shape and were afraid.
15. They ran out and into the forest.

A Feast at Last
1. The four animals came down from the window.
2. They walked to the door.
3. The donkey pushed the door open.
4. The four animals went inside.
5. The table was still full of food.
6. The four animals ate and were full.
7. The donkey went back out to the yard.
8. There, he lay down on straw.
9. The straw was warm.
10. The dog lay down behind the door.
11. The cat lay down by the warm ashes.
12. The rooster flew up onto the roof.
13. Soon, all four animals were asleep.
14. That night, they were warm, safe, and full.

1. The robbers waited in the forest all night.
2. The chief of the robbers waited too.
3. But the house was dark and quiet.

7. The house was warm now.
8. He wanted more light in the house.
9. He walked toward the warm ashes.
10. He saw small lights in the ashes.
11. He thought they were coals.
12. He reached toward the lights in the dark.
13. The cat woke and scratched his face.
14. He ran back for the door.
15. The dog woke and bit his leg.

The Robber's Story

5. "I went inside the house," said the robber.
6. "I wanted more light in the dark house."
7. "A witch sat by the warm ashes."
8. "She scratched my face with her nails!"
9. "A man stood behind the door."
10. "He cut my leg with a knife!"

Never Going Back
1. "A monster was in the yard," said the robber.
2. "The monster hit me with a club!"
3. "A judge was on the roof."
4. "He shouted for me to stop!"
5. The chief had never heard such a story.
6. The chief was afraid of the story too.
7. "We must never go back to that house," said the chief.
8. All the robbers agreed with the chief.
9. The robbers were still afraid of the witch and the monster.
10. They ran from the forest that night.

Home at Last
1. Morning came to the small house in the forest.
2. The house was quiet and warm.
3. The four animals waited for many days.
4. But the robbers did not come back.
5. "This house is home now," said the donkey.
6. "We have food, and we are safe," said the dog.
7. The cat and the rooster agreed with the others too.
8. The four animals wanted to stay in the house.
9. They never went on to Bremen.
10. They did not need Bremen now.
11. The small house in the forest was their home.
12. The four musicians sang together in the house.
13. The four animals lived there, safe and warm.
