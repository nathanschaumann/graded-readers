The Twelve Dancing Princesses

The Locked Room / The King's Proclamation / The First Prince / More Princes Fail
1. A king had 12 daughters.
2. All 12 were princesses.
3. The princesses shared one room.
4. Every night, the king locked their door himself.
5. He kept the only key.
6. Every morning, the princesses' shoes were danced to pieces.
7. No one saw the princesses leave the locked room.
8. New shoes were brought, but they were danced to pieces too.
9. This happened again and again, for many months.
10. The king asked his daughters where they went.
11. "We were asleep all night," said the eldest princess.
12. The other princesses said nothing at all.
13. The king never solved this mystery.
14. The king wanted to solve this mystery.
15. He made an offer to any man in the kingdom.
16. Any man could try to solve the mystery.
17. He would watch the princesses for 3 nights.
18. If he learned the mystery, he could marry one of the 12 princesses.
19. The winning man would also rule the kingdom one day.
20. But if he failed, he would be sent away from the kingdom forever.
21. Even so, many princes wanted to try.
22. No prince had solved the mystery before.
23. Prince after prince came to the castle to try.
24. Each prince had a room by the princesses' door.
25. He could watch the princesses' door all night.
26. Each morning, the king asked the prince what he had seen.
27. The first prince came to watch the princesses.
28. The eldest princess brought him wine in the evening.
29. The wine had a sleeping drink in it.
30. He drank all the wine and was asleep.
31. He was asleep all night and saw nothing.
32. Every morning, the princesses' shoes were danced to pieces again.
33. "What did you see?" the king asked.
34. "Nothing," said the prince.
35. The same happened on the second night.
36. The same happened on the third night.
37. The prince never saw the princesses leave or return.
38. He had failed to solve the mystery.
39. The king sent him away from the kingdom forever.
40. More princes came to try.
41. Each prince watched the princesses for 3 nights.
42. In the evening, he drank the wine.
43. Each prince was asleep all night and saw nothing at all.
44. One prince tried to stay awake.
45. But he too drank the wine and was soon asleep.
46. Prince after prince failed.
47. The king sent each one away from the kingdom forever.
48. Years passed, and still no prince solved the mystery.
49. Word of the mystery spread to other lands.
50. Fewer and fewer princes came to try.
51. At last, no more princes came at all.
52. The king began to lose all hope.

The Wounded Soldier / The Old Woman in the Woods / The First Night / Trees of Silver
1. An old soldier walked home.
2. He had been wounded.
3. The soldier had little money.
4. The soldier learned of the king's offer.
5. Any man could try to solve the mystery of the princesses.
6. If he learned it, he could marry a princess.
7. If he failed, he would be sent away from the kingdom forever.
8. He had little hope, too.
9. But he still wanted to try.
10. "I will try," said the soldier.
11. The soldier walked to the king's castle.
12. He knew the princesses' shoes were danced to pieces.
13. He walked through a forest.
14. The soldier saw an old woman.
15. "Where do you go?" the old woman asked.
16. "I go to try to solve the mystery," said the soldier.
17. "Many have failed," said the old woman.
18. "Do not drink the wine they bring you at night."
19. "Pretend to sleep."
20. She had a cloak.
21. "This cloak will make you invisible," she said.
22. "But others can still hear you," she said.
23. The soldier thanked her.
24. He walked on to the king's castle.
25. He told the king he still wanted to try.
26. The king said he could try, too.
27. The soldier was in the king's castle.
28. In the evening, the eldest princess brought him wine.
29. He knew not to drink the wine.
30. He poured the wine away, unseen.
31. He pretended to sleep.
32. He snored.
33. The eldest princess listened at his door.
34. "He sleeps," she said.
35. The 12 princesses put on dresses.
36. The eldest princess opened a trapdoor.
37. One by one, the princesses went down the stairs.
38. The soldier put on the cloak, opened the door, and followed them.
39. On the stairs, he stepped on the youngest princess's dress.
40. "Someone stepped on my dress!" she said.
41. "It was nothing," said the eldest.
42. They walked on down the stairs.
43. The stairs went down to a path.
44. The path had silver trees.
45. The soldier followed the princesses down the path.
46. He broke a branch from a tree.
47. It cracked.
48. The youngest princess was startled.
49. "Did you hear that?" she asked.
50. "That was only an owl," said the eldest.
51. The youngest princess said nothing more.
52. They walked on down the path.
53. No one in the castle knew where the princesses went at night.
54. But the soldier knew the mystery.
55. He still had 2 more nights to watch.

The Lake and the Dance / Gold and Diamond / The Soldier's Proof / The Princesses Confess / The Soldier's Choice
1. The princesses walked by trees of gold and trees of diamond.
2. At last, they came to a lake.
3. 12 boats were at the lake.
4. Each boat had a prince in it.
5. The soldier was in the youngest princess's boat.
6. "This boat feels heavy," said her prince.
7. "It was only the warm night," said the youngest princess.
8. The boats came to a castle.
9. At the castle, the princesses danced with the princes.
10. Their shoes were danced to pieces again.
11. Before morning, they returned to their room.
12. The soldier returned to his room, too.
13. The first night was over.
14. On the second night, the soldier followed the same steps.
15. The path had gold trees this time.
16. He broke a branch of gold, and it cracked.
17. The youngest princess was startled again, and grew more afraid.
18. "Sister, I heard it again," she said.
19. "That was only the wind," said the eldest.
20. They danced at the castle until their shoes were danced to pieces.
21. He hid the branch under his cloak.
22. This time, the path had diamond trees.
23. He broke a branch of diamond, and it cracked in his hand.
24. The youngest princess was startled a third time, and felt even more afraid.
25. "I know I heard something," she said.
26. "That was only a stone," said the eldest.
27. At the castle, the soldier took a cup of gold.
28. He hid the cup and the branches under his cloak.
29. In the morning, the king asked the soldier what he had seen.
30. "I did not drink the wine," said the soldier.
31. "I followed the princesses through a trapdoor."
32. "We walked down a path with trees of silver, gold, and diamond."
33. "Then we came to a lake with 12 boats."
34. "The boats went to a castle, where the princesses danced."
35. "They danced until their shoes were danced to pieces," said the soldier.
36. The soldier showed the king 3 branches.
37. "One is silver, one is gold, and one is diamond," he said.
38. He also showed the king a cup of gold.
39. The king had never seen such proof.
40. "Bring my daughters to me," said the king.
41. The 12 princesses came before the king.
42. The king looked at his 12 princesses.
43. He showed them the branches and the cup.
44. "What is this?" he asked.
45. The princesses said nothing.
46. At last, the eldest princess said, "A spell binds us."
47. "Every night, we must go to a castle," she said.
48. "We used the trapdoor," she said.
49. "We dance with 12 princes until morning," she said.
50. "We could not tell," she said.
51. The youngest princess said nothing.
52. The other princesses agreed.
53. The king remembered the locked door every night.
54. Now the king knew why.
55. The king looked at the soldier.
56. "Choose one of my daughters," said the king.
57. The soldier looked at the 12 princesses.
58. "I choose the eldest," said the soldier.
59. "I am no longer young," he said.
60. The eldest princess smiled at him.
61. That night, the spell broke.
62. That castle closed forever.
63. The 12 princes were free.
64. The soldier and the eldest princess were married.
65. All the people came to see the wedding.
66. The soldier had a home in the castle now.
67. No more shoes were danced to pieces.
