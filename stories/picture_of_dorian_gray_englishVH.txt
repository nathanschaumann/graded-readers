The Picture of Dorian Gray

The Studio
1. The studio was full of roses, and the door was open to the garden.
2. Lord Henry Wotton sat and smoked, and he looked at the picture that stood in the middle of the room.
3. It was a picture of a young man, and the young man was more beautiful than any man that Lord Henry had ever seen.
4. Basil Hallward stood in front of it and did not look at his friend.
5. "It is the best picture that you have ever painted, Basil," Lord Henry said.
6. "You must send it to the gallery this year."
7. "No, I will not send it anywhere," Basil said.
8. "I have put too much of myself into this picture."
9. Lord Henry laughed.
10. "I did not know that you were so vain, Basil," he said.
11. "You are a large plain man with dark hair, and this boy is young and beautiful."
12. "I am not vain," Basil said, "and I will tell you why I will not show the picture."
13. "Every picture that a man paints is a picture of the painter."
14. "It is not a picture of the person who sits for it."
15. "The person who sits is only the reason for the picture, and the painter puts himself on the canvas."
16. "I am afraid that men will see my secret in this picture, and so I will not show it."
17. Lord Henry laughed again and asked, "What is the secret?"
18. "Who is the young man, and why do you care so much about him?" Lord Henry asked.
19. "His name is Dorian Gray," Basil said.
20. "I have never heard that name," Lord Henry said.
21. "No one has heard it yet," Basil said, "but he is the most important thing in my life."
22. "Since I met him, I have painted better than I ever painted before."
23. "I must meet him, Basil," Lord Henry said.
24. "No, you must not," Basil said.
25. "You talk well, and you like to change the thoughts of other men, and I do not want you to change him."
26. "He is young, and I want him to stay as he is."
27. Lord Henry smiled and did not say more.

The Meeting
1. "Tell me how you met him," Lord Henry said.
2. Basil looked at the picture for a time, and then he spoke.
3. "I was at a party at the house of Lady Brandon, and I stood in the room for a time."
4. "I felt that someone was looking at me, and I turned, and I saw Dorian Gray."
5. "Dorian looked at me, and I was afraid, for I knew that he could take my whole life."
6. "I turned to go, but Lady Brandon stopped me and said that I must meet a young man."
7. "She took me to Dorian, and we spoke."
8. "I have seen him every day since, and I paint nothing but him."
9. "He is all my art now."
10. "In every picture that I paint, I see his face, and I have never seen anything so beautiful."
11. "That is why I will not show this picture, for men would see how much I love him."
12. Lord Henry looked at Basil for a time and said nothing.
13. A servant came into the studio and said, "Dorian Gray has come, sir."
14. "You must introduce me to him," Lord Henry said.
15. "I will," Basil said, "but do not spoil him, Harry, for he is the best thing in my life."
16. Basil went to the door, and Dorian came into the studio.
17. He was young and beautiful, and his face was honest.
18. "I am late, Basil," Dorian said.
19. "This is Lord Henry Wotton, and he has been looking at your picture," Basil said.
20. "You must not listen to him."
21. Dorian laughed and said, "I am not afraid of Lord Henry."
22. Lord Henry held out his hand and said, "I know your name, and I am glad to meet you."
23. "Basil has told me about you, and I have looked at your picture for a long time."
24. "I have not seen a picture so beautiful, and I have not seen a young man so beautiful."
25. Dorian looked at the picture and then at Lord Henry, and he smiled.

Youth and Beauty
1. "Harry, I want to finish this picture, and you must go," Basil said.
2. Lord Henry looked at Dorian and asked, "Do you want me to go?"
3. "No, do not go," Dorian said.
4. "Basil, I like to listen to Lord Henry, and I want him to stay."
5. Basil said nothing for a time, and then he painted again.
6. Dorian stood in front of Basil, and Lord Henry sat and looked at him.
7. "You have youth, and you have beauty, and they are the best things that a man can have," Lord Henry said.
8. "You have a beautiful face, and everyone will love you for it, but only while you are young."
9. "In a few years you will be old, and your face will not be beautiful."
10. "After that you will know that your youth is gone."
11. "Youth will not come again," Lord Henry said.
12. "So you must live now, for you are young."
13. "Beauty is better than any thought, for beauty needs no reason."
14. "Do not listen to men who tell you what to think."
15. "Live, and do what you want to do, for you are young."
16. Dorian listened, and his face was strange.
17. He had never heard these words, and he did not know what to say.
18. Basil painted on, and he did not look up.
19. "Stop, Harry," Basil said.
20. "You are confusing Dorian, and I cannot paint his face."
21. Lord Henry smiled and said, "I have said only what I think."
22. "You are young, Dorian, and you must think about what I said."
23. Dorian did not speak, and he looked at the roses in the garden.
24. "They are beautiful, and they will not last," Lord Henry said.
25. Dorian turned to him and asked, "Is it true that I will not be beautiful for long?"
26. "It is true," Lord Henry said, "and that is why you must live now."

The Wish
1. At last Basil put down his brush and said, "It is finished."
2. Lord Henry looked at the picture and said, "It is the best picture that you have ever painted."
3. "Dorian, come and look at it," Basil said.
4. Dorian came and stood in front of the picture, and he did not speak for a long time.
5. He saw his own face for the first time, and it was more beautiful than he had known.
6. Dorian's eyes were wet, and he said, "It is beautiful, but I will change, and the picture will not change."
7. "I will be old, and my face will not be beautiful, but the picture will always be young."
8. "I wish that I could always be young, and that the picture could grow old in my place."
9. "I would give my soul for that."
10. Basil looked at him and asked, "Dorian, what are you saying?"
11. "Harry has told you that you will be old, and now you are sad."
12. "I am jealous of the picture, for you like it better than you like me," Dorian said.
13. "You are jealous of a picture, and that is not right," Basil said.
14. Dorian saw a knife on the table, and he took it in his hand.
15. "I will cut the picture," Dorian said, "for I do not want it to be there when I am old."
16. "Do not do it, Dorian!" Basil cried, and he took the knife from Dorian's hand.
17. "That would be murder, for the picture is a part of my life."
18. "I like it better than any picture that I have made, and I like you better than the picture."
19. "I will give you the picture, Dorian," Basil said.
20. "It is yours, and I made it for you."
21. Lord Henry smiled and said, "It is a good thing to have a picture of yourself."
22. "Come with me, Dorian, and we will eat dinner and go to the opera."
23. Dorian looked at the picture once more and said, "I will go with you."

The Boy Without Parents
1. The next day Lord Henry went to see his uncle, Lord Fermor, who was an old man and knew everyone.
2. "Uncle George, who is Dorian Gray?" Lord Henry asked.
3. "He is the grandson of old Lord Kelso," the old man said.
4. "His mother was Margaret Devereux, and she was the most beautiful girl that I have ever seen."
5. "She ran away with a poor young soldier, and her father did not like it."
6. "The soldier was killed a year later, and Margaret died soon after."
7. "The boy grew up with his grandfather, and he has no one now."
8. "He has a great deal of money, for old Kelso left it all to him."
9. Lord Henry thanked his uncle and went out into the street.
10. He thought about Dorian, who was young and beautiful and rich and alone.
11. "It would be strange to make such a young man think as I think," he said to himself.
12. "He would do what I say, and he would not know why."
13. That night Lord Henry ate dinner at the house of his aunt Agatha, and Dorian was there.
14. Dorian spoke to everyone at the table, and everyone liked him.
15. After dinner Dorian sat by Lord Henry and said, "I have thought about what you told me, Harry."
16. "You told me to live, and I want to live, but I do not know how."
17. "I will show you," Lord Henry said.
18. "You are young, and you are beautiful, and you have money, and you can do what you want."
19. "Come with me to the theatres and the clubs, and I will teach you to see what is beautiful."
20. "You must not think about what men say, for men say what they do not think."
21. "Do you want to come?"
22. "I do," Dorian said, and he smiled.

The Actress
1. Three weeks later Dorian came to lunch at the house of Lord Henry, and his face was bright.
2. "Harry, I am in love," Dorian said.
3. "I have found a girl, and her name is Sibyl Vane."
4. "She is an actress, and she plays in a small theatre in a poor part of London."
5. "I went in one night because I saw a picture on the wall, and the theatre was dirty."
6. "The people in the theatre were rough, and the play was bad, and I wanted to go home."
7. "Then Sibyl came on, and she was the most beautiful girl that I have ever seen."
8. "She is only seventeen, and she has a small face and a great deal of brown hair."
9. "She played Juliet, and her voice was the most beautiful voice that I have ever heard."
10. "I have been to see her every night for a week, and each night she plays a different girl."
11. "One night she is Rosalind, and the next night she is Juliet, and I love them all," Dorian said.
12. "I spoke to her after the play, and I asked her to marry me, and she said yes."
13. "I love her, Harry, and I am going to marry her."
14. "You are going to marry an actress?" Lord Henry asked.
15. "You are young, and you do not know that you will be tired of her."
16. "I will never be tired of her," Dorian said.
17. "Basil and you must come to the theatre and see her," Dorian said.
18. "You will see that she is not like other girls."
19. "Come to the theatre tomorrow night, and bring Basil."
20. "I will bring him, for I want to see the girl who has done this to you," Lord Henry said.
21. Dorian laughed and thanked him, and his face was bright again.

Sibyl and Her Brother
1. That evening Sibyl Vane sat in a small room with her mother and her brother James, and her eyes were bright.
2. "I am so happy," Sibyl said, "for I love him, and he loves me."
3. "Who is he?" James asked.
4. "I do not know his name, and I call him the Prince," Sibyl said.
5. "He comes to the theatre every night, and he is young and beautiful and rich."
6. "When I am on the stage, I know that he is there, and I act better than I have ever acted."
7. James looked at Sibyl for a long time and said, "You are only seventeen, and you do not know men."
8. "You do not know his name," James said, "and I do not like it."
9. "A man who does not tell his name is not an honest man."
10. "He is a gentleman, James, and he will marry Sibyl," their mother said.
11. "We have no money, and we owe money to the man of the theatre."
12. "If Sibyl marries a rich man, we will not be poor."
13. "You think only about money, mother," Sibyl said.
14. "I do not think about money, for I love him."
15. "I am going away tomorrow, Sibyl, and I will be at sea for many years," James said.
16. "I am going to Australia, and I want you to be safe."
17. "I will be safe, James," Sibyl said, and she kissed him.
18. "Write to me from Australia."
19. Later James spoke to his mother alone.
20. "Take care of Sibyl, mother, for I do not trust this man," James said.
21. "If he does wrong to my sister, I will find him and I will kill him."
22. His mother cried out and said, "James, you speak like a bad man in a play."
23. "I speak as I feel, mother," James said, and he went out of the room.

The Bad Night
1. The next night Dorian, Basil, and Lord Henry sat in a box at the small theatre.
2. The theatre was dirty, and the people in it were rough.
3. "This is a strange place for a young man to find his love," Basil said.
4. "You will not think so when you see her," Dorian said.
5. "Sibyl Vane is the best actress that I have ever seen, and I will marry her."
6. Sibyl came on the stage as Juliet, and she was as beautiful as Dorian had said.
7. But her voice was not beautiful, and her words were bad.
8. She stood on the stage, and she did not know what to say.
9. Dorian looked at her, and his face was strange.
10. "She is beautiful, but she cannot act," Lord Henry said.
11. "She may be afraid because we are here," Basil said.
12. The people in the theatre laughed, and they called out to her.
13. A rough man cried, "Go home, girl, and say your words better!"
14. Sibyl did not hear them, and she went on.
15. Dorian could not look at her, and he did not speak.
16. "We must go, Dorian," Basil said.
17. "No, I will stay," Dorian said.
18. "I told you that she was the best actress that I had ever seen," Dorian said in a low voice.
19. "Now you think that I was wrong, and you are laughing at me."
20. "We are not laughing at you," Basil said.
21. "You are young, and you love her, and that is not a bad thing."
22. When the play was over, Lord Henry stood up and said, "She is bad, Dorian."
23. "You must not marry her, for she will never be a great actress."
24. "She is a beautiful girl, and that is all."
25. "I am sorry, Dorian," Basil said, "for I did not know that she would be so bad."
26. "Go and speak to her."
27. Dorian said nothing, and he went out of the box to find Sibyl.

Cruel Words
1. Dorian found Sibyl in her small room behind the stage, and she ran to him.
2. "Prince, I was so bad tonight," Sibyl said, and her face was bright with joy.
3. "I have never acted so badly, but I am not sorry."

7. "Before I met you, I loved the plays, and I thought that the people in them were true."
8. "Tonight I saw that the stage is only paint, and that the people on it are only actors."
9. "Now I know what love is, and I cannot act love when I have love."
10. "I was bad because I love you, and I will never act well again."
11. Dorian looked at her, and his face was hard.

13. "You have shown me that you are not the girl that I thought."

The Picture Changes
1. Dorian walked in the streets all night, and in the morning he went home.
2. In his room the picture stood on the wall, and he looked at it.
3. The face in the picture was young, but the mouth was strange.
4. The mouth was cruel now, and it had not been cruel before.
5. He told himself that the light was bad, and he looked at the picture again.
6. The change was still there.
7. He thought of the wish that he had made in the studio, and he was afraid.
8. "I wanted the picture to grow old, and I wanted to stay young," he said.
9. "Could it be true?"
10. "No, it cannot be true, and I am tired," he said to himself.
11. But the mouth in the picture was cruel, and he could not stop looking at it.
12. He thought of Sibyl, and he remembered her face when he left.
13. "I was cruel to Sibyl, and the picture shows it," Dorian said.
14. "I will go to her and ask her to forgive me, and I will marry her."
15. "If I do good, the picture will change back, and it will be beautiful again."
16. He put a large screen in front of the picture, for he did not want to see it.
17. He sat down and wrote a long letter to Sibyl, and he asked her to forgive him.
18. Later Lord Henry came into the room, and his face was strange.
19. "Dorian, I have come to tell you a sad thing," Lord Henry said.
20. "Sibyl Vane is dead."

22. Dorian cried out, and he did not speak for a long time.
23. "Did she do it because of me?" Dorian asked.
24. "I do not know," Lord Henry said, "but she was alone after you left."

The Locked Room
1. That day Basil came to the house, and Dorian met him in the hall.
2. "I am sorry about Sibyl, Dorian," Basil said.
3. "I have come to ask you for the picture."
4. "I want to send it to the gallery this year."
5. "No, you must not send it," Dorian said, and his face grew cold.
6. "You gave it to me, and I will not show it to anyone."
7. "Why will you not show it?" Basil asked.

The Room Again
1. Dorian walked home through the empty streets, and he thought only of the picture.
2. He went up to the room at the top of the house and opened the door with his key.
3. He put the lamp on the table and took the cloth from the picture.
4. The face in the picture was not better, and the mouth had a smile on it that was false.
5. There was red on the hands in the picture, and the red was wet.
6. "I did one good thing, and the picture is worse," Dorian said.
7. He thought of Hetty Merton and the village, and he thought about why he had left the girl alone.
8. He had not left the girl alone because he was a good man.
9. He had done it because he wanted to look at the picture and see that it was beautiful again.
10. "It was only vanity, and I was playing at a good man," he said.
11. He thought of Basil, and he thought of the red on the hands, and he thought of telling the police what he had done.
12. "No one could prove it, for the body is gone and Alan Campbell is dead," he said.
13. "Only the picture speaks against me, and no one but me has seen it."
14. "As long as the picture is in this room, I will not be free."
15. He looked around the room and saw the knife on the table.
16. He had used it to kill Basil, and he had cleaned it many times until nothing was left on it.
17. He took the knife in his hand and stood in front of the picture.
18. "It has killed the painter, and it will kill the painter's work," Dorian said.
19. "It will kill the past, and when the past is dead, I will be free."

The End
1. Dorian went to the picture and pushed the knife into it from the top to the bottom.
2. There was a cry, and there was the sound of a man falling to the floor.
3. The cry was so terrible that the servants woke in their beds.
4. Two men who were walking in the street below stopped and looked up at the house.
5. They went to the door and knocked, and Francis came down and opened it.
6. "Someone cried out at the top of this house," one of the men said.
7. "I heard it, and I did not know what it was," Francis said.
8. Francis took a lamp, and the two men followed him up the stairs.
9. They knocked at the door of the locked room, but no one answered.
10. Francis had not seen that room for many years, for his master kept the key.
11. The door would not open, and they pushed it until it broke.
12. They stood at the door for a moment before they went into the room.
13. The lamp on the table was still burning, and the room was cold.
14. On the wall of the room hung a picture of a young man.
15. It was the picture that Basil Hallward had painted, and it was as beautiful as on the day that it was finished.
16. On the floor lay a dead man in evening clothes, and there was a knife in his heart.
17. The man was old, and his face was full of lines, and it was terrible to look at.
18. "Who is he?" one of the men asked.
19. "I do not know him, for no one in this house is so old," Francis said.
20. But when they looked at his hands, they saw the rings on his fingers.
21. By the rings they knew that the dead man was Dorian Gray.
