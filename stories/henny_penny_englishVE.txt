Henny Penny

The Acorn Falls
1. Henny Penny lived on a farm.
2. One day, she stood under a tree.
3. An acorn fell from the tree.
4. It hit her on the head.
5. She did not see it fall.
6. "Oh no!" cried Henny Penny.
7. "The sky is falling!" she said.
8. "I must tell the king!"
9. Henny Penny ran off the farm.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Henny Penny lives on a farm, and one day while she stands under a tree, an acorn falls and hits her on the head, though she does not see it fall.
6-9: She cries out that the sky is falling, says she must tell the king, and runs off the farm.

Cocky Locky Joins
1. Henny Penny walked down a road.
2. Soon she saw a rooster.
3. His name was Cocky Locky.
4. "Where are you going?" asked Cocky Locky.
5. "The sky is falling!" said Henny Penny.
6. "I must tell the king," she said.
7. "I will come with you," said Cocky Locky.
8. Henny Penny and Cocky Locky walked on together.
9. They wanted to find the king.

Paragraphs:
1-4: Henny Penny walks down a road and meets a rooster named Cocky Locky, who asks where she is going.
5-9: She tells him the sky is falling and she must tell the king, so Cocky Locky joins her and they walk on together to find him.

Ducky Lucky and Goosey Loosey Join
1. Soon they came to a small pond.
2. A duck named Ducky Lucky stood by the water.
3. "The sky is falling!" said Henny Penny.
4. "We must tell the king," Cocky Locky told Ducky Lucky.
5. "I will come with you," said Ducky Lucky.
6. The three birds walked down the road.
7. They came to a green field next.
8. A goose named Goosey Loosey looked up.
9. "The sky is falling!" Ducky Lucky told Goosey Loosey.
10. "We must tell the king," said Henny Penny.
11. "I will come too," cried Goosey Loosey.
12. Four birds now walked on together.

Paragraphs:
1-6: They reach a small pond, where a duck named Ducky Lucky hears that the sky is falling and joins them on the road.
7-12: The three birds reach a green field, where a goose named Goosey Loosey hears the same news and joins the group.

Turkey Lurkey Joins
1. The four birds came to a barn.
2. A big bird named Turkey Lurkey was there.
3. "Where are you going?" asked Turkey Lurkey.
4. "The sky is falling!" said Henny Penny.
5. "We must tell the king," said Cocky Locky.
6. "I will come too," said Turkey Lurkey.
7. The birds walked on together.
8. They wanted to find the king.
9. The road led through the woods.

Paragraphs:
1-5: The four birds come to a barn, where a big bird named Turkey Lurkey asks where they are going.
6-9: They tell him the sky is falling and they must tell the king, so Turkey Lurkey joins them, and the road leads them on through the woods.

Foxy Loxy and the Short Way
1. The five birds walked into the woods.
2. Foxy Loxy saw them from the woods.
3. He came out to them.
4. "Where are you going?" asked Foxy Loxy.
5. "We must tell the king," said Cocky Locky.
6. "The sky is falling!" said Turkey Lurkey.
7. "I know a short way," said Foxy Loxy.
8. "Follow me through the woods," he said.
9. But the short way led to his den.
10. The birds did not know this.
11. They followed Foxy Loxy off the road.
12. Foxy Loxy led them deep into the woods.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The five birds walk into the woods, where Foxy Loxy sees them and comes out to ask where they are going, and they tell him they must reach the king.
7-12: Foxy Loxy says he knows a short way and leads them off the road, though the birds do not know his short way goes to his den.

Into the Den
1. They came to a big den.
2. "This den is the short way," said Foxy Loxy.
3. "It leads to the king's home."
4. "Only one bird can go at a time."
5. "Go in, and I will follow," said Foxy Loxy.
6. Cocky Locky went in first.
7. He did not come out.
8. Ducky Lucky went in next.
9. She did not come out.
10. Goosey Loosey went in too.
11. She did not come out at all.
12. Only Turkey Lurkey and Henny Penny stood outside.

Paragraphs:
1-5: They find a big den, and Foxy Loxy says it leads to the king's home, though only one bird can go in at a time.
6-12: Cocky Locky, Ducky Lucky, and Goosey Loosey each go into the den one after another, and none of them comes back out, leaving only Turkey Lurkey and Henny Penny outside.

Henny Penny Runs Home
1. Turkey Lurkey went into the den next.
2. He never came out of it.
3. Henny Penny stood outside.
4. She heard a noise from inside.
5. The noise scared Henny Penny.
6. She did not go inside.
7. Henny Penny ran all the way home.
8. She never found the king.
9. She never told him about the sky.
10. But Henny Penny was safe at home.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Turkey Lurkey goes into the den next and never comes out, leaving Henny Penny alone outside, and a noise from inside scares her.
6-10: She does not go inside, and instead runs all the way home, and though she never finds the king or tells him about the sky, she is safe at home.
