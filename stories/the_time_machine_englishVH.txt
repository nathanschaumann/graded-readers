The Time Machine

The Fourth Dimension
1. I will call him the Time Traveller.
2. He lived in a big house in Richmond, and every Thursday he asked his friends to dinner.
3. One Thursday I came to his house, and I sat down to dinner with Filby, the Psychologist, the Medical Man, and the Editor.
4. After dinner we went into the smoking room, and the Time Traveller sat by the fire.
5. "Every thing is long and wide and high," said the Time Traveller, "and that is 3 dimensions."
6. "But every thing must last for some time, and time is a dimension too."
7. "It is the fourth dimension."
8. "We can go up and down, and we can go back and forth, and we can go left and right."
9. "Why can we not go back and forth in time?"
10. "You cannot go back to a day that is gone," said the Psychologist.
11. "A man can go up, and he can come down again," said the Time Traveller.
12. "I say that a man can go back in time, and he can go forward in time, if he has a machine."
13. "That is not possible," said Filby.
14. The Time Traveller went out, and he came back with a small machine.
15. The machine had a little lever on it.
16. He put the machine on the table by the fire, and every one looked at it.
17. "It is not a trick," said the Time Traveller.
18. "I will push this lever, and the machine will go into the time that is coming."
19. He put out his hand and pushed the lever, and the machine was gone.
20. The table was empty, and the Psychologist looked under it.
21. "Where has it gone?" said the Editor.
22. "It has gone into the future," said the Time Traveller, "or into the past, for I do not know which."
23. "It is not here now," said the Medical Man.
24. "Do you believe me?" said the Time Traveller.
25. "No," said Filby.
26. "I have a big machine in my room, and I will go in it myself," said the Time Traveller.
27. He took us to the room, and the big machine was there, and it was not done.
28. It had bars of metal and bars of ivory, and it had a seat and 2 levers.
29. "Come next Thursday, and I will tell you what I saw," said the Time Traveller.
30. The Editor laughed, and the Medical Man did not speak.
31. Filby looked at the machine once more, and then he went home.
32. I went home, and I did not know what to think.

The Time Traveller Comes Back
1. The next Thursday I came to the house at 7, and the Time Traveller was not there.
2. The Psychologist, the Medical Man, the Editor, and Filby were in the room.
3. "Where is he?" said the Editor.
4. A paper was on the table, and it said, "Begin dinner, and I will come."
5. We sat down to dinner.
6. The door opened, and the Time Traveller came in.
7. His coat was dirty, his hair was white, and there was a cut on his face.
8. He walked with a limp.
9. No one spoke, and we all looked at him.
10. "Are you hurt?" said the Medical Man.
11. "I am not hurt," said the Time Traveller.
12. "I want food and drink."
13. He drank a glass of wine, and he went out of the room.
14. When he came back, he sat down and ate.
15. "Where have you been?" said the Editor.
16. "Let me eat," said the Time Traveller, "and then I will tell you a story."
17. "You will not believe it."
18. After dinner we went into the smoking room, and the Time Traveller sat by the fire.
19. "I have been in the time that is coming, and I have come back," said the Time Traveller.
20. "The machine in my room is a real machine."
21. "I will tell you the story, and you will say nothing."
22. "Do you promise?"
23. "We promise," said the Psychologist, and the Editor laughed.
24. This is the story that the Time Traveller told us, and I will tell it as he told it.
25. The Time Traveller sat on the big machine that day.
26. He had one hand on the lever that sent the machine forward, and he had one hand on the lever that stopped it.
27. He pushed the lever, and the room went dark, and then it was light, and then it was dark again.
28. Day and night came again and again, and each was too short to see.
29. The sun went by in a minute, and each minute was one day.
30. The houses fell down, and new houses came, and trees came and went.
31. The Time Traveller was afraid that the machine would hit a house, so he pushed the lever more.
32. He could not see day and night now, and the sun was a light in the sky.
33. The Time Traveller wanted to stop, and he pushed the other lever.
34. The machine turned over, and he fell down.
35. Hail fell on him, and he got up.
36. He looked, and he saw a big sphinx of white stone, and it had wings.
37. The hail stopped, and the sun came out.
38. He took the levers off the machine and put them in his pocket, so that no one could use it.
39. The Time Traveller saw many big houses, and he did not see one man.
40. He was afraid of the time he had come to, for he did not know how far he had gone.
41. "I have come to a time when men are not the men I know," he said.

The Little People
1. Some people came up the hill toward the Time Traveller, and they were small.
2. The biggest was not as tall as a man, and each had a small coat and long hair.
3. The people were beautiful, and they were not afraid, and they came near and looked at him.
4. "Good day," said the Time Traveller.
5. The people laughed, and they said some words, and he did not know the words.
6. One of them pointed at the sky and made a noise of thunder, and the Time Traveller shook his head.
7. The Time Traveller put his hand on his chest and said, "I am the Time Traveller."
8. One of the people touched his hand, and the rest laughed.
9. They gave him flowers, and they took him by the hand, and he went with them down the hill.
10. They came to a big house, and it was old, and the little people went in.
11. The big house was a hall, and it was old, and there were tables in it.
12. The little people sat on the floor, and they gave the Time Traveller fruit.
13. The fruit was sweet, and he ate it, for he was hungry.
14. The little people ate fruit too, and they did not eat meat.
15. The Time Traveller pointed at a thing, and the little people said its name, and he said it after them.
16. In a day he knew many words, and the people laughed at his words.
17. They were tired after a few words, and they went away to play.
18. They did not like to work, and they did not like to think.
19. All the people were small and beautiful, and they all had the same coat.
20. There was no old man and no old woman, and there was no work.
21. There were no fields, and there were no fences.
22. The houses were big and old, and some had fallen down.
23. The Time Traveller sat alone on the hill and looked at the sun.
24. "Men no longer need to work, for they have all that they want," he said to himself.
25. "There is no war, and there is no money, and there is no need to be strong."
26. "When men have no need to work, they do not think."
27. "Men have been small and weak for a long time, and they are the little people I see."
28. "This is the sunset of mankind."

26. He was not alone now, for Weena was kind.
