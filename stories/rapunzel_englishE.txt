Rapunzel

A House by the Wall / Caught in the Garden / The Witch's Bargain / A Promise Kept
1. A man and his wife lived in a small house.
2. Behind the house stood a high wall.
3. A witch owned a garden behind the wall.
4. Green lettuce grew in rows inside the garden.
5. The wife was soon to have a baby.
6. Every day, she wanted to eat the green lettuce.
7. "I must have that lettuce," she told her husband.
8. "The witch owns that garden," said the husband.
9. "Give me some, or I will be sick," she said.
10. That night, he decided to climb over the high wall.
11. That night, the man climbed into the garden.
12. The moon was out, and the garden was still.
13. He found the green lettuce.
14. He picked some and climbed back over the wall.
15. He gave the lettuce to his wife.
16. The wife ate the green lettuce at once.
17. Soon, she wanted more lettuce and looked at her husband.
18. "I must have more," she told her husband.
19. The husband did not want to climb the wall twice.
20. But he agreed and gave her a smile.
21. That night, he climbed over the wall once more.
22. He went to the same row of lettuce.
23. He did not know the witch was watching.
24. The witch stepped from behind a tree.
25. "You steal my lettuce," the witch said.
26. The man dropped the lettuce and stepped back.
27. "Forgive me," the man said.
28. "My wife needs it, or she will be sick," he said.
29. The witch looked at the man for a long time.
30. "You may take the lettuce you want," said the witch.
31. The man smiled and thanked the witch.
32. "But you must give me the baby," said the witch.
33. The man's smile disappeared at once.
34. "I have no choice," the man said.
35. "I agree," he said, and the bargain was made.
36. Months passed, and the baby was born.
37. The man and his wife had a baby girl.
38. Soon the witch was at their door.
39. "You must keep the bargain," said the witch.
40. The man and his wife cried, but they kept their bargain.
41. The witch took the baby girl.
42. "I will call her Rapunzel," said the witch.
43. The witch carried Rapunzel into a forest.
44. A high tower stood in the forest.
45. The witch kept Rapunzel inside the tower.
46. Rapunzel never knew her parents.
47. The husband and his wife never saw their daughter.

Paragraphs:
1-4: A man and his wife live behind a high wall, beyond which a witch's garden grows lettuce in rows.
5-10: Soon to have a baby, the wife craves the lettuce daily and tells her husband she will be sick without it, so that night he decides to climb the wall.
11-15: He climbs into the moonlit garden, finds the lettuce, picks some, and climbs back to give it to his wife.
16-18: She eats it at once, but soon wants more and tells her husband so.
19-23: Reluctant but smiling, he agrees, climbs the wall again that night, and returns to the same row, not knowing the witch watches.
24-29: The witch steps out and accuses him of stealing; he drops the lettuce, begs forgiveness, and explains his wife needs it, and she studies him a long while.
30-35: The witch allows the lettuce but demands the baby in return; the man's smile fades, but seeing no choice, he agrees, and the bargain is made.
36-39: Months pass, a baby girl is born, and the witch soon comes to demand the bargain be kept.
40-42: Weeping, they keep the bargain, and the witch takes the girl, naming her Rapunzel.
43-47: The witch carries Rapunzel to a forest tower and keeps her there, cut off from her parents forever.

Alone in the Tower / A Prince in the Forest / Watching the Witch / The Prince Calls
1. Twelve years passed in the tower.
2. The tower had no door and no stairs.
3. Rapunzel never went into the forest.
4. Rapunzel's hair grew long, down to the ground.
5. Rapunzel sang songs at a window.
6. Every day, the witch came to see Rapunzel.
7. The witch came to the tower and called.
8. "Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair," she called.
9. Rapunzel wound her long hair on a hook.
10. She let her hair fall from the window.
11. The witch climbed up the hair to the window.
12. A prince came to the forest one day.
13. He heard someone sing.
14. The prince followed the sound to a tower.
15. The tower stood high in the green forest.
16. He saw a small window high on the tower.
17. No hair hung from the window.
18. He looked for a door, but the tower had no door.
19. The prince tried to climb the tower wall.
20. But he could not climb the high wall.
21. He called, but no one answered.
22. The prince wanted to know her name.
23. He left the forest, but he did not forget the tower.
24. The prince came back to the forest.
25. He hid behind a tall tree.
26. Soon, the witch came to the tower and stood below the window.
27. "Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair," called the witch.
28. Long hair fell from the window.
29. The witch climbed up the hair.
30. The prince was watching from behind the tree.
31. He learned the witch's secret call.
32. He heard no other sound in the forest.
33. He saw the witch climb down, and soon she went away.
34. The prince came out from behind the tree.
35. He wanted to see her and knew he must come back.
36. He knew what to say now.
37. Soon, the prince came to the tower.
38. He stood below the window and called.
39. "Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair," he called.
40. Rapunzel heard a man's voice, not the witch's.
41. She was afraid and did not answer.
42. "Do not be afraid," the prince said.
43. "I heard you sing, and I came to see you," he said.
44. Rapunzel wound her hair around the hook.
45. The prince climbed up the hair to the window.
46. He saw Rapunzel for the first time.
47. "Who are you?" Rapunzel asked.
48. "I am a prince," he said.
49. Rapunzel had never seen a prince before.

Paragraphs:
1-4: Twelve years pass in the doorless tower; Rapunzel never leaves, and her hair grows down to the ground.
5: She sings there.
6-11: Every day the witch calls at the tower for Rapunzel to let down her hair, which she winds on a hook and drops, letting the witch climb up.
12-15: A prince hears singing in the forest and follows the sound to a tower.
16-18: He sees the small high window, bare of hair, and finds no door at all.
19-23: The prince cannot climb the high wall, and his calls go unanswered; wanting to know her name, he leaves but does not forget the tower.
24-26: He returns and hides behind a tree as the witch arrives at the tower.
27-32: The witch calls her secret call, hair falls, and she climbs up while the hidden prince watches and learns the call.
33-36: After the witch climbs down and leaves, the prince comes out, wanting to see Rapunzel and knowing now what to say.
37-41: The prince comes and calls the same words below the window, but Rapunzel, hearing a man's voice, is afraid and stays silent.
42-45: He tells her not to be afraid, so she winds her hair on the hook, and he climbs up to the window.
46-49: He sees Rapunzel at last; she asks who he is, and he says a prince.

A Secret Friendship / The Slip / Cast Into the Wilderness / The Witch's Trap / Falling and Blinded
1. Every day, the prince came to see Rapunzel.
2. They talked at the window every day.
3. Rapunzel told the prince about her life in the tower.
4. The prince told Rapunzel about his kingdom.
5. Rapunzel and the prince loved each other.
6. The prince wanted to take Rapunzel away.
7. "You must leave the tower," the prince said.
8. "But I could not climb down my own hair," Rapunzel said.
9. "Bring silk every time you come," she said.
10. "I will make a ladder from the silk."
11. "Then I will climb down to you."
12. The prince agreed to bring silk every day.
13. Every day, Rapunzel began to make the ladder.
14. One day, the witch came to the tower.
15. "Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair," she called.
16. Rapunzel let down her hair again.
17. Rapunzel was quiet and did not look at the witch.
18. "You climb slower than the prince does," Rapunzel said.
19. The witch stopped and looked at Rapunzel.
20. "What prince?" the witch asked.
21. Rapunzel knew she had said too much.
22. "Tell me the truth," the witch said.
23. Rapunzel could not keep the secret.
24. "A prince comes to see me every day," Rapunzel said.
25. The witch said nothing more that day.
26. The witch came back and held scissors.
27. "You will not see the prince again," said the witch.
28. The witch cut Rapunzel's long hair.
29. Rapunzel's hair fell to the ground.
30. The witch took Rapunzel away from the tower.
31. She left Rapunzel in a dry wilderness.
32. Rapunzel had no food and no water.
33. She cried in the wilderness.
34. Rapunzel looked around.
35. She did not know what to do.
36. The witch went back to the tower.
37. She tied Rapunzel's cut hair to the hook.
38. The witch waited for the prince to come.
39. Soon, the prince came to the tower.
40. "Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair," he called.
41. The cut hair fell from the window.
42. He did not see Rapunzel, but he saw the witch instead.
43. He took a step back.
44. No one else was in the tower.
45. "Where is Rapunzel?" the prince asked.
46. "You will never see her again," said the witch.
47. The prince heard the witch's words and stopped.
48. He could not believe Rapunzel was gone.
49. The prince stepped back to the open window.
50. He did not see thorns below.
51. The prince fell from the high window.
52. He fell into the thorns below the tower.
53. The thorns went into his eyes.
54. The prince could not see at all.
55. The witch was watching and said nothing.
56. No one came to find the prince that day.
57. He stayed there and could not stand.
58. Time passed in the quiet forest around him.
59. The prince did not move from the thorns.

Paragraphs:
1-4: Every day the prince visits, and they talk of her tower life and his kingdom.
5-9: In love, the prince wants to take her away, but she cannot climb down her own hair, so she asks him to bring silk each visit.
10-13: She plans a silk ladder, and the prince agrees to bring silk daily as she begins weaving it.
14-17: The witch comes and calls as always, and Rapunzel lets down her hair but stays quiet, avoiding her eyes.
18-21: Rapunzel slips and says the witch climbs slower than the prince, and the witch, alarmed, asks what prince.
22-25: Pressed for the truth, Rapunzel admits a prince visits daily, and the witch says nothing more that day.
26-29: The witch returns with scissors, vows Rapunzel will never see the prince, and cuts off her long hair.
30-35: The witch takes Rapunzel from the tower and abandons her, with no food or water, in a dry wilderness, where she cries, lost.
36-40: The witch ties the cut hair to the hook and waits, and soon the prince arrives and calls for the hair as usual.
41-44: The cut hair falls, and instead of Rapunzel he sees the witch and steps back.
45-46: He asks for Rapunzel; the witch says never again.
47-51: Stunned and disbelieving, the prince steps back to the open window, does not see the thorns below, and falls from it.
52-54: He falls into the thorns, which pierce his eyes and blind him.
55-59: The witch watches silently, no one comes, and the prince, unable to stand, stays in the thorns as time passes.

Blind and Alone / A Voice He Knew / Home to the Kingdom
1. Days passed, and the prince was still blind.
2. He left the thorns and walked into the forest.
3. The prince could not see where he walked.
4. He walked with his hands before him.
5. Years passed, and the prince wandered on.
6. He did not know the road home to his kingdom.
7. The prince lived on what he could find.
8. He never forgot Rapunzel.
9. The prince was tired, but he did not stop.
10. He wanted only to find Rapunzel again.
11. The prince wandered into the dry wilderness.
12. He heard a voice he knew.
13. It was Rapunzel's voice.
14. The prince walked to the voice.
15. Rapunzel saw the prince and knew him at once.
16. She ran to him and cried.
17. Rapunzel held the prince and did not let go.
18. Her tears fell on the prince's eyes.
19. His eyes began to heal.
20. The prince could see again.
21. He looked at Rapunzel with his own eyes.
22. "You found me," Rapunzel said.
23. "I will never leave you again," the prince said.
24. Rapunzel and the prince left the wilderness together.
25. The prince told Rapunzel he loved her.
26. Rapunzel told him she loved him too.
27. They went to the prince's kingdom.
28. The people of the kingdom welcomed the prince home.
29. Soon, the prince married Rapunzel.
30. Rapunzel became a princess.
31. Rapunzel's hair began to grow long again.
32. They never forgot the tower or the wilderness.
33. Rapunzel sang songs in the kingdom every day.

Paragraphs:
1-4: Days pass; still blind, the prince leaves the thorns for the forest, walking blindly with hands outstretched.
5-8: Years pass as he wanders, lost, living on what he finds, never forgetting Rapunzel.
9-10: Tired, he does not stop, wanting only to find her.
11-14: In the wilderness he hears a familiar voice, Rapunzel's, and walks toward it.
15-18: Rapunzel recognizes him at once, runs to him crying, and holds him as her tears fall on his eyes.
19-23: His eyes heal; seeing her at last, she says he found her, and he vows never to leave again.
24-27: Together they leave the wilderness, exchange love, and go to the prince's kingdom.
28-31: Welcomed home, the prince marries Rapunzel, now a princess whose hair grows long again.
32-33: They remember it all; Rapunzel sings daily in the kingdom.
