The Princess and the Pea

The Princess in the Storm
1. A prince wanted to marry a real princess.
2. He looked in every castle for a princess.
3. But he never found anyone.
4. One night, a storm came to the castle.
5. Someone knocked at the castle gate in the rain.
6. A girl stood outside in the storm.
7. "I am a real princess," the girl said.
8. The queen put a pea under twenty beds.
9. That night, the girl lay on all the beds.
10. She felt a hard pea under her all night.
11. "I did not sleep at all," the princess said.
12. So the prince married the real princess the next day.

Paragraphs:
1-6: A prince searches every castle but never finds anyone to marry; one stormy night a girl knocks at the castle gate and stands out in the rain.
7-12: The girl says she is a real princess, so the queen hides a pea under twenty beds; the girl feels it all night and cannot sleep, which proves she is real, and the prince marries her.
