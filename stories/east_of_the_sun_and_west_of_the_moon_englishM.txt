East of the Sun and West of the Moon

A Poor Family / The White Bear at the Door / Her Choice
1. A poor family lived in a small house.
2. The father and mother had little money and little food.
3. They had three daughters, and the youngest was the kindest.
4. She helped her mother with the work every day.
5. Soon, the little food they had was almost gone.
6. "We have so little food," the mother said one night.
7. "We will manage," the father said.
8. The youngest daughter said little, but she was afraid.
9. She wished to help her poor family.
10. That night, a storm came to the house.
11. Wind beat against the small house.
12. Snow fell, and the wind was cold.
13. The three daughters sat by the fire.
14. Someone knocked at the door.
15. The knock came again.
16. The father stood and walked to the door.
17. The father opened the door.
18. A white bear stood in the storm.
19. The father stepped back.
20. "Do not be afraid," the white bear said.
21. "I will make your family rich," the bear said.
22. "Give me your youngest daughter," the bear said.
23. The father did not want to lose his daughter.
24. The father called the mother and the three daughters to the door.
25. He told them what the white bear wanted.
26. "Where would she live?" the mother asked the bear.
27. "In a castle, far from here," said the bear.
28. "She will have all she needs there," the bear said.
29. The three daughters looked at the white bear.
30. "What do you say?" the bear asked the youngest daughter.
31. The youngest daughter looked at her mother and father.
32. She thought of the little money and the little food.
33. If she went with the bear, her family would be rich.
34. But she did not know the white bear at all.
35. She was afraid to leave her home.
36. She decided to go with the bear.
37. "I will go with you," she said to the bear.
38. Her mother cried.
39. The father and mother said goodbye to their daughter.
40. The other daughters said goodbye to her too.
41. The white bear told her to climb onto his back.
42. She held onto the bear, and he ran into the storm.
43. He ran through the night.

Paragraphs:
1-4: A poor family of five lives in a small house with little money, and of the three daughters, the youngest is the kindest and helps her mother with the work every day.
5-9: One winter the family's food is almost gone, and though the mother and father worry aloud, the youngest daughter stays quiet, only wishing she could help.
10-16: A storm comes that night, wind beating against the house as snow falls and the cold deepens, and the father is afraid of the storm; as the three daughters sit by the fire, a knock sounds at the door, then again, so the father goes to answer it.
17-21: The father opens the door to find a white bear in the storm, and though he steps back afraid, the bear tells him not to fear and promises to make his family rich.
22-25: The bear asks for the youngest daughter in return, and though the father does not want to lose her, the family has little money and little food, so he calls the mother and the three daughters to the door and tells them what the bear wants.
26-30: The mother, afraid for her daughter, asks where she would live, and the bear answers: a castle far away, where she would have all she needs; then the bear turns to the youngest daughter and asks what she says.
31-36: The youngest daughter weighs her family's poverty against her fear of the unknown white bear, and after she thinks and thinks, she decides to go with him.
37-40: She tells the bear she will go, and her mother cries as the whole family says goodbye to her.
41-43: The white bear has her climb onto his back, and holding on, she rides as he runs into the storm and through the night, until her home is far away.

The Mountain Castle / The One Rule / The Broken Rule
1. The bear and the youngest daughter came to a mountain.
2. A castle stood in the mountain.
3. The castle had rooms of gold.
4. The bear gave the youngest daughter a room.
5. She had never seen so much gold before.
6. But she still missed her family.
7. One night, she went to bed, and the room grew dark.
8. Someone came in and lay down beside her.
9. She did not see his face, and she did not know who he was.
10. Before light came, he was gone.
11. Night after night, someone came to her in the dark.
12. She wanted to light a candle and look at him.
13. But she was too afraid.
14. One day, the youngest daughter came to the bear.
15. "May I visit my mother and father?" she asked.
16. The white bear thought about this.
17. "You may go and see them," the bear said.
18. "But there is one rule," said the bear.
19. "Never talk with your mother alone," the bear said.
20. "Why not?" the youngest daughter asked.
21. "If you do, trouble will come to us both," said the bear.
22. She did not know why, but she agreed to the rule.
23. "I will not talk with my mother alone," she said.
24. The white bear ran with her back to her home.
25. Her mother held her and looked at her rich clothes.
26. "You look rich," her mother said.
27. The youngest daughter told her family about the castle and the gold.
28. But she did not tell them about the man in the dark.
29. The mother asked to speak with her daughter alone.
30. The youngest daughter thought of the bear's rule.
31. "I will not talk with you alone," she said.
32. "Why not?" her mother asked.
33. Her mother was afraid for her.
34. "That man may be a troll," her mother said.
35. But she was afraid for her mother too.
36. She agreed to talk with her mother alone.
37. She told her mother about the man in the dark room.
38. "I never see his face," she said.
39. Her mother gave her a candle.
40. "Light this at night, and look at his face," her mother said.
41. The youngest daughter took the candle.
42. Soon the white bear came to take her back to the castle far away.
43. She hid the candle in her clothes.

The Candle in the Night / The Prince's Grief / East of the Sun and West of the Moon / The Three Old Women
1. That night, someone came to the dark room.
2. Soon she knew he was asleep.
3. She took the candle and lit the room.
4. In the light, she saw a young man.
5. She kissed him.
6. Three drops of hot wax fell on his shirt.
7. The young man woke.
8. He looked at the candle she held.
9. He looked at the wax on his shirt.
10. "What have you done?" he said.
11. "I wanted to see your face," she said.
12. The young man looked sad.
13. "You should not have done this," he said.
14. "I am a prince," the young man said.
15. "A troll's curse fell on me," he said.
16. The youngest daughter was afraid.
17. "You have lived here almost one year," he said.
18. "A little more, and the curse would have ended."
19. "But now you have seen my face."
20. He looked at the wax on his shirt.
21. The prince looked sad.
22. The youngest daughter did not know what to say.
23. "I did not want this," she said.
24. "Now I must go," the prince said.
25. "Go where?" she asked.
26. The prince did not speak.
27. The prince spoke.
28. "I must go to the castle east of the sun and west of the moon."
29. "There, I must marry the troll princess," he said.
30. "I have seen her long nose," he said.
31. The youngest daughter did not want him to go.
32. "Take me with you," she said.
33. "No one finds that castle," the prince said.
34. "I do not know the way either," he said.
35. The youngest daughter was afraid for him.
36. Soon the castle shook.
37. The prince and the castle both disappeared.
38. The youngest daughter stood alone in a forest.
39. She decided to find the prince.
40. The youngest daughter walked through the forest for many days.
41. Soon, she came to a small house.
42. An old woman sat by the house.
43. "I look for a prince, east of the sun and west of the moon."
44. "I do not know that castle," the old woman said.
45. "But take this golden apple," she said.
46. "Take my horse, and ride to my sister," said the old woman.
47. "She may know the way."
48. The youngest daughter thanked her and rode on.
49. She came to the next old woman and asked the same question.
50. This old woman did not know the way either.
51. But she gave a golden comb, lent a horse, and sent her on her way.
52. The youngest daughter thanked her and rode to a third old woman.
53. She asked the same question again.
54. "But take this golden wheel," she said.
55. "Now go and ask the four winds, for they may know the way."

The Winds' Relay / The North Wind's Crossing
1. The youngest daughter came to the East Wind.
2. He was cold, and he blew against her face.
3. "I look for a prince, east of the sun and west of the moon."
4. "I have blown over many lands, but never so far," the East Wind said.
5. "My brother, the West Wind, is stronger, and he may know," he said.
6. He lifted her into the sky and blew her over green hills.
7. He set her down before his brother.
8. The West Wind was larger, and his voice was loud.
9. "I look for a prince, east of the sun and west of the moon."
10. "I have never blown so far," said the West Wind.
11. "But my brother, the South Wind, may know the way," he said.
12. He lifted her into the sky and blew her over dark mountains.
13. The youngest daughter held on and did not look down.
14. The South Wind stood warm and strong beside the sea, which stretched far below.
15. "I look for a prince, east of the sun and west of the moon."
16. "I have crossed wide seas, but never that far," he said.
17. "Only my brother, the North Wind, may know the way," he said.
18. The youngest daughter came to the North Wind.
19. He was the oldest and strongest of the four winds.
20. "I look for a prince, east of the sun and west of the moon."
21. "I went there once," the North Wind said.
22. "I blew an aspen leaf there once."
23. "I was tired for days after," he said.
24. "The way is far, over a wide sea," he said.
25. "I will try to blow you there," said the North Wind.
26. He lifted her into the sky and blew to the sea.
27. He blew all day and all night over the wide sea.
28. "I am almost too tired to go on," said the North Wind.
29. But he blew on, over the last of the sea.
30. At last, he saw land.
31. With his last strength, he set her down on the shore.
32. A castle stood there.
33. "This is the castle east of the sun and west of the moon," he said.

Trading for the Nights / The Third Night / The Wedding Test / The Test and the Wedding
1. She came to the castle east of the sun and west of the moon.
2. There she saw the troll princess, with a long nose.
3. "I will trade this golden apple for one night," the youngest daughter said.
4. The troll princess wanted gold, so she agreed.
5. That night, the youngest daughter came to the prince's room.
6. He lay asleep, but he did not wake.
7. The trolls had given him a sleeping drink.
8. The youngest daughter cried to him.
9. Soon she had to leave the room.
10. She traded the golden comb for one more night there.
11. Again, the prince lay asleep and did not wake.
12. The youngest daughter cried beside him all night.
13. Captives lived in the castle, and they heard her every night.
14. By day, the captives saw the prince, for he had no drink.
15. They decided to tell him what they had heard.
16. The captives told the prince what they knew.
17. "Someone cried by your bed every night," they said.
18. The prince did not know who cried.
19. But he thought it might be her.
20. That night, the trolls gave him the sleeping drink.
21. The prince did not drink it.
22. No one saw him pour it away.
23. He waited in the dark for her to come.
24. The youngest daughter traded the golden wheel for one more night.
25. She came to his room in the dark.
26. The prince was awake.
27. "Is it you?" he asked.
28. "Yes, it is," she said.
29. The prince was glad, and he held her, for it was her.
30. The prince told her they must go away from this castle.
31. "The trolls will not let me go," he said.
32. "I will make one test," said the prince.
33. He showed her his shirt, with the wax still on it.
34. "I will marry only the one who can wash this white," he said.
35. "The troll princess will try first," he said.
36. "But she will fail," said the prince.
37. "Then you will try," he said.
38. The youngest daughter agreed.
39. Soon, morning came to the castle.
40. The prince called the troll princess and her mother to him.
41. The troll princess wanted to be his bride.
42. She believed she could wash it.
43. The troll princess tried to wash the shirt.
44. The more she washed it, the darker it grew.
45. Her mother tried next, but the shirt grew darker still.
46. Soon the youngest daughter stepped forward.
47. "Let me try," she said.
48. The troll princess laughed at her.
49. But the prince let her try.
50. The youngest daughter washed the shirt.
51. Soon it was white.
52. "You will be my true bride," the prince said.
53. The troll princess and her mother grew angry.
54. They ran away into the mountains and never came back.
55. The prince freed all the captives in the castle.
56. The prince married the youngest daughter.
57. They left the castle and went far away together.
58. Her family was glad to see her again.
59. They never saw the trolls again.
