The War of the Worlds

The Red Light on Mars
1. In the last years of the 1800s, no one thought that anything was looking at our world.
2. People went about their work, and they were not afraid of the sky.
3. But on Mars there were old minds, and they were looking at the Earth.
4. Mars was cold and dying, while the Earth was warm and green.
5. The Martians wanted our world, and they made a plan to take it.
6. I lived in Woking, and Ogilvy lived near Ottershaw, where he had a big telescope on the roof of his house.
7. He looked at the stars every night.
8. One warm night I went up to the roof to look at the sky with him.
9. The sky was black and full of stars, and Ogilvy was looking through the telescope.
10. "There is something on Mars," he said.
11. "Come and look at it."
12. I put my eye to the telescope, and I saw a red light on Mars.
13. "What is it?" I asked.
14. "It is a fire on the planet," Ogilvy said.
15. "Gas is coming out of Mars, and it is burning."
16. "It came last night, and it came again."
17. "Is it a big fire?" I asked.
18. "Yes, it is a big fire," he said, "and it is on a planet far out in the sky."
19. "Do people live there?" I asked.
20. "I do not think so," Ogilvy said, "for the planet is cold, and the air is thin."
21. "But something is there on that planet, and I do not know what it is."
22. I looked at Mars again, and the red light was gone.
23. We stood in the dark and looked at the sky for a long time.
24. We went down, and I walked home.
25. The road was quiet, and Woking was dark.
26. The red light came on many nights, and then it came no more.
27. Ogilvy told the people of London about the red light, and they read about it.
28. Most people read the news and forgot it.
29. They did not know that each light was a gun on Mars, and that each gun had sent a cylinder to the Earth.
30. The first cylinder was far out in the sky, and it was coming to the Earth.

The Falling Star
1. One night I looked up from the door of my house and saw a star fall from the sky.
2. It was green, and it left a long white line in the dark behind it.
3. Many people saw it, and they said it was only a falling star.
4. It fell to the ground near Horsell, and no one heard a sound.
5. In the morning Ogilvy came to my house in a hurry.
6. "It was not a star, for I have seen it on the ground near Horsell," he said.
7. "Come with me and see it."
8. We walked to the place, where a big pit lay in the sand.
9. The sand was black and hot, and smoke came up from the hole.
10. In the pit there was a great round thing, half of it under the sand, and it was made of metal.
11. "It is very hot," Ogilvy said, "and no one can go near it."
12. I looked at the pit for a long time but did not go near it.
13. A noise came from inside the round thing, and it came again and again.
14. "Someone is inside," I said.
15. "No one could live in a fire like that," Ogilvy said.
16. "But look at the top."
17. I looked, and I saw that the top was a round lid, and that it was turning slowly.
18. "Someone is turning it," Ogilvy said.
19. "This thing is made, and it came from the sky."
20. "It came from Mars," I said.
21. Ogilvy did not say no, and he went away in a hurry to send word to London.
22. I stayed by the pit while more people came across the fields to look at it.
23. A boy ran to Woking to tell the town, and soon a crowd stood by the pit.
24. No one went near the cylinder, for the sand was hot and the noise went on.
25. Some people said it was a stone from the sky, and some said it was made.

The Crowd at the Pit
1. In the afternoon I went home to eat, and I told my wife about the pit.
2. "Is it safe?" my wife asked.
3. "I do not know," I said, "but I must go back and see it."
4. "Stay here with me," she said.
5. "I will come back soon," I said.
6. When I came back, 200 people stood by the pit.
7. Ogilvy was there with two friends from London, Stent and Henderson.
8. Some boys threw stones at the cylinder, and the stones fell into the sand.
9. "Go back, for the thing is hot and it is not safe," Ogilvy said to the boys.
10. The boys went back, and the crowd waited.

20. I stood at the front with Ogilvy and looked at the lid.
21. Mars was red in the sky above the pit.

23. Then there was a loud noise, and the lid fell off the cylinder.
24. It hit the ground, and the sand flew up.

26. The people at the front were afraid.
27. A man fell, and Ogilvy pulled him back from the edge.
28. "Get back!" Henderson said.
29. "Get back from the pit!"
30. The crowd stood still, and no one spoke.
31. The hole in the cylinder was black, and I could not see inside it.
32. I looked, and I saw two bright eyes in the dark.

The Thing in the Cylinder
1. The eyes moved, and something grey came up out of the hole.
2. It was as big as a bear, and it came up slowly.
3. It had two big dark eyes, and under the eyes there was a mouth.
4. The mouth had no chin, and it moved all the time.
5. It had many long arms, and they moved in the air.
6. Its skin was wet and shone in the light of the stars.
7. I stood still and could not speak.
8. It tried to stand on the edge of the cylinder, but it was too heavy.
9. "It is a Martian, and the Earth is pulling it down," I said.
10. The Martian fell back into the cylinder, and I heard it hit the metal.
11. A woman screamed, and the crowd ran back from the pit.
12. I ran too and did not look back.
13. When I stopped, I was far from the pit, and I stood by a tree.
14. Some people had come back and stood near the edge of the pit.
15. Ogilvy was with them, and Stent and Henderson were there too.
16. "What was it?" a man asked me.
17. "It was a Martian," I said, "and it came out of the cylinder."
18. "Will it come out again?" the man asked.
19. "I do not know," I said, "for it fell back because it was too heavy."
20. "Is it afraid of us?" the man asked.
21. "I do not know," I said, "for it is not like us."
22. "Will it kill us?" he asked.
23. "I do not know that," I said.
24. "Is it big?" the man asked.
25. "It is as big as a bear," I said.
26. We stood in the dark and looked at the pit.
27. A man said that Ogilvy was going to the pit with two friends, and that they had a white flag.

The Flag

The Road Home
1. I got up from the ditch and looked around.
2. The trees near the pit were on fire, and the sand was black.
3. Dead people lay on the ground, and I did not look at them for long.
4. I walked away from the pit and did not stop until I came to the road.
5. The road was quiet, and the lights of the houses were on.
6. On the road I saw a man in a cart, and he stopped and looked at me.
7. "What has happened to you?" he asked.
8. "The Martians are in the pit," I said, "and they have a light that burns people."
9. "Ogilvy is dead, and so are Stent and Henderson."
10. The man laughed, and he said that I was ill and drove on.
11. I walked on alone and looked back at the sky many times.
12. My wife was at the door of our house, and she looked at me.
13. "Where have you been?" she asked.
14. "Your hands are black, and your face is black."
15. "I have been at the pit," I said.
16. "Come in, and I will tell you."
17. We went into the house, and I sat down by the table.
18. I told her about the Martian, the flag and the light.
19. She did not say a word until I had finished.
20. "Will they come here?" she asked.
21. "I do not think so," I said, "for they are too heavy, and they cannot stand on the Earth."
22. "But they have that light, and it burns people," she said.
23. "It burned Ogilvy and the trees," I said, "but it cannot go far."
24. "They are in a pit, and they will stay in the pit."
25. "Tomorrow the soldiers will come," I said, "and they will have guns."
26. "Guns are good," my wife said, "but that light is not."
27. I ate some food and drank some wine, and I felt better.
28. But I could not forget the eyes of the Martian or the death of Ogilvy.
29. That night I lay in bed for a long time and did not sleep.

8. "Keep your soldiers far from the pit," I said.

10. All day a noise came from the pit, and it was the noise of metal.
11. It did not stop, and it was loud at night.

23. In the morning people said that a second cylinder lay in a wood near Weybridge.

The Machine on the Hill
1. That afternoon I heard guns near the pit, and the noise came again and again.
2. My wife and I went up the hill behind our house and looked toward Horsell.
3. There was smoke over the trees, and we saw soldiers running on the road below us.
4. "They are shooting at the pit," I said.
5. "Will they kill the Martians?" my wife asked.
6. "I do not know," I said, "so let us wait and see."
7. Then something rose over the trees, and it was taller than the houses.
8. It was a machine made of metal, and it stood on three long legs.
9. On top of the legs there was a big round hood, and the hood moved from side to side.
10. The machine took a step, and the trees fell under its feet.
11. "That is a machine," my wife said, "and it is walking!"
12. "A Martian is in it," I said, "for someone must be driving it."
13. The machine turned toward the soldiers, and the hood shone.
14. A flash of light went out from the hood, and the soldiers and their guns were on fire.
15. The trees near them were on fire too, and smoke went up into the sky.
16. No one shot at the machine again, and I did not see one soldier stand up.
17. "Are they all dead?" my wife asked.
18. "I do not know," I said, and I held her hand.
19. The machine walked on, and the light went across the town of Woking.
20. The roofs of the houses were on fire, and people ran out into the road.
21. "We must go now!" my wife said, and she took my hand.
22. We ran down the hill to our house.
23. I knew then that guns could not stop the Martians, and that the Martians had come to kill us all.

Dead London
1. I came to the river at Putney and went over the bridge into London.
2. The streets were empty, and the windows of the houses were broken.
3. Dead people lay in the road, and no one had moved them.
4. Red weed grew on the walls and the roofs.

6. I heard the cry again, and it was loud.
7. "Ulla, ulla, ulla, ulla," it said, and it came from the north.
8. I walked toward it and did not care if I lived or died.
9. I wanted to see the end.
10. The cry grew louder, and then it stopped.
11. The city was still.
12. I came to a hill, and on the hill there was a machine.
13. It stood on its three legs, and the hood was down.
14. It did not move.
15. Birds sat on the hood, and one of them flew down and came back.
16. I stood and looked at it and waited for the light to come.
17. The light did not come, and the machine did not move.
18. I went up the hill and saw that the hood was open.
19. Inside there was a Martian, and it was dead.
20. Its grey body lay half out of the hood, and the birds were eating it.
21. There were more dead Martians in a pit behind the machine, and birds were eating them too.
22. "They are dead," I said to myself, and I said it again.
23. I did not know why they were dead.
24. The Martians had come to a world that was not their own.
25. They had no way to fight the germs of the Earth, and the germs had killed them.
26. We have lived with germs for a long time, and many of us have died of them.
27. But the Martians did not know them, and they died soon.

The Bells of London

After the War
1. The dead Martians were taken away, and the machines were taken away too.
2. People looked at the bodies for a long time, and they saw that a Martian was only a head and two hands.
3. It did not eat as we eat, and it took blood from other living things.
4. Some of the machines were kept, and people could make metal and machines like them.
5. I went back to my house in Maybury with my wife, and the door was open.
6. The rooms were empty, and the red weed on the walls was dead.
7. On my desk I found a page that I wrote before the war.
8. It said that people would soon live in peace, and that no danger could come from the sky.
9. I read it to my wife, and we laughed.
10. "We did not know," she said.
11. "The Martians did not know about the germs," I said.
