Ali Baba and the Forty Thieves

Two Brothers / Open Sesame / The Cave of Treasure / The Golden Cup
1. Ali Baba lived in a house with his wife.
2. He was a poor woodcutter.
3. Every day, Ali Baba took his donkeys into the forest.
4. He sold wood in the town.
5. Ali Baba's brother was Cassim.
6. Cassim was a rich merchant with many servants.
7. Cassim gave Ali Baba no money.
8. Ali Baba never asked his brother for money.
9. He and his wife worked every day.
10. One morning, Ali Baba took his donkeys into the forest.
11. He walked into the trees to find wood.
12. He heard many horses coming near.
13. Ali Baba stopped his work and listened.
14. Ali Baba hid behind a tree.
15. His donkeys waited near the trees.
16. He saw many men ride out of the trees on horses.
17. Ali Baba counted the men and found there were 40 robbers.
18. The robbers stopped their horses before a great rock.
19. The robber captain got down and walked to the rock.
20. "Open Sesame!" said the robber captain.
21. The great rock opened to show a dark cave inside.
22. Poor Ali Baba watched from behind his tree and was afraid.
23. The 40 robbers went into the dark cave.
24. They stayed inside for some time.
25. The robbers came out again.
26. The rock closed behind them.
27. The robber captain got back on his horse.
28. The 40 robbers rode away into the forest.
29. Ali Baba came out from behind his tree.
30. Ali Baba took his donkeys back to the great rock.
31. "Open Sesame!" said Ali Baba.
32. The rock opened, and Ali Baba went inside.
33. He saw a cave full of gold.
34. Ali Baba had never seen so much gold.
35. No robbers were in the cave.
36. Ali Baba put some gold into sacks.
37. He put the sacks on his donkeys.
38. He walked out, and the rock closed behind him.
39. Ali Baba took his donkeys and the gold to his house.
40. Ali Baba's wife saw the gold.
41. "Where did you find this gold?" she asked.
42. Ali Baba told her of the robbers and the cave.
43. Ali Baba's wife wanted to count the gold.
44. There was too much gold to count one by one.
45. "I will borrow a cup from Cassim's wife," she said.
46. She went to Cassim's house to ask for a cup.
47. Cassim's wife gave her a cup.
48. But she wondered why a poor woodcutter's wife needed this cup.
49. Cassim's wife put some fat in the bottom of the cup.
50. Ali Baba's wife counted the gold with the cup.
51. She gave the cup back to Cassim's wife.
52. Cassim's wife looked at the bottom of the cup.
53. A gold coin was stuck to the fat.
54. Cassim's wife showed Cassim the gold coin.
55. "Your poor brother has gold?" said Cassim.
56. Cassim was angry that Ali Baba had not told him.
57. "I will find out where this gold came from," said Cassim.

The Secret Told / Open Barley / Ali Baba's Search / A Secret Kept
1. Cassim went to Ali Baba's house.
2. Cassim was rich, but he wanted more.
3. "Where did you find this gold?" Cassim asked.
4. Ali Baba did not want to tell his brother the secret.
5. But Cassim was angry, and he stayed.
6. "Tell me now!" said Cassim.
7. He told Cassim about the robbers and the cave.
8. "Open Sesame!" Ali Baba told him.
9. These words would open the rock, he said.
10. Ali Baba told Cassim not to forget the words.
11. Cassim listened to the words.
12. "I will get much more gold than you did," said Cassim.
13. The next morning, Cassim took 10 donkeys to the forest.
14. Cassim came to the rock with his donkeys.
15. "Open Sesame!" said Cassim.
16. The rock opened, Cassim went inside, and it closed behind him.
17. He saw the cave full of gold.
18. Cassim took much more gold than Ali Baba did.
19. He filled sack after sack and put them on his donkeys.
20. Cassim walked to the rock to leave.
21. But he had forgotten the words.
22. "Open Wheat!" said Cassim.
23. The rock did not open.
24. "Open Barley!" said Cassim.
25. Cassim was alone and afraid, and he called for help.
26. But no one came.
27. Then the robbers came back to the cave.
28. The rock opened, and the robbers found Cassim inside.
29. The robbers killed Cassim to keep their secret.
30. Cassim did not come home that night.
31. Cassim's wife was afraid and went to Ali Baba.
32. "Cassim went to the forest and did not come back," she said.
33. Ali Baba was afraid for his brother.
34. He took a donkey and went to find Cassim.
35. Ali Baba came to the rock.
36. The rock opened, and Ali Baba went inside.
37. He found Cassim's body in the cave.
38. Ali Baba knew the robbers had killed Cassim.
39. He put his brother's body on the donkey.
40. Ali Baba also took some gold for Cassim's wife.
41. Ali Baba waited until night to go home.
42. He brought the donkey home in the dark.
43. Cassim's wife cried when she saw her husband.
44. No one in the town saw Ali Baba come home.
45. Cassim's wife did not know what to do.
46. She was afraid that neighbors would ask why Cassim had died.
47. Cassim had a clever servant named Morgiana.
48. She went to the neighbors and looked sad.
49. "Cassim was ill, and now he has died," said Morgiana.
50. The neighbors were sad for the family.
51. Cassim was buried, and no one asked why.
52. Ali Baba wanted to help his brother's family.
53. He took Cassim's wife and Morgiana into his house.
54. Ali Baba gave Cassim's wife a home and money.
55. Morgiana was now a servant in Ali Baba's house.
56. Ali Baba was glad, for Morgiana was clever.
57. The secret of the robbers and the cave stayed safe.

The Mark on the Door / Every Door the Same / The Oil Merchant
1. The robbers came back to the cave many times.
2. One day, they saw that Cassim's body was gone.
3. "Someone knows our secret," said the robber captain.
4. The robber captain was angry that someone knew their secret.
5. He sent one robber into the town.
6. "Find out who took the body," said the robber captain.
7. This robber walked into the town.
8. He heard that a rich merchant named Cassim had died.
9. He heard that Cassim had a brother named Ali Baba.
10. The robber found Ali Baba's house in the town.
11. He had chalk with him.
12. The robber marked Ali Baba's door with the chalk.
13. "Now I know which house it is," he said.
14. The robber went back to the forest.
15. The next morning, Morgiana went out into the town.
16. She saw that someone had marked the door with chalk.
17. Morgiana wondered why the door was marked.
18. No one in the house had marked the door.
19. Morgiana took chalk.
20. She marked every other door on the street too.
21. All the doors on the street looked the same.
22. That night, the robber led the others back to the street.
23. He looked for the door he had marked.
24. But every door looked the same.
25. "I cannot tell which house it is," said the robber.
26. The robber captain was angry at him.
27. The robbers could not find the house, so they went back to the forest.
28. The robber captain went to see Ali Baba's house himself.
29. The robber captain disguised himself as an oil merchant to get inside.
30. He hid 39 robbers in 39 jars.
31. He filled one more jar with real oil.
32. The disguised captain had 40 jars on his donkeys.
33. He rode to Ali Baba's house with the jars.
34. "I am an oil merchant," the disguised captain said to Ali Baba.
35. "May I stay the night with my jars?" he asked.
36. Ali Baba was kind to all guests.
37. "You may put your jars in my courtyard," said Ali Baba.
38. "Stay and eat with us tonight," said Ali Baba.
39. Ali Baba's son also welcomed the guest.
40. He put his jars in the courtyard.
41. He thanked Ali Baba and came in to eat.
42. Morgiana cooked for the guest that night.

Is It Time? / The Captain's Escape / The Dagger Dance / Morgiana's Reward
1. That night, Morgiana needed oil for the lamps.
2. She went to the jars in the courtyard.
3. Morgiana opened the first jar to get oil.
4. A robber inside the jar whispered, "Is it time?"
5. Morgiana was afraid, but she stayed calm.
6. "Not yet," she whispered back.
7. Morgiana checked the next jar.
8. Another robber was hidden in that jar too.
9. She checked every jar in the courtyard.
10. A robber was hidden in each jar but one.
11. The last jar held real oil.
12. Morgiana took the oil and heated it until it boiled.
13. She poured the hot oil into each jar with a robber inside.
14. The hot oil killed the robbers in the jars.
15. The disguised captain waited for his men to come out of the jars.
16. He had told them to come out when he gave a signal.
17. But no robber came out of any jar.
18. The disguised captain went to the courtyard alone.
19. He looked inside the jars, one by one.
20. He found his men dead in the jars.
21. The disguised captain was afraid.
22. The disguised captain ran from the courtyard in the night.
23. In the morning, Ali Baba heard what Morgiana had done.
24. Morgiana told Ali Baba about the oil merchant's jars.
25. "The jars were full of robbers, not oil," said Morgiana.
26. Ali Baba knew that Morgiana had saved his life.
27. He was glad and thanked Morgiana.
28. The captain was now alone, for all his men were dead.
29. He wanted revenge.
30. The captain disguised himself again, as another merchant.
31. He went to the town and met Ali Baba's son.
32. The captain became friends with Ali Baba's son.
33. Ali Baba's son did not know who this merchant was.
34. One day, Ali Baba's son asked the merchant to his home.
35. The captain came to Ali Baba's house as a guest.
36. Ali Baba welcomed the guest.
37. Morgiana looked at the guest.
38. She recognized the captain, though he was disguised.
39. But Morgiana said nothing yet.
40. Morgiana asked to dance for the guest with a dagger.
41. She danced before the captain with the dagger.
42. Then Morgiana killed the captain with the dagger.
43. Ali Baba came and found the dead captain, with Morgiana and the dagger.
44. "You have saved my life again," said Ali Baba.
45. "The guest was the robber captain," said Morgiana.
46. Ali Baba knew that Morgiana had saved him twice.
47. "You are no longer a servant," said Ali Baba.
48. He gave Morgiana her freedom.
49. Ali Baba's son wanted Morgiana for his wife, and Ali Baba was glad.
50. Ali Baba married Morgiana to his son.
51. They alone knew the secret of the cave.
52. The family used the gold wisely.
