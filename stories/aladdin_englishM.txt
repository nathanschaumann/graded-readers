Aladdin

A Poor Tailor's Son
1. Aladdin lived with his mother in a house.
2. His father had worked as a tailor.
3. But the tailor died.
4. After that, Aladdin's mother had little money.
5. She worked hard for money every day.
6. Aladdin did not want to work.
7. He did not want a trade.
8. Aladdin played in the market each day.
9. "You must work," his mother said.
10. "You must learn a trade," she said again.
11. He was now a young man, but still poor.
12. One day, a stranger came to the market.
13. The stranger looked hard at Aladdin.
14. He had a long beard and rich clothes, and he did not smile.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Aladdin lives with his mother in a house; his father, once a tailor, died, and since then his mother has worked hard for money every day.
6-10: Aladdin does not want to work or learn a trade, and plays in the market each day despite his mother's words.
11-14: Aladdin still will not work, and he is now a poor young man with no trade, until one day a stranger comes to the market, looks hard at him, and does not smile.

An Uncle from Africa
1. The stranger was a magician from Africa.
2. The magician was looking for Aladdin, a poor young man with no trade.
3. "Was the tailor your father?" the magician asked.
4. "Yes, but my father died," said Aladdin.
5. "I am your uncle," the magician said.
6. "I am your father's brother from Africa."
7. The magician gave Aladdin some gold, for his mother still worked hard every day.
8. But Aladdin did not know this man.
9. The magician went to Aladdin's house.
10. Aladdin's mother did not know of this uncle, and she was afraid.
11. "Aladdin's father had no brother," she said.
12. The magician said he had been away.
13. He gave Aladdin's mother gold.
14. Aladdin's mother believed the magician.
15. The magician said he would help Aladdin learn a trade.
16. "I will make him a rich merchant," he said.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The stranger is a magician from Africa who has been looking for Aladdin, a poor young man with no trade; he asks if Aladdin's father was the tailor, then claims to be his uncle, the tailor's brother from Africa.
7-10: The magician gives Aladdin gold, since his mother still works hard every day, and Aladdin is glad since he is still poor, though he does not know this man; the magician goes to Aladdin's house, but Aladdin's mother is afraid since she has never heard of this uncle.
11-16: The magician tells her he had been away, gives her gold too, and promises to make Aladdin a rich merchant, so she believes him and smiles at him.

The Cave in the Hills
1. The magician came for Aladdin.
2. "Come with me on a walk," the magician said.
3. They walked into the hills.
4. They came to a place with no one.
5. The magician said magic words that Aladdin did not know.
6. The ground shook and opened.
7. Aladdin was afraid.
8. Where the ground had opened was a stone with a metal ring.
9. "Under this stone are steps down to a cave," the magician said.
10. "The cave holds gold and treasure."
11. "Only you can lift this stone," said the magician.
12. The magician gave Aladdin a ring to keep him safe.
13. Aladdin lifted the metal ring.
14. The stone came up, and stone steps went down into the dark.

Paragraphs:

10-14: The magician says the cave holds gold and treasure and only Aladdin can lift the stone, then gives him a ring to keep him safe, and when Aladdin lifts the metal ring, the stone comes up to reveal steps leading down into the dark.
