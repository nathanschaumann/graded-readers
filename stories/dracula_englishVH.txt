Dracula

The Road East
1. Jonathan was a lawyer in London, and he worked for an old man named Hawkins.
2. One day a rich man, Count Dracula, wrote to Hawkins.
3. The Count wanted to buy a house in England, and he needed a lawyer to help him.
4. Hawkins was too sick to go, so he sent Jonathan to the Count's castle in Transylvania.
5. Jonathan loved a woman named Mina, and she wanted to marry him.
6. "I will write to you every day," Jonathan said to Mina at the train.
7. "Come home soon," Mina said.
8. He rode a train for many days, and the land was wild in the east.
9. Every night he wrote in his book, and he wrote to Mina.
10. At last he came to a town named Bistritz, and he found a room at an inn.
11. A letter from the Count was there.
12. "Come to my country," the Count wrote.
13. "Sleep well tonight, and take the coach to my castle tomorrow."
14. "I will wait for you there."
15. The woman of the inn asked Jonathan about the letter.
16. "I must go to the castle of Count Dracula," Jonathan said.
17. The woman cried out, and she looked afraid.
18. "Must you go there?" she asked.
19. "Yes, I must," Jonathan said.
20. "It is my work."
21. "Tomorrow is a bad day," the woman said.
22. "Tomorrow night all the bad things will walk in this land."
23. "Do you know where you are going?"
24. "I do not know what you mean," Jonathan said.
25. The woman began to cry, and she took a small cross from her neck.
26. "Take this, and put it on your neck," she said.
27. Jonathan did not want it, but she cried, so he took it.
28. "I will put it on my neck, and I will not take it off," he said.
29. The man of the inn said nothing.
30. Jonathan went up to his room with the cross on his neck.
31. That night he wrote in his book, but he did not sleep well.
32. A dog cried in the yard until the morning.
33. In the morning the coach came to the door, and the woman was there.
34. "Do not go," she said.
35. Jonathan said no, and he went out to the coach.

The Coach
1. The coach was old, and four horses pulled it up the road into the mountains.
2. Two men and two women rode in the coach with Jonathan.
3. Each of them looked at him, and each of them looked afraid.
4. "Where do you go?" an old woman asked.
5. "I go to the castle of Count Dracula," Jonathan said.
6. The people in the coach did not say a word, and each of them put a hand on a cross.
7. The old woman gave Jonathan some garlic.
8. "Put it on your neck," she said, "for the night is bad."
9. Jonathan did not know why he must do it, but he took the garlic.
10. The road went up and up, and the sun was low.
11. At last the coach came to a high place between two mountains, and the driver stopped the horses.
12. A black coach was there, and four black horses stood before it.
13. A tall man sat in front of the black coach.
14. Jonathan could not see his face, but his eyes were red in the light.
15. "You are early tonight," the tall man said.
16. The people of the old coach looked at the ground, and the driver put Jonathan's bag down.
17. Jonathan went to the black coach, and the tall man took his hand.
18. The hand was cold and strong.
19. The black coach went fast, and Jonathan could not see the road.
20. A wolf cried in the night, and then another wolf cried.
21. Jonathan saw a small blue fire near the road.
22. The tall man stopped the horses and went to look at the fire.
23. The fire was there, and then it was gone.
24. The coach went on, and the wolves came near.
25. The horses cried out, for they were afraid.
26. The tall man put up his hand, and the wolves went away.
27. At last the coach came to a big old castle on a mountain.
28. The castle was dark, and there was no light in it.
29. The tall man put Jonathan's bag down before the door and went away.
30. Jonathan did not see where he went.
31. He stood before the door and waited.

The Count
1. The door opened, and an old man stood there with a lamp in his hand.
2. He was tall, and he was dressed in black from his head to his feet.
3. "Welcome to my house," the old man said.
4. "I am Count Dracula, and you are Jonathan."
5. "Come in, and do not be afraid."
6. Jonathan put out his hand, and the Count took it.
7. The hand was cold, and Jonathan thought of the driver.
8. The Count took the bag in one hand and went before Jonathan into the castle.
9. In a big room a fire was lit, and food was on the table.
10. "Eat, and then you will sleep," the Count said.
11. Jonathan ate and drank, for he was cold and hungry.
12. The Count did not eat.
13. "I ate before you came," he said, "and I do not drink wine."
14. He sat by the fire and looked at Jonathan.
15. "Was the road bad?" the Count asked.
16. "Yes, it was bad, and the night was cold," Jonathan said.
17. "Your driver put up his hand, and the wolves went away."
18. "He is a strong man," the Count said, and he looked at the fire.
19. In the light of the fire, Jonathan saw the Count's face.
20. It was white, and the mouth was red, and the teeth were long.
21. Jonathan wanted to look away, but he did not.
22. A wolf cried in the night, and then another wolf cried.
23. "Listen to the wolves," the Count said.
24. "They cry every night."
25. "I know them well, and they know me."
26. "Are you not afraid of them?" Jonathan asked.
27. "No," the Count said, and he did not say more.
28. "You are tired," the Count said.
29. "Go to your room, and sleep well."
30. "I sleep in the day, and I will wake at night."
31. "You will see me at night, and not before."
32. "I am glad you came, for I need a lawyer."
33. "Tomorrow you will help me with my house in England."
34. Jonathan went to his room, and the bed was big and the room was warm.
35. He put his hand on the cross on his neck, and he did not know why.

The Mirror
1. In the morning Jonathan woke.
2. A small mirror was on the wall, and he went to it to shave.
3. "Good morning," the Count said behind him.

7. The Count looked at the blood, and his eyes were red.
8. He put his hand out to Jonathan's throat.

10. He took his hand away and did not say a word.
11. "Take care," the Count said.

13. "And this is a bad thing."
14. The Count took the mirror and threw it out of the window.
15. It broke on the ground, and the Count went out of the room.
16. Jonathan went to the room where he had eaten, and food was on the table.
17. He ate, and he did not see the Count.
18. There was no other man in the castle, and the Count did all the work.
19. Jonathan thought of the mirror and the driver and the wolves.
20. He wrote in his book, and he wrote of the mirror.
21. That night the Count came to the room and sat by the fire.
22. "Tell me about the house in England," he said.
23. "I have a house for you," Jonathan said.
24. "It is a big old house with a high wall, and it is near London."
25. "Is it near other houses?" the Count asked.
26. "No," said Jonathan, "there is a house for sick men."
27. "That is good," the Count said.
28. "I am old, and I like an old house."
29. "I like a house that is dark."
30. "Tell me all about it, and tell me about London."
31. Jonathan told him all that he knew, and the Count asked many things.
32. The wolves cried, and the Count listened to them.

The Count's Land
1. In the next days Jonathan found a room full of books, and the books were from England.
2. "I read every day," the Count said, "but I do not speak as you do."
3. "Help me speak well, for I want to be a man of London."
4. "I want to walk in the streets of London, and I want to be one of the crowd."
5. On the table was a big map of London, and the Count knew each street.
6. He put his hand on the map, and he put it on the house.
7. "Here is the house," the Count said.
8. "It is near a church, and it has a high wall."
9. Jonathan told the Count the names of the streets, and the Count said each name again.
10. Jonathan looked at the map and saw that the Count had a plan.
11. Every night the Count and Jonathan talked by the fire.
12. "This is my land, and my people have lived here for a long time," the Count said.
13. "My fathers were strong men, and they fought many wars."
14. "They fought the Turks, and they held this land."
15. "I am the last of them, and I am old."
16. The Count's eyes were red as he spoke, and his hand was on the table.
17. "In London no one knows me," he said.
18. "I will be a stranger there, and I will be one man in a crowd."
19. Jonathan wrote it in his book, but he did not know what the Count meant.
20. When the day came, the Count stopped, and he went out of the room.
21. Jonathan went to the window and looked at the mountains.
22. He could see the road, and there was no man on it.
23. The road went down into the trees, and Jonathan wanted to be on it.
24. He thought that he would ask the Count to let him go home.

The Locked Doors

2. Van Helsing and Seward went with him.
3. Lucy was weak, and her face was white, but she looked at Arthur.
4. "Arthur, my love," Lucy said.
5. "I am glad you came."
6. Arthur took her hand and sat by her bed.
7. Lucy's eyes were shut, and her words were slow.
8. "Kiss me, Arthur," Lucy said.
9. Arthur bent over her, but Van Helsing took his arm and pulled him back.
10. "No!" Van Helsing said.
11. "Not now!"
12. Arthur looked at Van Helsing, and he did not know why.
13. Lucy's lips were red, and her teeth were long.
14. Seward saw them, and he did not speak.
15. Lucy slept, and her breath was slow, and it grew slower.
16. Arthur held her hand and did not let it go.
17. In the night Lucy died, and Arthur cried.
18. Van Helsing put his hand on Arthur's arm.
19. "She is gone," Van Helsing said.
20. "I did not kiss her," Arthur said.
21. "You will know why," Van Helsing said.
22. "Go to bed, for you are tired."
23. Arthur went out of the room, and Seward and Van Helsing did not go.
24. After a time Lucy's teeth were not long, and her face was white and good to see.
25. "She looked well, as she did before," Seward said.
26. "It is not done," Van Helsing said.
27. Van Helsing said no more, and Seward did not know what he meant.
28. Seward stayed by the coffin and looked at Lucy's face.
29. Lucy was put in a coffin, and Van Helsing put garlic flowers in the coffin with her.
30. The coffin was put in a tomb in a church yard.
31. Seward asked Van Helsing why he put the flowers there.
32. "I will tell you tomorrow," Van Helsing said.
33. Arthur and Quincey went home, and they did not speak.

The Beautiful Lady
1. Many days later a paper told of children who were lost in the night.
2. Each child was found the next day, and each child was weak.
3. Each child had two small marks on its neck.
4. "A beautiful lady took me," the children said.
5. Van Helsing read the paper with Seward.
6. "Look at the marks on the children," Van Helsing said.
7. "They are the marks that Lucy had on her neck."
8. "Come with me tonight to the tomb," Van Helsing said.
9. That night Van Helsing and Seward went to the church yard, and they took a bag.
10. Van Helsing opened the tomb and opened Lucy's coffin.
11. The coffin was empty.
12. "Lucy is not here," Seward said.
13. "Do you see?" Van Helsing said.
14. Seward looked into the coffin again.
15. Van Helsing shut the tomb, and the two men stood behind a tree.
16. They waited a long time in the dark.
17. At last a white shape came through the dark, and it held a child.
18. It was Lucy, and her lips were red with blood.
19. Seward did not speak, for he could not believe it.
20. Lucy saw the men, and she put the child down.
21. Her face was white, and her eyes were red.
22. Van Helsing stood before the tomb and held up a cross.
23. Lucy looked at the cross and stepped back.
24. Seward saw her face, and it was not the face of Lucy.
25. Lucy could not go to the tomb, and she went away into the dark.
26. Van Helsing put holy bread in the door of the tomb, and he shut it.
27. Lucy could not go in now.
28. The men took the child and put it in the road, where a man would find it.
29. "Come again tomorrow night with Arthur," Van Helsing said.
30. "What is Lucy?" Seward asked.
31. "Tomorrow I will tell you all," Van Helsing said.

The Door of the Tomb
1. The next day Van Helsing spoke to Arthur and Quincey and Seward.
2. "Lucy is dead, but she walks in the night," Van Helsing said.
3. "She takes blood from children."
4. "You are mad," Arthur said, and he was angry.

6. That night the four men went to the church yard.
7. Van Helsing opened the tomb, and Arthur looked at the coffin.
8. The coffin was empty.
9. "Lucy is gone," Arthur said.
10. "Who took her?"
11. "No one took her," Van Helsing said.
12. "Wait, and you will see."
13. The men stood behind the trees and waited.
14. Late in the night a white shape came through the dark.
15. It was Lucy, and she held no child.
16. Her lips were red, and her eyes were red.

20. Van Helsing stood before the door of the tomb and held up the cross.
21. Lucy looked at Arthur, and then she looked at the cross.
22. She could not come near, and she showed her long teeth.
23. Quincey held Arthur's arm, and Arthur stopped.
24. Lucy went away from the door, and the men waited.
25. Van Helsing took the holy bread out of the door of the tomb.
26. Lucy came back, and this time she went into the tomb.
27. The men went in after her, and Van Helsing shut the door behind them.
28. Lucy lay in the open coffin, and her eyes were shut.
29. Her cheeks were pink, and her lips were red, and she looked well.
30. "Lucy," Arthur said, and his voice was low.
31. Arthur looked at her for a long time and could not speak.
32. Quincey stood behind Arthur, and he did not speak either.
33. "Lucy is dead, and this is not Lucy," Van Helsing said.

The Stake
1. "Lucy is not alive, and she is not dead," Van Helsing said.
2. "She will take the blood of more children, and the children will die."
3. "She will make more like herself."
4. "She must be free, and you must do it, Arthur."
5. "She was to be my wife, and I loved her," Arthur said.
6. "You loved her, and so it must be your hand," Van Helsing said.
7. Arthur looked at Lucy, and then he looked at Van Helsing.
8. "Tell me what I must do," Arthur said.
9. Van Helsing gave Arthur a long stake of wood and a hammer.
10. "Put the point over her heart," Van Helsing said.
11. "Strike it with the hammer."
12. Seward and Quincey stood by the coffin, and each of them held a lamp.
13. Arthur put the stake over Lucy's heart, and his hand was cold.
14. He struck the stake with the hammer.
15. Lucy cried out, and her body shook.
16. Blood came up around the stake, and her lips were red with it.
17. Arthur struck again and again, and the stake went in.
18. At last Lucy did not move.
19. Her lips were not red now, and her teeth were not long.
20. Her face was Lucy's face again, and it was good to see.
21. "Lucy is dead now," Van Helsing said.
22. Van Helsing said some words over Lucy, and no one moved.
23. Arthur cried, but his face was glad.
24. "Thank you," Arthur said.
25. "I love her, and I am glad that I did it."
26. Van Helsing cut off Lucy's head and put garlic in her mouth.
27. Arthur went out of the tomb, and Quincey went with him and took him home.
28. Van Helsing and Seward shut the coffin, and they shut the door of the tomb.
29. "Our work is not done," Van Helsing said.
30. "Someone made Lucy what she was, and we must find him."

Mina and Van Helsing
1. One morning Mina read in the paper that Lucy was dead.
2. She cried, for Lucy was her friend, and she did not know how Lucy died.
3. Jonathan sat by her and held her hand.
4. That afternoon a man came to their door, and he was old and strong.
5. "I am Van Helsing," the man said.
6. "I was Lucy's friend, and I want to read the book you wrote at Whitby."
7. Mina gave Van Helsing her book about Whitby.
8. She gave him Jonathan's book too, for she had written it out.
9. "It tells of the Count and his castle," Mina said.
10. "My husband was there, and he saw wolves and blood."
11. Van Helsing took the two books and said that he would read them that night.
12. He went away, and Mina and Jonathan waited.
13. The next day Van Helsing came back, and his eyes were red.
14. "You are a strong woman, and your husband is a strong man," he said.
15. "I read the books all night, and now I know much that I did not know."
16. Jonathan looked at him and did not speak.
17. "Tell me about the night on the cliff," Van Helsing said to Mina.
18. Mina told him of the seat and the tall dark shape with red eyes.
19. She told him of the two small marks on Lucy's neck.
20. "I thought a pin cut her," Mina said.
21. "Was it the pin?"
22. "No," Van Helsing said.
23. "It was not the pin."
24. "Do you believe my book?" Jonathan asked.
25. "I believe every word," Van Helsing said.
26. "I thought I was mad," Jonathan said, and he cried.
27. "You are not mad," Van Helsing said.
28. "You saw the Count, and the Count is real."
29. Mina asked how Lucy died.
30. "It is a hard story, and I will tell it at Seward's house," Van Helsing said.
31. "Come there tomorrow."
32. Mina and Jonathan said that they would come.

The Rules
1. The next day Mina and Jonathan went to Seward's house.
2. Arthur and Quincey and Seward were there, and Van Helsing sat with them.
3. Mina had written out all the books, and each man read them.
4. When they had read all the books, Van Helsing began to speak.

6. "He is dead, but he cannot die."
7. "He takes the blood of men and women."
8. "He has come to London, for there are many men and women there."
9. "He is as strong as 20 men, and he may be a wolf or a bat or a mist."
10. "He has no face in a mirror."
11. "He sleeps in the day, in earth from his own land."
12. "He cannot come into a house unless someone asks him in."
13. "Garlic and the cross and holy bread keep him back."
14. "The stake, and the cutting off of the head, will end him."
15. "We saw it with Lucy," Arthur said.
16. "How do we find him?" Arthur asked.

18. "If we find each box and put holy bread in it, he cannot sleep in the day."
19. "The house he bought is Carfax," Jonathan said.
20. "I sold it to him."
21. "Carfax is next to my house!" Seward said.
22. "Renfield stood at its door and said, 'Master.'"
23. "The boxes are there," Van Helsing said.
24. "When do we go?" Quincey asked.
25. "We go tomorrow night," Van Helsing said.
26. "Take a cross, and take garlic."
27. "I will go," Arthur said.
28. "I will go too," Quincey said.
29. "I will go," Seward said, "for the house is next to mine."
30. Jonathan said that he would go too, and Van Helsing said yes.
31. Mina wanted to go with the men, but they said no.
32. "It is not safe for a woman," Jonathan said.

Carfax
1. The next night five men went to Carfax, and Jonathan had the key.
2. Each man took a lamp and a cross and garlic.
3. Van Helsing took a bag of holy bread, and Arthur took three dogs and a whistle.
4. The wall was high, and the old house was dark.
5. Jonathan opened the door, and the air in the house was bad.
6. The men went through the old rooms and found nothing.
7. At last they came to the old church next to the house.
8. The door was locked, and Jonathan opened it with the key.
9. The smell of earth was strong in the church.
10. There were 29 big boxes on the floor, and each box was full of earth.
11. Van Helsing put his hand in the earth and said that it was earth from the Count's own land.
12. "The Count had 50 boxes," Van Helsing said.
13. "Where are the other boxes?" Quincey asked.
14. "He has put them in other houses," Jonathan said.
15. Van Helsing put holy bread in each box, and the men looked on.
16. On a table in the old house Seward found a paper with the names of other houses in London.
17. "The Count has other houses," Seward said.
18. "We will go to each house, and we will find each box."
19. Rats came out of the dark, and there were many of them.
20. Their eyes were red in the light of the lamps.
21. The rats came toward the men, and the men held up their crosses.
22. Arthur blew the whistle, and the three dogs ran in.
23. The rats ran away, and the dogs went after them, and the church was still.
24. The Count was not there, and the men went out.
25. "He will know what we did," Jonathan said.
26. "He will not sleep here again," Van Helsing said.
27. "We must find the other boxes before he finds them."

1. The next morning Van Helsing spoke to Mina.
2. "The Count made you drink his blood, and now he has a road to you," he said.
3. "But it is a road to him too."
4. "In the morning you will tell us what he hears and what he sees."
5. "I will put you to sleep, and you will speak."
6. Mina sat in a chair, and Van Helsing moved his hand before her face.
7. Mina's eyes shut, and she spoke in a low voice.
8. "I hear water," Mina said.
9. "I hear wood, and I hear a rope, and I hear men above me."
10. "It is dark, and the water moves."
11. "He is on a ship," Van Helsing said.
12. "He is in his box, and the ship is on the sea."
13. Seward went out and found the name of a ship in the paper.
14. "The Czarina Catherine left London this morning for Varna," Seward said.
15. "That is the ship," Van Helsing said.
16. "The ship will take many days," Van Helsing said.
17. "We will go by land, and we will be in Varna first."
18. "I must go with you," Mina said.
19. "It is a long road, and it is not safe," Jonathan said.
20. "It is not safe for me to stay," Mina said, "for the Count may come to me."
21. Van Helsing looked at Mina, and he said yes.
22. "It is good that you are with us," he said.
23. Each man took a cross and garlic and holy bread.
24. Quincey took a big knife from Texas, and Jonathan took a knife too.
25. That night they went to the train.
26. The train went east, and the land was wild.
27. The men did not sleep, and they looked out at the dark land.
28. Mina sat by Jonathan and held his hand.
29. She did not sleep, and she thought of the Count on the sea.

The River
1. The train took many days, and at last they came to Varna.
2. The men went to the sea and looked for the Czarina Catherine, but the ship did not come.
3. They waited for many days, and each night Jonathan looked at Mina.
4. Mina was pale, and her mark was red.
5. At sunset Van Helsing put Mina to sleep again.
6. "I hear water," Mina said.
7. "I hear wood."
8. "The ship is not in the sea, and it is not in Varna."
9. "It is going up a river, and the Count is in it."
10. "It has gone to Galatz," Van Helsing said.
11. "We must go there now."
12. The men went to Galatz by train, and the ship was there.
13. The box was gone, and the men of the ship did not know where.
14. A man came to the ship with a paper, and he took the box away.
15. The men of the town said that the box went up the river on a boat.
16. "The boat is fast, and it is going to the mountains," a man said.
17. "The Count is going home to his castle," Van Helsing said.
18. "We must go there before him, and we must be there at sunset."
19. Van Helsing put a plan before the men.
20. "Jonathan and Arthur will go up the river in a fast boat," he said.
21. "Seward and Quincey will ride on horses by the river."
22. "I will go with Mina in a carriage to the castle."
23. "The Count must sleep in the box in the day, and he will come to the castle at sunset."
24. In the morning Jonathan and Arthur found a fast boat, and they paid the men of the boat with gold.
25. Seward and Quincey bought two horses.
26. Jonathan held Mina's hand for a long time.
27. "I will see you at the castle," Jonathan said.
28. "Be safe," Mina said.
29. The boat went up the river day and night, and the men of the boat worked hard.
30. Jonathan looked at the water every hour, and he looked for a boat with a box.
31. In each village the two men asked the same thing.
32. "Have you seen a boat with a box?" Arthur asked.
33. "Yes," a man said, "the boat went by two days ago, and Szgany men were in it."
34. "The men were afraid of the box, and they did not sleep."
35. Seward and Quincey rode on the bank of the river, and they slept by a fire at night.
36. Quincey did not speak much, but he looked at the sky every night.
37. "The days are short in the mountains," he said.
38. "We must go faster, or the Count will wake before we come."
39. At last the river was small, and the boat could not go on.
40. In a village at the foot of the mountains a man told Jonathan about the Szgany.
41. "They put the box on a cart," the man said, "and they went up the road to the pass."
42. Seward and Quincey came down from the hills that night, and the four men met.
43. "The castle is above the pass," Jonathan said.
44. "We will go up the road, and we will find the cart before sunset."

The Circle
1. Van Helsing and Mina went to the mountains in a carriage.
2. The road was bad, and the horses were afraid.
3. At sunset they came to a high place between two mountains.
4. "This is the pass, and the castle is near," Van Helsing said.
5. "We must not go on in the dark."
6. Van Helsing put holy bread on the ground in a big circle.
7. He put the horses and the carriage inside the circle, and he made a fire.
8. "Do not go out of the circle, whatever you hear," he said.
9. Mina sat by the fire, and Van Helsing held a cross.
10. In the night the wolves cried, and the horses cried out.
11. A mist came over the ground, and in the mist there were three women.
12. Two of the women had black hair, and the third woman had yellow hair.
13. Mina knew them, for she had read of them in Jonathan's book.
14. "Come to us, sister," the women said.
15. "Come out of the circle, and be one of us."
16. Mina stood up, and her face was white.
17. She took a step toward the women, and Van Helsing held her arm.
18. "They are not your sisters," he said.
19. "Stay in the circle."
20. Van Helsing held up the cross, and the women could not come in.
21. They stood at the edge of the circle, and their eyes were red.
22. The yellow woman laughed, and the sound was sweet.
23. "Come to me," the yellow woman said to Mina.
24. Mina put her hand on the cross, and she did not move.
25. The wolves came near the circle, but they did not come in.
26. The night was long, and the wolves cried until the sky was white.
27. Mina slept a little by the fire, and Van Helsing did not sleep.
28. When the sun came up, the mist went away, and the women were gone.
29. The wolves went away too, and the horses were still.
30. "We will go to the castle," Van Helsing said.

The Three Tombs
1. Van Helsing took a stake and a hammer and a bag of garlic.
2. "Stay in the circle, and do not go out of it," he said to Mina.
3. "I will come back when the women are dead."
4. Mina sat by the fire and held the cross.
5. Van Helsing went up to the castle, and the big door was open.
6. The sun was in the sky, and no wolf was near.
7. The rooms were dark, and there was dust on the floor.
8. He went down the old stairs to the old church, and his hands were cold, but he did not stop.
9. There were three tombs in the church, and the lids were shut.
10. Van Helsing opened the first tomb, and the yellow woman lay in it.
11. Her lips were red, and she looked young and good to see.
12. "I must do it," Van Helsing said, but he did not move.
13. He put the stake over her heart, and he struck it with the hammer.
14. The woman cried out, and her body was dust.
15. Van Helsing opened the second tomb, and a woman with black hair lay in it.
16. He opened the third tomb, and another woman with black hair lay in it.
17. He struck the stake into each of them, and each of them was dust.
18. At the end of the church he found the tomb of the Count, and it was empty.
19. He put holy bread and garlic in it, and no one could sleep there again.
20. Van Helsing went out of the castle, and he shut the door.
21. He put holy bread in the door, and no man could open it.
22. He went back to Mina in the circle, and she looked at his face.
23. "Are the women dead?" Mina asked.
24. "They are dead, and it is done," Van Helsing said.
25. "We must wait for the Count."

The End of the Count
1. Van Helsing and Mina went up on a high rock above the road.
2. They looked down the road toward the river, and they waited there.
3. The sun was going down, and the mountains were red.
4. "The Count will wake at sunset," Mina said.
5. A cart came up the road, and Szgany men rode by it.
6. A big box was on the cart, and the horses ran fast.
7. "That is the box," Van Helsing said.
8. "The Count is in it, and the Szgany will take it to the castle."
9. Behind the cart Jonathan and Arthur rode up the road.
10. Seward and Quincey came down from the other side.
11. The four men rode toward the cart, and each man held a knife.
12. The Szgany saw them, and each Szgany held a knife too.
13. The cart ran on, and the sun was near the mountain.
14. Mina held Van Helsing's arm and looked down at the road.
15. "Jonathan is there," she said.
16. "He will end the Count."
17. The sun touched the mountain.
18. Jonathan and Quincey jumped onto the cart, and the Szgany ran at them.
19. Arthur and Seward fought the Szgany hand to hand.
20. A Szgany cut Quincey in the side, but Quincey did not stop.
21. Jonathan took his big knife and opened the box.
22. The Count lay in the box, and his face was white.
23. His eyes were open, and they were red, and they looked at the sun.
24. "His eyes are open," Jonathan said.
25. "Cut his throat," Quincey said.
26. "I will put my knife in his heart."
27. Jonathan cut the Count's throat with the knife.
28. Quincey put his big knife in the Count's heart.
29. The Count cried out, and his body was dust.
30. The wind took the dust out of the box.
31. The sun went down behind the mountain, and the Szgany ran away.
32. Quincey sat down on the ground, and blood ran from his side.
33. Arthur and Seward held him, and Jonathan put his hand on Quincey's arm.
34. Van Helsing and Mina came down from the rock and ran to him.
35. "I am glad," Quincey said.
36. "It was worth it."
37. The last light of the sun fell on Mina's face.
38. "Look at Mina!" Quincey said.
39. "The mark is gone."
40. Mina put her hand to her head, and there was no mark.
41. Mina's face was white and clean, and Jonathan held her.
42. Quincey shut his eyes, and he did not speak again.
43. The men cried, and Arthur held his hand.
44. "He was a strong man, and he was a good man," Van Helsing said.
45. They put stones over Quincey on the mountain, and they went down to the river.
46. A year later Jonathan and Mina had a son, and they named him Quincey.
47. Seven years later they went back to the mountains with the boy and Van Helsing.
48. The castle was old and dark, and no wolf was there.
49. "Your boy will know what Quincey did," Van Helsing said.
