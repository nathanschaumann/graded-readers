The Pied Piper of Hamelin

Rats in Hamelin / Townsfolk Beg the Mayor / The Piper Arrives / The Piper Plays His Pipe / The Piper Asks for Gold
1. Hamelin was a town by a river.
2. Rats came into every house in the town.
3. Man: Rats are in my house.
4. Woman: Rats ate all my food.
5. Woman: We have no food left.
6. Every day more rats came to Hamelin.
7. Man: This town is full of rats.
8. Woman: We cannot live like this.
9. Man: Someone must take the rats away.
10. Woman: The mayor must help us.
11. The people of Hamelin were afraid.
12. The people came to the mayor's house.
13. Man: The rats are in every house.
14. Woman: We have no food because of the rats.
15. Mayor: I did not know it was this bad.
16. Man: You must help us with the rats.
17. Mayor: I will call my council.
18. The mayor called his council to his house.
19. Mayor: We must find a way to stop the rats.
20. The council did not know what to do.
21. Mayor: I will pay gold for help.
22. Man: Who can take so many rats away?
23. Mayor: I do not know, but I will pay gold.
24. A man came to Hamelin.
25. No one in Hamelin knew this man.
26. He wore a coat of many colors.
27. He carried a pipe.
28. Piper: I can take your rats away.
29. Mayor: Can you take our rats away?
30. Piper: My pipe can call every rat in town.
31. Mayor: I will pay gold if you help us.
32. Piper: How much gold will you give me?
33. Mayor: I will give you one bag of gold.
34. Piper: One bag of gold is enough for me.
35. Mayor: Take the rats away, and I will give you the gold.
36. Piper: I will start now.
37. The piper began to play his pipe.
38. Rats came out of every house in Hamelin.
39. The rats followed the sound of the pipe.
40. The piper walked through the town.
41. Man: Look how the rats follow him.
42. The piper walked toward the river.
43. The rats followed him all the way there.
44. The piper played his pipe at the river.
45. The rats ran into the river.
46. They could not swim back to land.
47. The rats drowned in the river.
48. Man: The rats are all gone from Hamelin.
49. The piper went to see the mayor.
50. Piper: The rats are gone, as I promised.
51. Piper: Now I want my gold.
52. Mayor: Are the rats gone from Hamelin?
53. Piper: Every rat drowned in the river.
54. Mayor: That is good for our town.
55. Mayor: A pipe did all of this work?
56. Piper: My pipe called every rat in town.
57. Mayor: The rats would have left.
58. Piper: I want the full bag of gold now.
59. Mayor: I will think about it.

Paragraphs:
1-5: Rats fill every house in Hamelin, and townsfolk complain that the rats eat their food.
6-11: More rats come every day, and the frightened people decide the mayor must help them.
12-17: Townsfolk beg the mayor to help with the rats, and he calls his council.
18-20: The council meets, but no one knows how to stop the rats.
21-23: The mayor offers gold for help, and the people wait.
24-30: A colorfully dressed stranger with a pipe arrives in Hamelin and offers to take the rats away.
31-36: The mayor agrees to pay one bag of gold, and the piper takes the job.
37-40: The piper plays his pipe, and rats pour out of every house to follow the sound through town.
41-44: Townsfolk watch as the piper leads the rats toward the river and plays once more.
45-48: The rats rush into the water and drown, and the relieved townsfolk watch the piper return.
49-54: The piper tells the mayor the rats are gone, and the mayor is glad to hear it.
55-59: The mayor doubts the pipe's power and puts off paying the full bag of gold.

The Broken Promise / The Empty Town / The Children Follow / To the Mountain / The Door in the Mountain / The Lame Boy's Story / Never Found
1. The mayor called his council again.
2. Mayor: The piper wants the full bag of gold.
3. Man: That is a lot of gold to pay.
4. Mayor: The rats are gone, so why pay so much?
5. Woman: We could give him a smaller bag.
6. Mayor: Yes, give him a smaller bag.
7. The mayor called the piper back to him.
8. Mayor: This is some gold for your work.
9. Piper: This is not the full bag we promised.
10. Piper: You broke the promise you made.
11. Mayor: Take the gold, or take nothing at all.
12. Piper: You will be sorry for this.
13. The piper left the mayor's house.
14. Many days passed after the piper left.
15. The people of Hamelin went back to their houses.
16. One day, most of the town went to church.
17. The mayor and the council went.
18. The children stayed in town to play.
19. The piper came back into the town.
20. He carried his pipe again.
21. Piper: The mayor broke his promise to me.
22. Piper: Now I will take something else.
23. The piper began to play his pipe.
24. Children came out of every house in town.
25. Girl: Do you hear that pipe?
26. Boy: Yes, I hear it too.
27. The children ran through town.
28. More children came out to follow.
29. The children followed the piper through town.
30. Girl: Where is he taking us?
31. Boy: I do not know, but I will follow.
32. One boy was lame and could not run.
33. He fell behind the other children.
34. The children followed the piper out of town.
35. The children followed him.
36. He walked toward the mountain.
37. The children followed him up a path.
38. Boy: My leg is tired, and I am falling behind.
39. Girl: Run, do not fall behind!
40. The other children ran away from him.
41. The piper reached the mountain.
42. Boy: Wait for me! I cannot run!
43. No one heard the boy call out.
44. He was now behind the other children.
45. A door opened in the mountain.
46. The piper walked through the door.
47. One by one, the children went inside.
48. The lame boy ran toward the door.
49. Boy: Wait! Do not close the door!
50. The door closed before he could reach it.
51. The boy stood by the mountain.
52. He called out at the mountain.
53. Boy: Let me in! The other children are inside!
54. The mountain grew silent.
55. The lame boy walked back into Hamelin.
56. Man: Where are the children?
57. Boy: The piper took them into the mountain.
58. Woman: Why are you not with the children?
59. Boy: My leg was too tired to run.
60. Man: Did the mountain have a door?
61. Boy: The door is closed now.
62. Mayor: This happened because I broke my promise.
63. Man: We should have paid the piper his gold.
64. Woman: Now the children are gone.
65. The people of Hamelin searched the mountain for days.
66. They never found the door again.
67. Man: The mountain looks as it always did.
68. Woman: But no children are inside it now.
69. The people of Hamelin waited for the children.
70. The children of Hamelin never came back.
71. Only the lame boy remembered what happened.
72. He told other people the piper's story.
73. The people of Hamelin never forgot the piper.

Paragraphs:
1-6: The council decides to pay the piper far less gold than promised.
7-9: The mayor offers the smaller bag, and the piper refuses it.
10-13: The piper accuses the mayor of breaking his promise and leaves in anger.
14-18: Days pass, and while most of the town, including the mayor and council, go to church, the children stay outside.
19-23: The piper returns, recalls the broken promise, and begins to play his pipe again.
24-29: The children hear the pipe, run outside, and follow the piper through town.
30-34: The children wonder where they are going, and a lame boy falls behind as they leave town.
35-40: The children follow him up a path toward the mountain, and the lame boy falls behind as the others run on.
41-44: The piper reaches the mountain, but no one hears the boy's call, and he falls behind.
45-49: A door opens in the mountain, the children go inside, and the lame boy races to reach it.
50-54: The door closes before he arrives, and his calls to be let in go unheard.
55-59: The lame boy returns alone and tells the townsfolk that the piper took the children into the mountain.
60-64: The boy describes the closed door, and the mayor admits his broken promise caused the children's loss.
65-69: The people search the mountain for days, but they never find the door, and the children do not come back.
70-73: The children never return, and only the lame boy remembers and tells the piper's story.
