Graded English readers

Public-domain tales retold in plain, literal English at five levels. Each level caps sentence length and how much new vocabulary a chapter may bring in, and an open scorer checks every story against its limits.

Read the method and its limits.

Chapters are 300 to 600 words at every level. Guiraud is unique word stems divided by the square root of the word count, and a lower value means more repetition.
